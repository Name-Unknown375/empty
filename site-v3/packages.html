Package FAQ

What's included in a rental package?

Every package includes tables and chairs sized to your guest count. The Covered tier adds a professionally-installed marquee tent; Garden Premium upgrades to Resin Garden chairs and adds bistro string lighting and spandex-covered cocktail tables. The 10% bundle discount is built into every price shown.

Is delivery included in the package price?

The package price covers the equipment, already discounted 10% versus booking the items separately. Delivery, professional setup, and pickup are quoted separately based on your city and shown up front — no surprise fees. Warehouse pickup is available on smaller packages if you'd prefer to skip delivery.

Can I customize a package or add items?

Yes. Packages are a starting point — add linens, a dance floor, heaters, or a carnival-game bundle, or swap table styles. The bundle discount stays intact on the package portion, and everything arrives on one truck.

What if my guest count isn't 50, 100, or 150?

Those three sizes are starting points. Tell us your exact headcount and we'll build a custom-size package the same day, with the same 10% bundle pricing.

How much deposit is required to book a package?

A 25% deposit secures your date, with the balance due before your event. Your date is locked once the deposit is paid. Deposits aren't refundable if you cancel.

How much do event packages cost?

Starting prices — 50-guest Essentials tier, 10% bundle discount already applied: backyard from $230, wedding from $246, corporate from $305. Prices scale with guest count and tier, up to the 150-guest Garden Premium.

Don't See The Right Size?

We quote custom-size packages for any guest count. Email or call us and we'll build it same-day.
