What our Willoughby chair lineup covers

A Chiavari ready to load — individually wiped, inspected, stacked.

Three chair lines cover almost every Willoughby event:

  • White Chiavari ($8.50/day) — the wedding-reception standard. Best for Yorkson and Willoughby Heights residence receptions under marquee, indoor venues, head-table seating at LEC galas, and any Willoughby booking where the photos prioritize formal seating.
  • Fanback folding ($3.25/day) — the workhorse for the LEC and community-scale events. Right for Langley Events Centre corporate galas (300+ chair orders are routine), community fundraisers, and high-headcount community-venue seating.
  • Resin Garden ($5.50/day) — the outdoor-resilient pick. Right for Sendall Gardens heritage-garden ceremonies, Willoughby Community Park open-lawn weddings, Jacob Hoogendoorn Park ceremonies, and any Willoughby booking on morning-dew grass.

Mixing lines is free. Sendall Gardens Resin Garden ceremony + Yorkson Chiavari residence reception is the most-booked Willoughby wedding pairing.

Langley Events Centre

The LEC drives the largest single-event Willoughby chair counts. Corporate galas, sports banquets, and community fundraisers regularly ship 300+ Fanback folding chairs with Chiavari for head-table and dignitary seating alongside. Loading-dock access is ample; the LEC events team coordinates vendor delivery windows.

Sendall Gardens heritage

Sendall Gardens’ heritage garden ceremony zones run on garden-permit windows. Resin Garden every time for the morning-dew grass and garden-edge surface conditions. Truck access at the perimeter with hand-carry to the specific ceremony zone within the garden.

Yorkson and Willoughby Heights residential

Yorkson and Willoughby Heights’ newer residential streets accept any chair configuration with friendly truck access. Most weddings are 80–130 guests with Chiavari-only configurations under marquee at the larger lots, or two-chair-line setups for ceremonies that flip from outdoor to indoor.

Willoughby Community Park and Jacob Hoogendoorn Park

Both parks run on park-permit windows for outdoor ceremonies. Resin Garden for the open-lawn ceremony zones.

Routley residential

Routley’s residential streets accept standard delivery patterns. Most Routley backyards accept a single chair-line configuration; rare two-chair-line bookings for the larger lots.

Per-item pricing

The same in Yorkson as at the LEC. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The Highway 1 delivery line is the variable, not the per-chair rate.

Willoughby Chair Rentals FAQ

Can you handle a Langley Events Centre corporate event?
Yes — the LEC is one of our larger single-event chair-count venues. 300+ Fanback folding chairs for corporate galas and sports-banquet seating is routine; Chiavari for head-table and dignitary seating ships alongside. Loading-dock access is ample, and we coordinate with the LEC events team for the timed delivery window.
Which chair works for a Sendall Gardens or Willoughby Community Park ceremony?
For Sendall Gardens' heritage garden ceremony zones and Willoughby Community Park's open-lawn zones, Resin Garden is the strongest pick — both run on morning-dew grass and the garden-edge surface conditions don't tolerate Chiavari's lacquered hardwood well across a full ceremony arc.
What's the standard install for a Yorkson backyard wedding?
Yorkson's newer streets accept any chair configuration. Most Yorkson backyards are large enough for two-chair-line setups at 100-guest scale; the Chiavari-only configuration handles smaller 60–80 guest weddings with the reception flowing directly from the ceremony space.
Can I mix chair types for a Willoughby event?
Yes — Fanback folding for the Langley Events Centre corporate event, Chiavari for the post-event reception elsewhere; or Resin Garden for a Sendall Gardens ceremony followed by Chiavari indoors at a Yorkson residence reception. Single quote, single delivery, no mixing surcharge.
How fast can you deliver chairs to Willoughby?
Willoughby is one of our shortest Township delivery routes — 25 minutes via Highway 1. Short-notice walk-in additions or chair swaps mid-event can typically be on-site within 60 minutes during business hours.
