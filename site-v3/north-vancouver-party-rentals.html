Steep driveways, hairpin roads, and North Shore access

A single high-peak on a garden lawn, mountains behind.

North Van delivery isn’t urban delivery. Four operational realities shape the quote.

Bridge + traffic

We plan delivery windows around the Ironworkers Memorial crossing and Lonsdale traffic patterns. Friday afternoon and Sunday morning teardowns get the widest windows.

Driveway grade and width

Our delivery trucks handle most North Van driveways, but unusually steep or narrow lots need staging at the road. We confirm access during quoting — never on event day.

Overhead branches and clearance

North Shore yards have more tree cover than most Lower Mainland properties. We measure marquee peak clearance before quoting; the backyard tent sizing playbook covers the measurements we ask for.

Mountain weather

Elevation-driven afternoon wind is the constant. Sidewalls go up year-round on any North Van mountain-adjacent venue.

Per-item pricing

The same on a Lynn Valley acreage as in any North Van neighbourhood. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, grade, access — never the per-item rate.

Lonsdale waterfront and Quay-area events

The other half of our North Van calendar is waterfront — restaurant patio events, Lonsdale Quay-area weddings, and corporate gatherings along the Burrard Inlet. The operational pattern is different from mountain installs: smaller footprints (a 20×20 or 20×30 typically clears the patio), faster setup, and a stronger emphasis on cocktail-hour highboys and bistro lighting than full-scale dinner seating. Wind exposure is meaningful at the water’s edge — sidewalls go up year-round, even on summer evenings.

A 100-guest North Vancouver wedding rental order

For a 100-guest North Vancouver wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Lynn Valley acreage with ceremony on the lawn under tree cover and reception under a marquee — a typical order looks like:

  • 1 × 20×60 marquee tent with sidewalls (covered reception; ceremony is open-air under the trees)
  • 100 × white Chiavari chairs (ceremony rows under canopy, then re-staged at reception tables)

Setup runs 5–6 hours; teardown 3–4. Both quoted up front. For sizing variations, our 50/100/150/200 rental checklist maps the same configuration to your guest count.

North Vancouver FAQ

What's included on a North Vancouver rental quote?
Every North Van quote we send is itemised: rental items by line, delivery, crew setup time, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no North Shore uplift, no surprises at the truck. If your quote says $X, you pay $X.
Can your truck handle steep North Van driveways?
In most cases yes — our delivery vans handle grades up to about 10% loaded, and most Lonsdale driveways stay within that. For unusually steep or narrow lots (some Edgemont and lower Capilano properties) we'll either stage at the road and hand-carry, or send a smaller truck for that delivery. Either way we confirm the access during quoting, not on event day.
How clean are the chairs and tables when they arrive?
Every chair is individually wiped and inspected before it leaves our warehouse. Cushions are spot-cleaned, legs checked for damage, spindles checked for cracks. Tables get the same treatment. Anything that doesn't meet our condition bar comes off the inventory roster, not onto your booking.
What's the wind plan for North Van mountain or waterfront ceremonies?
Elevation-driven afternoon winds are real. For any mountain-adjacent venue we recommend ballasted weights at every leg even when staking is possible, sidewalls year-round, and a slightly larger marquee than the headcount strictly requires — extra footprint absorbs gust load better than a tightly-sized tent. We don't compromise on anchor strategy here.
Can you do same-week North Vancouver bookings?
Sometimes — depends on the equipment. Chair, table, and 10×10 popup tent orders are usually feasible inside a 5–7 day window if our trucks aren't already booked. Marquee tents 20×40 and larger need installer scheduling that's hard to compress under 7 days, especially for North Van where the access window is wider than urban deliveries.
How much do party rentals cost in North Vancouver?
Party rental pricing in North Vancouver depends on the items and your guest count. As a guide, chairs run $3.25–$8.50 each per day, 6-foot banquet and 5-foot round tables $10.95–$13.50 per day, popup tents from $75 per day, and marquee tents from $550 per event — setup and takedown included. Bundling a tent, tables and chairs into one of our packages saves 10%, and paying in full within 24 hours of your quote saves another 10%. Build your order online for an instant, itemised North Vancouver quote — delivery and setup are priced up front with no hidden fees or fuel surcharges.
What does the delivery and setup fee cover?
When you add delivery and setup to your order, our crew handles round-trip transport, professional setup of tents and dance floors, takedown, and pickup. Chairs and tables are stacked and placed where you want them. Delivery and setup start at $175 and depend on distance from our Surrey warehouse — quoted up front with no hidden charges or fuel surcharges. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.
Can you set up a tent on grass, gravel, or a paved driveway?
Yes to all three. Our marquee tents use stake-down installation on grass and ballasted (weighted) installation on pavement, gravel, and stamped concrete. Tell us the surface when you book and we'll send the right hardware. We do not tent on artificial turf.
What happens if the weather is bad or I need to cancel?
If you need to reschedule because of weather, we'll roll your deposit forward to a new date at no charge — as long as we're notified before crew dispatch. Your date is locked once the deposit is paid; deposits aren't refundable if you cancel outright.
