Maple Ridge FAQ

Do you deliver party rentals to Maple Ridge?
Yes — we deliver and set up throughout Maple Ridge, including Hammond, Silver Valley, and Albion. Standard Maple Ridge delivery runs just 30-40 minutes. Delivery and setup are available as add-on services — we'll quote them up front with no surprise fees at the venue.
How far in advance should I book for an event in Maple Ridge?
For peak season (May through September) in Maple Ridge, we recommend booking 6–8 weeks out — especially for weekend weddings. Off-season and weekday events can often be booked 1–2 weeks out. You can check live availability and reserve with a 25% deposit on our online booking page 24/7.
Have you worked at venues in Maple Ridge like Golden Ears Provincial Park?
Yes — we regularly deliver rentals to Golden Ears Provincial Park, Alouette Lake, and Kanaka Creek Regional Park and dozens of private residences across Maple Ridge. We know the setback rules for most public parks in Maple Ridge and can advise on what permits (if any) your venue requires for tents.
How much do party rentals cost in Maple Ridge?
Party rental pricing in Maple Ridge depends on the items and your guest count. As a guide, chairs run $3.25–$8.50 each per day, 6-foot banquet and 5-foot round tables $10.95–$13.50 per day, popup tents from $75 per day, and marquee tents from $550 per event — setup and takedown included. Bundling a tent, tables and chairs into one of our packages saves 10%, and paying in full within 24 hours of your quote saves another 10%. Build your order online for an instant, itemised Maple Ridge quote — delivery and setup are priced up front with no hidden fees or fuel surcharges.
What does the delivery and setup fee cover?
When you add delivery and setup to your order, our crew handles round-trip transport, professional setup of tents and dance floors, takedown, and pickup. Chairs and tables are stacked and placed where you want them. Delivery and setup start at $175 and depend on distance from our Surrey warehouse — quoted up front with no hidden charges or fuel surcharges. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.
Can you set up a tent on grass, gravel, or a paved driveway?
Yes to all three. Our marquee tents use stake-down installation on grass and ballasted (weighted) installation on pavement, gravel, and stamped concrete. Tell us the surface when you book and we'll send the right hardware. We do not tent on artificial turf.
What happens if the weather is bad or I need to cancel?
If you need to reschedule because of weather, we'll roll your deposit forward to a new date at no charge — as long as we're notified before crew dispatch. Your date is locked once the deposit is paid; deposits aren't refundable if you cancel outright.
