How we quote a North Shore delivery

We load at 9317 188 St in Surrey and cross Lions Gate or Ironworkers before we set a single chair. North Vancouver is 55–70 minutes; West Vancouver is 60–75, depending on the bridge. Friday afternoon installs and Sunday teardowns get the widest windows. The crossing is in the quote before the truck leaves — not added at the venue.

Per-item rates are the same as the rest of the Lower Mainland. A Chiavari is $8.50/day in Lynn Valley and in Surrey. There is no North Shore uplift. If the quote says $X, you pay $X. Delivery, crew setup, and pickup are line items shown up front. A 25% deposit locks the date.

City of North Vancouver, District of North Vancouver, and West Vancouver are three municipalities and two access patterns. Dedicated pages: North Vancouver party rentals and West Vancouver party rentals. Lonsdale, Edgemont, Capilano

Steep driveways, Lonsdale Quay corporate events, and the elevation change between sea-level and the upper benches. Most lots we can drive; unusually steep or narrow aprons we stage at the road and hand-carry. Chair, table and tent notes for this side: chair rentals in North Vancouver.

Deep Cove, Lynn Valley, Grouse

Hairpin access out to Deep Cove, tree cover that eats marquee peak height, and afternoon wind off the mountain. Sidewalls go up year-round on mountain-adjacent installs; we pair engineered stakes with ballast as the default on North Shore grass. Marquee notes: tent rentals in North Vancouver.

Ambleside, Whytecliff, British Properties

Seawall ceremonies, Taylor Way up to British Properties, ferry timing at Horseshoe Bay, and tidal windows at the water. Whytecliff is chairs-and-arch, cart-down-stairs, no tent — the Whytecliff Park rental guide is the operational playbook. Reception is usually a North Shore home or venue 10–15 minutes east.

A 100-guest North Shore wedding on the truck

Same catalogue as the rest of the Lower Mainland — we own the tent, chairs, tables, and dance floor. A typical Saturday: ceremony on a Lynn Valley lawn, dinner under a marquee.

  • 1 × 20×60 marquee with sidewalls
  • 100 × white Chiavari (ceremony rows, then restaged at dinner — 10% spare is already how we count packages)
  • 12 × 5ft round tables (8 per table)
  • 6 × 6ft banquet (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
  • 1 × 12×16 dance floor, 2 × cocktail highboys, bistro lighting on the ridge

Setup 5–6 hours; teardown 3–4. Both quoted up front. Guest-count maps: 50 / 100 / 150 / 200 rental checklist. Layout before you book: free layout planner.

North Shore party rental FAQ

Do you deliver party rentals to the North Shore?

Yes. We deliver and set up across the North Shore — the City and District of North Vancouver plus West Vancouver. That includes Lonsdale, Deep Cove, Lynn Valley, Ambleside, Dundarave, British Properties, Whytecliff, and Horseshoe Bay. Delivery and crew install are quoted up front.

Is North Shore the same as North Vancouver?

Searchers type North Shore for the whole north-of-the-inlet side: North Vancouver and West Vancouver. We keep separate city pages for North Vancouver and West Vancouver because the routes and venues differ — Lions Gate versus Ironworkers, Lonsdale driveways versus Whytecliff tide windows. This page is the regional hub.

How long is a North Shore delivery from Surrey?

North Vancouver runs about 55–70 minutes from our Surrey warehouse; West Vancouver about 60–75 minutes, depending on Lions Gate or Ironworkers traffic. We build the bridge crossing into the delivery window before we quote, not after the truck is in the queue.

Do you set up at Whytecliff, Grouse, or Capilano?

Yes. We regularly deliver to Grouse Mountain, Capilano Suspension Bridge, Lynn Canyon, Ambleside Park, Lighthouse Park, and Whytecliff Park. Whytecliff in particular needs cart access from the upper lot and tidal-window planning — our Whytecliff Park rental guide covers the operational details. Tents are generally not approved at Whytecliff; that order is chairs and an arch.

How far in advance should we book North Shore party rentals?

Peak season (May through September) weekend weddings: 6–8 weeks. Off-season and weekdays can often book 1–2 weeks out if the marquee is free. A 25% deposit locks the date on online booking.
