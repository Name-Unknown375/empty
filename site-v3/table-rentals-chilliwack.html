Chilliwack Table Rentals FAQ

How many guests do your banquet and round tables seat in Chilliwack?
Our 6-foot rectangular banquet tables seat 6–8 guests; 8-foot tables seat 8–10. Our 5-foot rounds seat 8; 6-foot rounds seat 10. For Chilliwack weddings and events, we'll help you pick the right mix based on your guest count and floor plan when you book.
Do you deliver linens or chairs with the tables?
Yes. We stock tablecloths, napkins, and runners in-house and deliver them to Chilliwack on the same truck as your tables and chairs. Sizing lives on our tablecloth rentals page. Chargers and place settings sit with the caterer. Chairs, dance floors, and tents can all be added to the same delivery in your online cart.
Can you deliver tables the day before the event in Chilliwack?
Yes — we offer early-bird delivery in Chilliwack for an additional fee, typically a day before your event. This is especially popular for morning ceremonies and early Saturday setups. Request early delivery in the booking notes or mention it on the phone.
Do you deliver party rentals to Chilliwack?
Yes — we deliver and set up throughout Chilliwack, including Downtown Chilliwack, Sardis, Promontory, and Vedder Crossing. Standard Chilliwack delivery runs about 45-60 minutes via Highway 1. Delivery and setup are available as add-on services — we'll quote them up front with no surprise fees at the venue.
Have you worked at venues in Chilliwack like Cultus Lake?
Yes — we regularly deliver rentals to Cultus Lake, Chilliwack Heritage Park, and venues near Bridal Veil Falls Provincial Park and dozens of private residences across Chilliwack. We know the setback rules for most public parks in Chilliwack and can advise on what permits (if any) your venue requires for tents.
