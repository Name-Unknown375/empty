West Vancouver Christmas Light FAQ

What's included in each package?
Every West Vancouver package includes: on-site design consultation, fixed-price written quote, commercial-grade LED strands (we own them — you don't buy them), WCB-covered install crew, season-long maintenance with a 24–48 hour fix guarantee, January takedown, and free climate-controlled storage of your lights through the off-season. There's no separate storage fee, no hourly labor charge, and no surcharge for returning to fix a failed strand.
Do you provide the lights, or do I need to buy them?
We provide the lights. Our commercial-grade LED strands are part of the package — you don't buy or own any lights. They're installed, maintained, taken down, and stored by us for use on your home each November. If you'd prefer to use lights you already own, we offer a 'hang-your-own' install service at a reduced rate, but we cannot warranty the strands.
When do you take down lights? Can I keep them up until spring?
Standard takedown is in the first two weeks of January. If you'd prefer to keep lights up longer (we've had West Vancouver clients ask for Chinese New Year, Valentine's Day, and 'until the snow melts'), just tell us at booking — there's no additional charge, we just slot you into the late-takedown queue.
When should I book Christmas light installation in West Vancouver?
Our install calendar for West Vancouver typically fills by late October for November installs. For guaranteed installation before December 1st, book by the first week of October. We accept bookings year-round — if you're reading this in spring or summer, locking in now also locks in this season's pricing and secures a priority install slot.
Do you install on 2-story and 3-story homes in West Vancouver?
Yes. Our crews are WCB-certified for work at height and carry the ladder and rope equipment needed for 2-story and 3-story homes throughout West Vancouver. We carry appropriate liability insurance and can provide proof of insurance to your homeowner's policy or strata if needed. If your home has unusual access challenges (peaked roofs over 30 ft, or extreme pitches), we'll flag it at the design consult and price accordingly.
Do you install Christmas lights in West Vancouver and surrounding neighborhoods?
Yes — West Vancouver is one of our core service areas. We install throughout all West Vancouver neighborhoods. Most West Vancouver installations are completed in a single 2–4 hour visit. Book a free design consultation to walk your property before October to secure a November install slot.
