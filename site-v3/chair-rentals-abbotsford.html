What our Abbotsford chair lineup covers

A Chiavari ready to load — individually wiped, inspected, stacked.

Three chair lines that cover almost every Abbotsford event:

  • White Chiavari ($8.50/day) — the wedding-reception standard. Lacquered hardwood frame, ivory cushion, stacks 8 high. Best for indoor barn-style receptions on vineyard properties, Clayburn Village heritage venues, Tradex gala head-table seating, and any Abbotsford venue where the photos prioritize formal seating.
  • Fanback folding ($3.25/day) — the workhorse. Steel frame, folds flat, stacks compactly. Right for ceremony rows on farm acreages, Tradex Exhibition Centre corporate seating at gala scale, school events, and any high-headcount booking. A 500-chair Tradex order ships on a single multi-load run.
  • Resin Garden ($5.50/day) — the outdoor-resilient pick. Resin frame and seat, more substantial visual than Fanback but more forgiving than Chiavari to weather and damp grass. The right call for vineyard ceremonies, Matsqui farm ceremonies, Mill Lake Park lakeside weddings, and any Abbotsford booking that runs partly under sky.

Mixing lines is free. We carry enough of each that the full ceremony + reception split ships without short-shipping.

Vineyards, Tradex, Mill Lake — Abbotsford chair patterns

Banquet rows and fanback chairs dressed under a frame tent.

Vineyard and farm weddings — Mount Lehman, Matsqui

Abbotsford vineyards and farms (Mount Lehman, Matsqui, Sumas) host a lot of the Fraser Valley’s most-photographed weddings. Two-chair-line setup is the standard: Resin Garden for the outdoor ceremony in the rows or on the lawn, Chiavari for the indoor barn or marquee reception. Truck access on rural roads is ample.

Tradex Exhibition Centre — gala-scale corporate

Tradex hosts the region’s largest single-event seating configurations. 500+ Fanback folding chairs is a routine order; the loading-dock access is ample and we coordinate the delivery window around the venue’s primary programming. Head-table Chiavari ships alongside.

Mill Lake Park and civic ceremonies

Mill Lake Park’s lakeside ceremony zone runs on city event permits with venue-coordinated delivery windows. Resin Garden for the lakeside ceremony; Chiavari indoors if the reception flips to a hotel or restaurant in central Abbotsford.

Clayburn Village heritage

Clayburn Village’s heritage streets run smaller, more intimate weddings (40–80 guests) with Chiavari indoors at the heritage venues. Truck access through the village is tight; we use shorter delivery windows.

Sumas Mountain hillside

Sumas Mountain acreages run rural-road delivery with longer driveways. Most Sumas weddings are 100–150 guests with the full chair-line mix.

Per-item pricing

The same on a Mount Lehman vineyard as at Tradex. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — never the per-chair rate.

Same-day swaps and short-notice add-ons

Abbotsford delivery runs roughly 60–75 minutes via Highway 1. For mid-event swaps and short-notice additions during business hours, we typically arrive within 90 minutes. For weekend long-haul Tradex events, we pre-stage backup inventory on-site.

Abbotsford Chair Rentals FAQ

What's included on an Abbotsford chair rental quote?
Every Abbotsford chair quote we send is itemised: chairs by line, delivery, crew positioning to your floor plan, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Fraser Valley uplifts, no surprises at the truck. Highway 1 corridor delivery is straightforward and we don't tack on a Valley-distance premium.
Which chair works for a vineyard, farm, or Mill Lake ceremony?
For vineyard and farm ceremonies on Abbotsford acreages and Mill Lake Park's lakeside zone, Resin Garden is the strongest pick — the resin frame and seat handle damp grass, dew, and the long Fraser Valley afternoons better than Chiavari's lacquered hardwood. Most vineyard weddings book Resin Garden for the ceremony and Chiavari indoors at the barn or under a marquee for the reception.
Can you handle a 500-guest Tradex Exhibition Centre event?
Yes — Tradex is one of the largest single-event chair counts on our calendar. We've shipped 500+ Fanback folding chairs to Tradex galas and corporate events, plus the Chiavari line for head-table and dignitary seating. Tradex's loading dock and floor access are ample; we coordinate the delivery window during quoting.
Can I mix chair types for an Abbotsford event?
Yes — Fanback folding for the ceremony rows on a Matsqui farm, Chiavari at the reception tables in the barn; or Resin Garden for an outdoor vineyard ceremony followed by Chiavari indoors at a Clayburn Village heritage venue. Single quote, single delivery, no mixing surcharge.
Do you deliver to Sumas Mountain or McKee acreage?
Yes — both are routine Abbotsford bookings. Sumas Mountain hillside acreages occasionally need slightly extended delivery windows for the longer driveways; we walk the site during quoting if access is non-standard. McKee runs standard residential delivery.
