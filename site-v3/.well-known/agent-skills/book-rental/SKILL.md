# Book a Rental — Forever Party Rentals

Forever Party Rentals is a party equipment rental company serving the Lower Mainland of British Columbia (Vancouver, Surrey, Burnaby, Langley, Abbotsford, Chilliwack, and surrounding cities).

## What we rent

- **Tents** — 10x10, 20x20, 20x40, 20x60, 40x60, 40x80 frame and pole tents.
- **Seating** — chiavari chairs, folding chairs, lounge seating.
- **Tables** — round (60"), rectangular (6ft, 8ft), cocktail.
- **Linens, dance floors, lighting, heaters.**
- **Packages** — pre-built bundles for backyard weddings/parties sized for 50, 100, and 150 guests.

## How to help a customer request a quote

Collect these details from the customer, then direct them to the booking widget or contact channel.

1. **Event date** (required).
2. **Delivery address or pickup preference** (pickup available in Surrey).
3. **Guest count** — drives tent size and table/chair counts.
4. **Event type** — wedding, birthday, corporate, backyard party, etc.
5. **Items of interest** — or let them browse the catalog.

## Sizing guidance (quick reference)

- 50 guests (seated dinner): ~20x40 tent.
- 100 guests (seated dinner): ~20x60 or 30x40 tent.
- 150 guests (seated dinner): ~30x60 or 40x60 tent.
- For ceremony-to-reception flips, consult the blog post at /blog/tent-layout-ceremony-to-reception-flip.html.

Deeper sizing guides live in the blog: https://www.foreverpartyrentals.com/blog

## What NOT to promise

- Exact pricing — this varies by date, location, and items. Route the customer to the booking widget or contact form for a real quote.
- Same-day availability — inventory is not exposed publicly; confirm via booking widget or by contacting the team.
- Service outside the Lower Mainland — we don't currently deliver outside BC's Lower Mainland.
