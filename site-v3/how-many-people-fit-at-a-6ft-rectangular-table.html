How Many People Fit at a 6 Ft Rectangular Table?

A 6-foot banquet table seats 6 comfortably for plated dinners and 8 max with a chair on each end. The top measures 72" × 30" — the rental-industry standard. We deliver 6 ft banquet tables across Metro Vancouver from $10.95 / day, and our free layout planner lets you test the floor plan before you book.

How many people can sit at a 6 foot table?

Six plated / eight family-style. Rent this table from $10.95 — open the layout planner to test the floor plan, then book. Place-setting width is the constraint: catering schools teach 24 inches of edge per guest for plated dinner — that's a charger plate, two glasses, flatware on either side, and elbow room. A 72-inch table edge ÷ 24 inches = 3 chairs per side, total of 6. That's the comfortable number.

Ten guests at a 6-footer leaves about 14 inches per person at the edge — narrower than a placemat. We don't recommend it. If you need 10 at one table, rent an 8-foot banquet table (seats 8 plated, 10 family-style) or a 5-foot round (seats 8 comfortably, 10 tight).

How many chairs for a 6 foot table?

Eight chairs is the full count: three on each long side plus one at each 30-inch end. Family-style dining makes that work because the food sits down the centre and place settings shrink to about 18 inches per guest. Folding banquet chairs and Chiavari chairs both have ~18-inch seat widths, so 8 fits cleanly with either type. For plated service, drop the two end chairs and seat 6.

FAQ

How many chairs do I need for a wedding using 6 ft rectangular tables?
For plated service: divide your guest count by 6. For family-style: divide by 8. Always order 4–8 spare chairs on top of the table-count — it covers plus-ones, walk-ups, and the moment somebody breaks one. Our wedding clients in Surrey, Langley, and Vancouver typically rent Chiavari chairs for plated dinners.
What size tablecloth fits a 6 ft banquet table?
A 90" × 132" linen drops floor-length on all four sides — the wedding standard. A 60" × 102" linen drops mid-thigh and is what you'll see at corporate events and conferences. Skip the 54" × 108" "banquet topper" unless you're layering over a base.
Do you deliver 6 ft banquet tables across Metro Vancouver?
Yes — we deliver banquet tables across Vancouver, Burnaby and New Westminster, plus Surrey, Langley, Richmond, North Vancouver, Abbotsford, Maple Ridge, Coquitlam, Delta, White Rock, Pitt Meadows, Mission, Chilliwack, Harrison Hot Springs, and 12+ other Lower Mainland cities. Delivery and setup are quoted up front based on your venue address — no surprise fuel surcharges.
Can I pick up 6 ft tables instead of paying for delivery?
Yes. Customer pickup is free from our Surrey warehouse at 9317 188 St. A 6-footer folds to 72" × 30" × 4" and weighs under 75 lb — fits in any SUV, pickup truck, or cargo van. Mid-size sedans usually take 2–3 with the rear seats down.
Are the tables wiped and inspected between rentals?
Every table is wiped down, checked for cracks or wobbles, and re-stickered with our QA tag before loading. If a table arrives at your venue with any visible damage, our delivery crew swaps it out from truck stock or we deliver a replacement within 60 minutes. That's our standard SOP — not a special service.
