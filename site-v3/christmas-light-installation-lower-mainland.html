Lower Mainland Coverage FAQ

What cities across the Lower Mainland do you install Christmas lights in?

Fourteen cities across Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley: Surrey, Langley, Vancouver, Burnaby, Abbotsford, Coquitlam, Delta, Langley Township, Maple Ridge, New Westminster, North Vancouver, Port Moody, Richmond, and White Rock. For smaller or more remote communities we offer priority-booking or by-quote service depending on travel distance.

How is pricing structured across the Lower Mainland?

We price by package (Starter / Classic / Premium), not by city or square footage. Pricing is fixed regardless of whether you're in central Vancouver or further out in Abbotsford — no surprise zone surcharge, no fuel fee, no travel surcharge within our 14 service cities.

Do you schedule installs differently across Lower Mainland cities?

Our install calendar runs October through the first week of December. Cities closer to our service hub (Surrey, Langley, Delta, White Rock) have slightly more same-week slots available. Farther cities (Maple Ridge, Abbotsford, North Vancouver) are grouped into regional install days — typically one or two install days per week dedicated to each area to minimize crew windshield time.

Can I see an example of a local install before I book?

Yes — at the free design consultation we can share references and examples from properties we've installed in your immediate neighborhood, if privacy permits. Many of our clients are happy to recommend us, and for strata and commercial clients we can provide proof-of-insurance documentation up front.
