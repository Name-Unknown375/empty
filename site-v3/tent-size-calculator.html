How We Size a Tent

Square feet per guest

Cocktail events run about 8 sq ft per guest. Seated dinners need 12–15 sq ft once you add chair push-back and aisles. Ceremony rows fall in between. Our engine sizes from the actual furniture footprint, not just a rule of thumb.

Table pitch, not table size

A 5ft round is 5 ft across — but with chairs it's a 9.2 ft envelope, and with walking room it sits on a ~10.75 ft pitch (the catering-industry standard). That's why round-table layouts need wider tents than most people expect.

Dance floors & extras

We size dance floors at roughly 3 sq ft × 40% of your guest list, snapped up to our real floor sizes (8×8 to 16×16). Head tables and buffet lines reserve their own zones before the seating grid is placed.

Tent Sizing FAQ

What size tent do I need for 50 guests?

For 50 guests at long banquet tables run family-style, a single 20×60 ft marquee (1,200 sq ft) works — even with a dance floor — with tent rental from $1,260. Round tables need more width: two 20×60 marquees joined side-by-side (40×60 ft, 2,400 sq ft) from $2,520. Smaller groups: 20 guests fit a 20×20 at long tables ($550) or a 20×40 at rounds ($1,100).

What size tent do I need for 100 guests?

For 100 guests — round or long banquet tables, with or without a dance floor — two 20×60 marquees joined side-by-side: a 40×60 ft footprint, 2,400 sq ft (24 sq ft per guest), with tent rental from $2,520. The dance floor tucks into a corner of the tent beside the seating rather than demanding its own full-width zone.

What size tent do I need for 150 guests?

For 150 guests at long banquet tables, our 30×60 joined with a 20×60 (50×60 ft, 3,000 sq ft) works — with or without a dance floor — with tent rental from $3,150. Round tables with a dance floor step up to the 30×60 joined with both 20×60s (70×60 ft, 4,200 sq ft) from $4,410.

Do I need sidewalls on my tent?

In the Lower Mainland we recommend sidewalls for any event outside July–August, for evening events, and for any site exposed to wind — they keep rain, wind, and chill out and hold heat in if you add a tent heater. Sidewalls rent per panel, so you can wall in only the weather side.

Do I need a permit for a party tent in BC?

It depends on the municipality and tent size. Many Metro Vancouver municipalities require a permit for larger tents (commonly over 60 square metres / about 645 sq ft) on private property, and parks have their own booking rules. A 20×20 backyard tent rarely needs one; joined marquees for 100+ guests often do. We walk every customer through their city's rules — read our tent permit guide or call 778-990-7983.
