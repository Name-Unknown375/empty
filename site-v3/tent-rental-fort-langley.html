Bedford Landing and heritage village residences — the marquee core

A 20×40 marquee staged on Fort Langley grass — Bedford Landing residence standard.

Bedford Landing residence receptions are the largest single share of Fort Langley marquee bookings. Most are 80–120 guest weddings with a 20×40 or 20×60 marquee on the residential lawn. Newer streets, wider lots, friendlier truck access than the historic village core.

Heritage village residence weddings (the streets around Glover Road and the historic core) run smaller — most accept a 20×30 marquee comfortably; the largest heritage backyards can fit a 20×40. Truck-access logistics are tighter here, and we use shorter delivery windows to avoid blocking storefronts.

The marquee itself is the same on a Bedford Landing lot as on a heritage village backyard. What changes is the install procedure — pre-stage location, crew window length, and equipment positioning around tight side passages.

Fort Langley National Historic Site

Pop-up canopies with sidewalls staged for an outdoor event.

Fort Langley NHS ceremony zones run on heritage permits with strict setback rules. Most accept 20×20 cocktail tents or 20×30 supplemental marquees; full reception marquees rarely fit the permitted footprint. The standard pattern: smaller ceremony marquee at the NHS, reception marquee at the adjacent residential venue.

Parks Canada coordinates the delivery window — we book it through their events team during quoting.

Derby Reach Regional Park and Fort to Fort Trail

Derby Reach’s ceremony zones and the Fort to Fort Trail riverside zones run on park-permit windows. Fraser-adjacent exposure means afternoon onshore wind off the river. Sidewalls year-round; stakes plus supplemental ballast at the river-facing corners.

Most Derby Reach ceremony marquees are 20×20 to 20×30. The receptions flip to an adjacent venue.

Fort Wine Co and intimate-scale venues

Fort Wine Co and the smaller heritage village venues run intimate-scale weddings (40–80 guests typical). Most Fort Wine Co marquee bookings are 20×40 supplemental tents for the outdoor reception zone or cocktail-hour cover. Stakes on grass; full ballast on the hardscape segments.

Glover Road corridor — newer residential

Glover Road corridor addresses straddle the heritage village and the Township edge. Truck access is standard; most lots accept a 20×40 marquee comfortably. Larger lots and rural-edge properties can fit a 20×60 — the standard scale for vineyard-adjacent receptions when the Fort Langley booking sits closer to the agricultural land.

A 100-guest Fort Langley tent rental order

For a 100-guest Fort Langley wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Bedford Landing residence with ceremony in the side garden and reception under marquee on the back lawn — a typical tent-side order looks like:

  • 1 × 20×60 marquee tent (covered reception; ceremony in the side garden is open-air)
  • 4 × tent sidewalls (full enclosure for evening)
  • 8 × bistro string-light strands across the marquee ridge
  • 1 × tent heater (October–April bookings; optional in summer)
  • Engineered ground stakes (default for Bedford Landing lawn)

Setup runs 4–5 hours for a 20×60 + sidewall configuration; teardown is 2–3 hours. Both quoted up front. The 50/100/150/200 rental checklist maps the marquee sizing to your headcount. For heritage village backyards and tighter lots, the configuration scales down to 20×40. Bedford Landing weddings rarely book the marquee alone — chairs, tables, dance floor, and lighting all ship on the same delivery, off our full Fort Langley party rentals catalogue.

Fort Langley Tent Rentals FAQ

What's the standard install for a Bedford Landing or heritage village backyard wedding?
20×40 marquee on the lawn (most common Fort Langley footprint), engineered ground stakes, sidewalls year-round for the open-field installs, bistro lighting along the ridge. Setup runs 3–4 hours; teardown is 2–3. The heritage-village core streets need shorter delivery windows and pre-staged equipment closer to the property line.
Can you install at Fort Langley National Historic Site or Derby Reach Park?
Yes — both run on park/heritage permits with venue-coordinated delivery windows. Most NHS and Derby Reach marquees are 20×20 to 20×30 footprints; full reception tents rarely fit the permitted setbacks. The standard pattern is a smaller ceremony marquee at the park or NHS, with the reception marquee at a Bedford Landing residence or heritage venue.
How do you handle the village's tight street access?
Shorter delivery windows, pre-stage equipment closer to the property line, and tight-window crew install. We complete delivery in compressed windows to avoid blocking village storefronts and visitor traffic. The marquee itself doesn't change; the install logistics do.
What's the Fraser-side wind plan for Derby Reach or Fort to Fort Trail ceremonies?
Fraser-side afternoon wind is more consistent than the urban Township subregions. Stakes plus supplemental ballast at the river-facing corners, sidewalls year-round, marquee size one tier above the strict headcount requirement on exposed sites. Most Fraser-adjacent installs are smaller (20×30) given the permitted setbacks.
Can you do a Fort Wine Co outdoor reception marquee?
Yes — Fort Wine Co's outdoor reception zones accept 20×40 footprints. The venue runs intimate-scale weddings (40–80 guests typical), so most Fort Wine Co tent installs are smaller than the broader Township vineyard standard. Stakes for grass; ballast on any hardscape segments.
