Vancouver tent install patterns we cover

Ceremony chairs staged before tent install.

Park ceremonies

Stanley Park, Queen Elizabeth Park, and VanDusen all run Park Board permit processes for tents above their threshold. Couples pull the permits; we know which ceremony zones at each park accept which marquee footprints. Park installs typically run smaller (20×20 or 20×40) given the protected sightlines.

Rooftop and high-rise installs

Yaletown, downtown, and Mount Pleasant rooftops are common Vancouver tent setups. Three things confirmed up front: freight-elevator dimensions, weight rating, and the building manager’s Saturday availability. Ballast (never stakes), sidewalls year-round.

West End and Kitsilano backyards

Standard backyard pattern: setbacks, slope, sprinkler heads, and where the truck can stop without blocking the alley. Most accept a 20×40 marquee comfortably — that’s the urban backyard ceiling, same 24×44 ft clear rectangle as in the Lower Mainland tent size guide. If you’re between 20×40 and 20×60, measure the rectangle, not the lot. We confirm that on the quote for any backyard we haven’t installed at.

Per-item pricing

The same in Yaletown as in any neighbourhood we serve. Our 20×40 marquee is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, freight elevator, slope — never the tent rate.

Loft conversions and East Vancouver industrial events

Mount Pleasant and East Van industrial-loft conversions have grown into a steady rhythm of wedding receptions, milestone celebrations, and corporate functions. Most lofts are wired for daytime use, not for dance-floor lighting and DJ infrastructure at midnight; we bundle bistro string lights and (where needed) battery power stations onto the same quote. Loft setups typically skip the marquee entirely (the building is the venue) and run on chairs, tables, and lighting alone.

A 100-guest Vancouver tent rental order

For a 100-guest Vancouver wedding — say a Saturday in July with ceremony at a park and reception in a Yaletown event space, where the tent covers the cocktail hour or supplemental outdoor space — a typical tent-side order looks like:

  • 1 × 20×40 marquee tent (covered cocktail hour or supplemental outdoor reception zone)
  • 4 × tent sidewalls (urban wind + evening enclosure)
  • 6 × bistro string-light strands across the marquee ridge
  • 1 × tent heater (October–April bookings)
  • Ballasted weight kits (default for paved or rooftop installs; engineered stakes on grass)

Setup runs 3–4 hours for a 20×40 + sidewall configuration; teardown is 2–3 hours. Both quoted up front. For full ceremony + reception under one tent at a single-venue Vancouver wedding, the 50/100/150/200 rental checklist maps the marquee size to your headcount. Park-permit ceremonies and Yaletown loft receptions usually pair the marquee with chairs, tables, and lighting on one quote — see tables, chairs, and the Vancouver setup for the bundle.

Vancouver Tent Rentals FAQ

What's included on a Vancouver tent rental quote?
Every Vancouver tent quote we send is itemised: the marquee, sidewalls, anchor kit, lighting add-ons, delivery, professional crew install, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Vancouver-zone uplifts, no surprises at the truck.
Can you set up a tent at Stanley Park or Queen Elizabeth Park?
Yes — we deliver to all the major Vancouver park venues regularly. Stanley Park ceremonies require a Vancouver Park Board permit (the couple pulls it, not us), and our crew coordinates the truck-only access window with the Park Board's events team directly. For the full Stanley Park playbook, see our [Stanley Park rental guide](/blog/stanley-park-wedding-rentals-guide).
Do you install marquee tents on Vancouver rooftops?
Yes — Yaletown and downtown rooftop tent installs are common bookings for us. We confirm the building's freight elevator dimensions, weight rating, and the rooftop's structural capacity before quoting. Rooftops always get ballasted weights (never stakes), and we recommend sidewalls year-round given the urban wind tunnel effect.
What tent size for a 100-guest Vancouver wedding?
For a covered ceremony + reception under one tent, a 20×60 marquee is the right starting size for 100 guests. For 150–200 guests, 30×60 or two linked 20×40 tents. Smaller backyard or rooftop venues might cap at 20×40 — we measure footprint during the pre-quote walk for any property we haven't installed at.
How fast can you deliver a Vancouver marquee on short notice?
Same-week is usually feasible for chair, table, and 10×10 popup orders. Marquee tents 20×40 and larger need 7+ days of installer scheduling, especially for park ceremonies and rooftop installs that require building or permit coordination. Call before booking online and we'll tell you straight whether the timeline works.
Do you rent chairs and tables with a Vancouver tent?
Yes — most Vancouver tent bookings add chairs, tables, and lighting on the same truck. This page is tent sizing and install. Vancouver party rentals covers the full setup; chair rentals in Vancouver covers Chiavari, Fanback, and Resin Garden lines.
