Port Moody install patterns

Inlet-facing waterfront residential

Burrard Inlet exposure produces sustained afternoon wind that inland Tri-Cities venues don’t see. Sustained-wind-rated install hardware and commercial-grade LED strands are the default; sidewall lighting on porches handles wind cleanly through the season. Most waterfront installs run the Classic or Premium package.

Heritage Woods elevation

Higher-elevation residential with afternoon wind patterns that lower Moody Centre doesn’t share. Driveway grade access during November narrows earlier than valley-floor residential — we book mountain installs first when peak season arrives. Most properties run the Classic package given the linear footage of mid-to-large rooflines.

Brewery district commercial

Restaurants, tasting rooms, and retail exteriors along Brewers Row and the Inlet-side commercial strip. Commercial installs need building-management coordination on access; we book after-5pm or weekend install timing to avoid disrupting tenants. Inlet wind funnels directly through the brewery district — sustained-wind-rated patio-area lighting is the standard.

Rocky Point and Moody Centre residential

Mid-density residential closer to the waterfront amenity zone. Most run the Starter or Classic package — warm-white C9 along the main roofline with optional tree accents. Less wind exposure than direct waterfront properties, so standard install hardware works.

Free off-season storage and the year-2 math

Year one, you pay for design, install, lights, takedown, and storage as a fixed package. Year two, the math gets meaningfully better — your strands are already in our warehouse. For Inlet waterfront and Heritage Woods installs especially, the sustained-wind-rated commercial-grade LEDs are a significant year-one cost; year-2 pricing typically drops 20–30% because those materials are already paid for.

A typical Port Moody waterfront install

For an Inlet-facing two-storey home with rooflines, two front-yard trees, and a covered porch, a typical Classic package install looks like:

  • ~150 linear feet of warm-white C9 LED along the main and gable rooflines (sustained-wind-rated hardware)
  • ~50 linear feet of porch wrap and column accent
  • ~80 linear feet of mini-lights wrapping front-yard trees (sheltered side)
  • ~30 linear feet of pathway lights along the front walk
  • Wind-rated clip-style attachment, WCB-covered crew
  • One on-site design walk-through, season-long maintenance, January takedown, free storage

Setup runs 5–6 hours; takedown 3–4 hours. Both quoted up front.

Port Moody Christmas Light FAQ

How much does Christmas light installation cost in Port Moody?
Port Moody installations run $8.50–$12 per linear foot. Inlet-facing waterfront and Heritage Woods elevation properties sometimes price slightly higher within that range given the wind-rated install requirements. Quote covers commercial-grade LED strands, design walk-through, install, season-long maintenance, takedown, and free off-season storage.
Do you install Christmas lights at Inlet-facing waterfront homes?
Yes — Inlet-facing residential blocks along the Burrard Inlet are some of our most-photogenic Port Moody installs. Open-water wind exposure is meaningful; we use sustained-wind-rated install hardware and commercial-grade LED strands rather than delicate mini-lights on exposed rooflines. Sidewall lighting on porches handles the wind cleanly through the season.
Can you do Brewery District commercial installs?
Yes — brewery district restaurants, retail, and tasting-room exteriors along Brewers Row are routine Port Moody commercial installs. Commercial installs need building-management coordination on access windows; we book the slot and confirm permits during quoting. Most commercial installs run after-5pm install timing to avoid disrupting tenants.
When do you install in Port Moody — and when's the deadline to book?
Port Moody installs run mid-October through early December. Booking opens in August; September–early October is the comfortable window. Heritage Woods installs sometimes book earlier than valley-floor residential because higher-elevation weather windows narrow as the season progresses.
What if a strand fails during the season?
We're on-site within 24 to 48 hours to replace it — no charge. Most installers make you wait until spring. We don't.
What happens to my lights after the season?
We come back, take it all down, label your strands by section, and store them in our climate-controlled warehouse until next October. No storage fee. Year two onward, you skip the buy-new step entirely.
