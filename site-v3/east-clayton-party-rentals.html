Mid-event swaps and warehouse pickup

Two operational features matter for East Clayton specifically. Warehouse pickup: chair, table, popup tent, and dance-floor section pickup is available at our warehouse for budget-conscious East Clayton bookings — drops the delivery line item entirely.

East Clayton FAQ

What's included on an East Clayton rental quote?
Every East Clayton quote we send is itemised: rental items by line, delivery, crew setup time, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no East Clayton uplifts, no surprises at the truck. If your quote says $X, you pay $X.
Do you handle Cloverdale Rodeo Grounds events?
Yes — Cloverdale Rodeo Grounds events (community gatherings, fairs, corporate functions, charity events) are regular bookings for our crew. The venue runs its own access protocol; we coordinate vendor sign-in and equipment staging during quoting. Most Rodeo Grounds setups skew toward higher-volume seating (Fanback chairs, banquet tables) without a marquee.
Can you tent in a Clayton Heights backyard?
Yes — Clayton Heights backyard installs are some of our most-frequent East Clayton bookings. Most lots accept a 20×20 to 20×40 marquee comfortably. Engineered ground stakes are our default anchor on grass; we confirm slope, sprinkler infrastructure, and alley access during the pre-quote walk.
How clean are the chairs and tables when they arrive?
Every chair is individually wiped and inspected before it leaves our warehouse. Cushions are spot-cleaned, legs checked for damage, spindles checked for cracks. Tables get the same treatment. Anything that doesn't meet our condition bar comes off the inventory roster, not onto your booking.
Can you do mid-event swaps in East Clayton?
Yes — and proximity is the reason. If a chair leg fails, a table breaks, or you suddenly need 20 more cocktail highboys mid-reception, replacements can typically be on-site within 60–75 minutes for any East Clayton event. We don't promise this for cities further out.
How much do party rentals cost in East Clayton?
Party rental pricing in East Clayton depends on the items and your guest count. As a guide, chairs run $3.25–$8.50 each per day, 6-foot banquet and 5-foot round tables $10.95–$13.50 per day, popup tents from $75 per day, and marquee tents from $550 per event — setup and takedown included. Bundling a tent, tables and chairs into one of our packages saves 10%, and paying in full within 24 hours of your quote saves another 10%. Build your order online for an instant, itemised East Clayton quote — delivery and setup are priced up front with no hidden fees or fuel surcharges.
What does the delivery and setup fee cover?
When you add delivery and setup to your order, our crew handles round-trip transport, professional setup of tents and dance floors, takedown, and pickup. Chairs and tables are stacked and placed where you want them. Delivery and setup start at $175 and depend on distance from our Surrey warehouse — quoted up front with no hidden charges or fuel surcharges. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.
Can you set up a tent on grass, gravel, or a paved driveway?
Yes to all three. Our marquee tents use stake-down installation on grass and ballasted (weighted) installation on pavement, gravel, and stamped concrete. Tell us the surface when you book and we'll send the right hardware. We do not tent on artificial turf.
What happens if the weather is bad or I need to cancel?
If you need to reschedule because of weather, we'll roll your deposit forward to a new date at no charge — as long as we're notified before crew dispatch. Your date is locked once the deposit is paid; deposits aren't refundable if you cancel outright.
