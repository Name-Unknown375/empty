### Fort Langley National Historic Site

Fort Langley NHS ceremony zones are heritage-permit coordinated and accept smaller chair counts (40–100 typical). Resin Garden handles the morning Fraser-side dew best; Chiavari works for the indoor heritage-building receptions. We coordinate the delivery window with the Parks Canada events team during quoting.

### Heritage village streets and Glover Road corridor

The historic village's tight streets need shorter delivery windows and pre-staged equipment. We stage close to the property line and complete delivery in compressed crew windows to avoid blocking storefront and visitor traffic. Glover Road corridor addresses get standard delivery.

### Derby Reach Regional Park

Derby Reach's ceremony zones run on park-permit windows with Fraser River exposure. Most ceremony zones accept 50–100 chair configurations. Resin Garden for the outdoor rows; Chiavari indoors if the reception flips to an adjacent venue.

### Bedford Landing — newer residential

Bedford Landing's residential streets are newer and accept standard delivery patterns. Most Bedford Landing backyard weddings are 80–120 guests with a Chiavari-only configuration under marquee.

### Fort Wine Co and the smaller heritage venues

Fort Wine Co and the smaller heritage village venues run intimate-scale chair bookings — 40–80 guests is typical. Chiavari for the indoor or covered reception, Fanback or Resin Garden for adjacent outdoor ceremonies. Single quote, single delivery, no mixing surcharge.

### Per-item pricing

The same on Bedford Landing as in the heritage village. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — village-street staging time, permit windows — never the per-chair rate.
