## Boundary Bay wind plan — what's different about Tsawwassen installs

![A clean white marquee tent set against a vibrant blue sky with fluffy clouds](/images/lifestyle/tent-golden-hour-2.jpg)
*A 20×60 marquee with sidewalls up — Tsawwassen installs default to enclosure year-round.*

A Tsawwassen marquee is structurally identical to a Burnaby marquee with doubled anchor weight and full sidewalls — same template we use for West Vancouver waterfront and Deep Cove installs. The tent itself doesn't change. What changes is install procedure.

Three specific patterns drive that:

- **Southwest-facing peninsula exposure.** Most Tsawwassen residences face Boundary Bay or the Strait of Georgia — and the afternoon onshore wind is consistent enough year-round that sidewalls aren't optional. They go up Friday afternoon at install and stay up through teardown Sunday.
- **Cliff-edge airflow at English Bluff.** The English Bluff residences sit on top of the peninsula's cliff edge with concentrated airflow patterns that lower-elevation Tsawwassen Shores residences don't see. Doubled anchor strategy is the default.
- **Ferry-terminal traffic scheduling.** Highway 17 delivery aligns with ferry-loading windows. We schedule installs to avoid peak crossings, especially summer weekends.

## English Bluff — cliff-edge residence installs

![A large high-peak tent and two canopies set up in a backyard beside a pool](/images/lifestyle/backyard-party-tents-pool.jpg){.float-right}
*Main tent plus two canopies around a backyard pool.*

English Bluff's cliff-edge residences host some of the most-photographed Lower Mainland wedding sightlines — ocean-and-island backdrops with mountain-edge framing. Most are 100–150 guest configurations with a 20×60 marquee on the residence lawn.

The cliff-edge wind plan is the operational anchor of every English Bluff install:

- Engineered stakes paired with concrete ballast at every leg.
- Sidewalls at install (not optional, not "if it rains").
- Marquee oriented with the open side away from the prevailing wind line — we walk the lot during quoting to confirm.

## Tsawwassen Shores and waterfront residential

Tsawwassen Shores' newer waterfront residential streets accept standard delivery patterns with friendly truck access. Most Tsawwassen Shores weddings are 100–150 guests with a 20×60 marquee on the lawn.

The waterfront orientation still calls for stakes-plus-ballast at the bay-facing corners and sidewalls year-round — the airflow is gentler than English Bluff but still consistent enough that "optional sidewalls" isn't a category.

## Beach Grove and ferry-terminal-adjacent acreage

Beach Grove's shoreline-adjacent residences and the ferry-terminal-adjacent peninsula acreage lots host the larger Tsawwassen weddings (150–200 guests). 30×60 marquees on the acreage lawns are routine; truck access is rural-road and ample.

Tsawwassen Village heritage venues and restaurant lawns occasionally book supplemental marquees for outdoor cocktail-hour cover. 20×20 to 20×40 footprints; Village-street truck access is tighter than the residential edges and we use shorter delivery windows.
