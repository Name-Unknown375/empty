## White Rock tent install patterns we cover

![A large high-peak tent and two canopies set up in a backyard beside a pool](/images/lifestyle/backyard-party-tents-pool.jpg){.float-right}
*Main tent plus two canopies around a backyard pool.*

### White Rock Pier and Marine Drive promenade

Tight permit setbacks, constant onshore wind, and city event-coordination. Stakes-free installs (full ballast every leg), sidewalls year-round, marquee size adjusted to fit the specific permit zone.

### West Beach and East Beach — sand installs

Beach-sand installs cap at 20×40 given the ballast load required to hold the marquee against the wind. Sand can't hold stakes; we use full ballast at every leg. Sidewalls are mandatory on any beach install we do — no exceptions.

### Semiahmoo Park and peninsula acreage

Grass installs with stakes plus ballast. Larger marquees (20×60, 30×60) are routine on the peninsula's acreage lots; truck access is rural-road with longer driveways.

### Peace Arch Park

Park-permit coordination required; we book the install window during quoting. Standard grass install with stakes plus ballast.

### Uptown and Centennial Park area — hillside backyards

Hillside backyard installs need slope-aware anchor strategies — supplemental ballast on the downhill side for steeper grades. We walk the lot during quoting if the slope is significant.

### Per-item pricing

The same on the beach as on the peninsula. Our 20×40 marquee is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — anchor strategy, after-hours timing — never the tent rate.
