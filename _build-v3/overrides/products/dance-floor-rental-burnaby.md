## What our Burnaby dance floor lineup covers

Three floor sizes that cover almost every Burnaby event:

- **12×12 portable dance floor** — 60–80 guests, intimate venues, smaller heritage rooms.
- **12×12 portable dance floor** — the workhorse. 100-guest weddings, most marquee setups, most rooftop installs.
- **16×16 portable dance floor** — 150–200 guests, larger acreage and corporate-gala receptions.
- **Subfloor panels** — engineered platforms that level grass, sloped lawns, and uneven patios beneath the dance floor. Adds 90–120 minutes to setup.
- **Edge ramps** — included on every floor install. Trip-hazard safety, not aesthetic.

> The floor goes down level. Heels don't catch panel seams. We don't ship a floor that bounces under load.

### Heritage venue installs

Hart House, Burnaby Village Museum, and the Shadbolt Centre all have existing flooring that accepts the dance floor directly with edge ramps only. No subfloor needed; setup runs 45–60 minutes for a 12×12.

### Brentwood and Metrotown rooftops

Rooftop dance floors need three things confirmed before quoting: freight-elevator dimensions, weight rating, and the rooftop's structural capacity. Floor panels at <60 lb each fit nearly every freight elevator. Ballast goes on tent components; the floor sits flat on the rooftop surface.

### Deer Lake Park and backyard receptions

Grass installs require engineered subfloor underneath the dance floor. Adds 90–120 minutes to setup but ensures the floor stays gap-free under 80–100 dancing guests at midnight.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Brentwood as on Deer Lake. Our 12×12 portable dance floor is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery and subfloor — surface, elevator, slope — never the per-floor rate.

## A 100-guest Burnaby dance floor rental order

For a 100-guest Burnaby wedding at Deer Lake Park's lakefront lawn under marquee, a typical dance floor order looks like:

- 1 × **12×12 portable dance floor** (standard for 100 guests)
- Subfloor panels (level the grass beneath the floor)
- Edge ramps on all four sides
- Crew install (90–120 minutes for floor + subfloor)

For a Hart House heritage reception with existing flooring, the same 12×12 ships without subfloor — install drops to 45–60 minutes. For a Brentwood rooftop reception, the floor sits flat on the surface; check freight-elevator capacity during quoting.
