## Surface, wind, and the Langley anchor strategy

### Engineered stakes on farm soil

Stakes hold meaningfully better than ballasted weights on irrigated agricultural land. We confirm sprinkler infrastructure and irrigation lines before staking; on irrigated properties we ballast instead.

### Open-valley wind

Afternoon thermal patterns produce gust loads at agritourism venues and rural acreages that flat urban yards don't see. Sidewalls year-round on any open-field installation.

### Vineyard and berry-farm staging

Row spacing, harvest paths, and tasting-room hours determine our staging window. We don't drive across irrigation lines or stage equipment in active rows. Coordination with the venue's events team happens during quoting.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Township 7 lawn as in any Langley backyard. Our 20×40 marquee is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — surface, sprinkler routing, distance — never the tent rate.
