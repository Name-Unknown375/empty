## Hatzic, Silverhill, Heritage Park — Mission chair patterns

### Hatzic Valley and Hatzic Lake acreage

Hatzic Valley acreage weddings drive the upscale Mission calendar. Most are 100–150 guest configurations with a two-chair-line setup. Hatzic Lake outdoor ceremonies run on lakeside grass; Resin Garden every time. Truck access on Hatzic-area rural roads is ample.

### Silverhill and Cedar Valley — valley-view acreage

Silverhill and Cedar Valley sit higher up the valley with longer driveways and more pronounced mountain-and-valley views. Most are 100–150 guests with Chiavari-only configurations under marquee. The view is the photographic anchor; the chairs frame it.

### Fraser River Heritage Park

Fraser River Heritage Park's river-overlook ceremony zones are heritage-permit coordinated. Resin Garden for the damp-grass and river-mist exposure. Hand-carry from the parking lot to the specific ceremony zone for some configurations.

### Westminster Abbey-adjacent and abbey-view venues

The Benedictine monastery at Westminster Abbey doesn't host weddings, but its hillside position shapes the photo aesthetic at the surrounding venues. Abbey-view restaurant and residence receptions skew Chiavari-only with intimate guest counts (60–100 typical).

### Mission Leisure Centre and downtown community events

Mission Leisure Centre and downtown Mission community-venue events run on Fanback folding for the high-headcount registrations and seated-row community events. Truck access is standard.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Hatzic acreage as at a downtown Mission venue. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The Lougheed corridor delivery line is the variable, not the per-chair rate.
