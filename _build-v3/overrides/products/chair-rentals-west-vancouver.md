### Whytecliff, Lighthouse Park, Ambleside ocean ceremonies

Cart-carry from the upper parking lot is the operational reality. Resin Garden weighs less and stacks tighter than Chiavari, which makes a meaningful difference on a 100-chair cart-down to the lower beach. Tidal timing matters for setup — see the [Whytecliff Park rental guide](/blog/whytecliff-park-event-rental-guide).

### British Properties estate receptions

Terraced lawns with city-light backdrops. Chiavari is the standard; setup includes levelling on uneven grass where needed. Estate driveways are wide enough for our delivery vehicle in nearly every case — the lawn access is the question, not the road.

### Park Royal and Marine Drive corporate

Mid-rise office spaces and the larger Park Royal venues run their own freight-elevator timing. Fanback folding ships compactly through urban elevators; Chiavari fits but takes more trips. We confirm dimensions and weight rating before quoting.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Caulfeild as on the Ambleside seawall. Our Chiavari is one rate, full stop. The line that changes is delivery — bridge timing, cart-carry, ferry schedule — never the per-chair rate.
