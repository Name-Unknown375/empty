### Vineyard and berry-farm ceremonies

Krause Berry Farms, Township 7, and the smaller agritourism properties have row spacing and harvesting calendars that determine where chairs stage. We coordinate during quoting; we don't drive across irrigation lines.

### Acreage receptions

Full mowed-grass setup. Chiavari for receptions under marquee, Fanback for larger ceremony rows, mixed configurations common. Most Langley acreage weddings split 100 ceremony chairs (Fanback) + 100 reception chairs (Chiavari) when guest count exceeds 100.

### Campbell Valley Regional Park

Open-air park ceremony zones with farmland-style afternoon wind. Chair-only setups work without marquee infrastructure for forest-canopy zones; meadow and clearing zones accept marquee + Chiavari.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Brookswood acreage as at Township 7. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, surface, multi-venue — never the per-chair rate.
