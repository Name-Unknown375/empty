## Swaneset, Pitt Lake, Osprey Village — Pitt Meadows chair patterns

### Swaneset Bay Resort & Country Club

Swaneset drives most of the Pitt Meadows wedding calendar. Most are 120–200 guest configurations — the resort's golf-course setting and banquet halls accommodate the larger guest counts. Standard ceremony-reception arc: Resin Garden for the outdoor course-adjacent ceremony lawn, Chiavari for the indoor banquet reception. For winter or shoulder-season weddings, the resort's indoor ceremony spaces use Chiavari throughout.

### Pitt Lake shoreline

Pitt Lake's shoreline ceremony zones are seasonal — most happen May through September given the lake-edge access patterns. Resin Garden every time; the lakefront grass and shoreline surface don't tolerate Chiavari well across a full ceremony arc.

### Osprey Village waterfront

Osprey Village's pier and adjacent restaurants run intimate-scale weddings (60–100 guests typical). Chiavari for the indoor restaurant receptions; Resin Garden for any waterfront pier-adjacent ceremony zone.

### Pitt-Addington Marsh and nature-reserve ceremonies

Pitt-Addington Marsh and the surrounding nature-reserve zones run on permit-coordinated delivery for outdoor ceremonies. Resin Garden for the marsh-edge ceremony surface — the morning marsh-mist and protected-grass dampness don't tolerate Chiavari.

### Central Meadows and South Bonson backyards

Standard residential backyard patterns. Most lots accept any chair configuration; truck access is friendly across the residential streets.

### Per-item pricing

The same at Swaneset as at a Central Meadows backyard. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The Lougheed corridor delivery line is the variable, not the per-chair rate.
