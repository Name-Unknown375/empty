## Where the install pattern changes

![A marquee tent set up at a backyard wedding with sidewalls open](/images/lifestyle/tent-setup.jpg){.float-right}
*A 20×40 with sidewalls open on a mid-bench West Van property.*

### Ocean-side installs (Ambleside, Dundarave, waterfront homes)

Howe Sound inflow wind shapes everything. Sidewalls year-round. Ballast at every leg paired with stakes where the surface accepts them. Marquee sized one tier above the strict headcount requirement to absorb gusts.

### British Properties estate lawns

Terraced grade is the operational reality. Most estates accept a 20×60 comfortably on the main lawn; the dance floor portion typically needs subfloor levelling. We grade-check during quoting and budget the levelling time before the install starts.

### Cypress Park and mountain-adjacent venues

Elevation-driven afternoon wind is predictable from late morning. Same doubled anchor strategy as ocean-side installs, plus we recommend booking the install for the morning shift to finish before the afternoon wind pattern builds.

### Whytecliff and Lighthouse Park

Tight venues. Full marquees rarely work here; 10×10 popups for the officiant or cake-table cover are the common pattern. Reception happens at an adjacent estate or waterfront home, and we move the full marquee setup there.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Caulfeild as in Horseshoe Bay. Our 20×40 marquee is one rate, full stop. The line that changes is delivery and anchor — ballast count, ferry timing, cart access — never the tent rate.

## Ocean-side anchor strategy and the wind plan

A West Van waterfront marquee is structurally a Burnaby marquee with twice the anchor weight and full sidewalls. The marquee itself doesn't change; what changes is the install procedure. Every leg gets ballast (concrete weights, not water-fillable, because water leaks and concrete doesn't). Engineered stakes pair with ballast wherever the surface accepts them — sandy lawn, grass, even some packed gravel. Sidewalls go up at install, not "if it rains" — Howe Sound weather changes in fifteen minutes and we don't bring sidewalls back to install mid-reception. The [Lower Mainland tent size guide](/blog/tent-size-guide-lower-mainland-wedding) covers the footprint math; the anchor strategy is what's specific to West Van.
