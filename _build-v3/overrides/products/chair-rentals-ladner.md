## Westham Island, Ladner Village, Reifel — Ladner chair patterns

### Westham Island farm weddings

Westham Island agritourism farms run a steady share of the Ladner wedding calendar. Cross the single-lane bridge, deliver to the farm, install on the lawn or farm-edge clearing. Most Westham Island weddings are 80–120 guests with a two-chair-line setup. Resin Garden for the outdoor farm ceremony (the morning grass is dew-prone, the farm-edge surface is uneven), Chiavari for the reception inside the farmhouse or under marquee on the lawn.

### Ladner Village heritage and 48 Avenue

Ladner Village's heritage streets and 48 Avenue restaurant row run smaller weddings (60–100 guests typical) with Chiavari-dominant configurations. Truck access is tight; shorter delivery windows and pre-stage close to the property line are the install pattern.

### George C. Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary

Reifel Sanctuary's nature-walk and pond-adjacent ceremony zones run on permits with strict setback rules. Resin Garden for the damp-grass and marsh-adjacent ceremonies. Hand-carry chairs from the parking lot to the specific ceremony zone.

### Port Guichon and Holly — waterfront residential

Port Guichon's waterfront residential streets and Holly's adjacent residential blocks accept standard delivery patterns. Most Port Guichon weddings are 80–120 guests on the residence lawn with Chiavari-only configurations under marquee.

### Millennium Park and community events

Millennium Park and Ladner community-venue events run on Fanback folding for the larger seated-row configurations. Park-permit delivery windows are standard.

### Per-item pricing

The same on Westham Island as in Ladner Village. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — bridge timing, heritage-street access, permit windows — never the per-chair rate.
