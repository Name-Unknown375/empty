## Heritage, corporate, rooftop, backyard — Burnaby chair patterns

### Heritage venues (Hart House, Burnaby Village Museum)

Venue-coordinated delivery slots, smaller chair counts (often under 100), and a strong preference for Chiavari to match the historic aesthetic. Heritage venues typically don't allow Fanback folding for the reception itself; they're acceptable for outdoor ceremony rows on adjacent lawns.

### Corporate plaza and rooftop events

Controlled freight elevators, after-5pm timing constraints, security-desk vendor sign-in. We confirm building access before quoting. Chiavari chairs are the standard for Brentwood and Metrotown corporate gatherings; Fanback folding for tenant-event registrations or larger seated audiences.

### Park ceremonies (Deer Lake)

Truck access timed to the venue's permit window, soft lakefront grass for ballast-anchored ceremony chairs, and re-stage to reception venue when applicable.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Brentwood as on Deer Lake. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — elevator access, after-hours timing, distance — never the per-chair rate.

## Same-day rooftop deliveries and mid-event swaps

Burnaby's rooftop event density makes freight-elevator coordination part of every quote. For mid-event swaps (a chair leg fails, a cushion tears, you suddenly need 20 more for walk-ins), Burnaby's proximity to our warehouse means replacements can typically be on-site within 60–90 minutes during business hours.
