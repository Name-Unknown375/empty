*A 20×60 marquee staged on a Yorkson residential lawn.*

Willoughby's residential streets are some of the friendliest install conditions in the Township. Yorkson, Willoughby Heights, and Routley are newer developments with:

- Wider lot frontage than the established Township villages
- Paved approach streets with ample maneuvering room
- Standard driveway depth at most addresses
- Few overhead-branch constraints compared to mature-tree neighbourhoods

Most installs are 20×40 to 20×60 marquees on the residential lawn. The marquee itself is the same template we use across the Lower Mainland; the install efficiency is what makes Willoughby a friendly delivery.

## A 100-guest Willoughby tent rental order

For a 100-guest Willoughby wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Yorkson residence with ceremony at Sendall Gardens and reception under marquee on the residence lawn — a typical tent-side order looks like:

- 1 × **20×60 marquee tent** at the residence (Sendall Gardens ceremony is chairs-only)
- 4 × **tent sidewalls** (full enclosure for evening)
- 8 × **bistro string-light strands** across the marquee ridge
- 1 × **tent heater** (October–April bookings; optional in summer)
