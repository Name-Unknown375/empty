## New West install patterns

### Queen's Park Victorian heritage

Detailed trim, complex rooflines, protected sightlines. We use clip-style installs that respect the heritage character — no staples, no nails. Most Queen's Park homes run the Classic or Premium package given the linear footage of detailed roofline trim. Pricing typically lands in the $9.50–$12/ft range.

### Sapperton residential

Older homes with detailed character and established yards. Most run the Classic package — warm-white C9 along main and gable rooflines plus accent strands on porches and tree wraps. $9–$11/ft pricing.

### Pier Park and Fraser-River-adjacent

Fraser River exposure produces afternoon wind that more inland New West venues don't see. Sustained-wind-rated install hardware on the riverside-facing rooflines; sidewall lighting on porches handles wind cleanly through the season.

### Anvil Centre and downtown commercial

Storefront facades, restaurant exteriors, hotel buildings, civic-area retail. Commercial installs need building-management coordination on access windows; we book the slot during quoting. Most run on warm-white C9 or alternating-pattern designs.

### Queensborough industrial-loft

Converted industrial spaces sometimes book year-round festive lighting or Christmas-season tenant-event installs. Freight-elevator access coordinated with building management; non-damaging mounting on heritage industrial finishes.
