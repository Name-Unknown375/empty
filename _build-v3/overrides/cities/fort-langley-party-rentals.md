## Heritage village constraints and Fort Langley operational realities

### Village-core access is the bottleneck

Narrow streets, heritage-protected sightlines, and limited staging. Some village addresses require hand-carry from the road. We confirm access during quoting, and we factor any hand-carry distance into the crew time line — not surfaced as a surprise on event day.

### Heritage venues skip the marquee

Historic Site, Bedford Landing's heritage character, and Glover Road's older properties typically run on chairs, tables, and lighting rather than full tent setups. Small-scale Chiavari + banquet + bistro is the most common Fort Langley village booking.

### River and farmland wind

Bedford Landing's Fraser River frontage and Derby Reach's open zones both produce afternoon wind that village-interior addresses don't see. Sidewalls year-round for outdoor receptions; ballasted weights for waterfront installs.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Glover Road acreage as in the village core. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes is delivery — access, distance, hand-carry — never the per-item rate.

## Bedford Landing condo and waterfront events

Bedford Landing's mix of waterfront condos and townhomes has grown into a regular slice of our Fort Langley calendar — milestone parties, engagement celebrations, and smaller wedding receptions on Fraser River-facing balconies and shared courtyards. Condo events need freight-elevator dimensions and weight ratings confirmed before quoting; courtyard events need ballast (most Bedford Landing common areas don't allow staking) and almost always sidewalls. The river breeze is meaningful enough that even a summer cocktail hour wants windbreak. For full Fort Langley wedding planning, our [Fort Langley wedding venues guide](/blog/fort-langley-wedding-venues-rental-guide) covers the village's most-booked sites.

## An 80-guest Fort Langley village wedding rental order

For a smaller 80-guest Fort Langley wedding — say a Saturday in August at the Historic Site with reception at a Glover Road acreage — a typical rental order looks like:

- 80 × **white Chiavari chairs** (ceremony at the Historic Site, then re-staged at the Glover Road reception)
- 10 × **5ft round tables** for the reception
- 5 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration)
- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor**
- 2 × **cocktail highboys** for the cocktail hour
- Bistro string lighting (Glover Road reception)
- Optional: 1 × **20×40 marquee tent** if the reception is fully outdoors

Setup at the Historic Site runs 2–3 hours (chairs only); the Glover Road reception adds 3–4 hours for tables, lighting, and (if booked) marquee. Both quoted up front.
