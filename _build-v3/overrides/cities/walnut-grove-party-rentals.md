### Older trees, mature yards

Walnut Grove's tree cover is more mature than Willoughby's newer blocks. We measure marquee peak clearance against existing tree canopy during the pre-quote walk; our 13' peak fits most but not all yards.

### Backyard reception scale

Most Walnut Grove backyard weddings run 60–120 guests under a 20×40 marquee with cocktail hour staged in the front yard or side patio. Established-suburb lot sizes give us flexibility on layout that newer-development townhouse complexes don't.

### Derby Reach Park access from the south

Walnut Grove sits on the south approach to Derby Reach Regional Park — the trailhead and ceremony zones along the Fort to Fort Trail are accessed from this side. We coordinate Derby Reach permits with Metro Vancouver Regional Parks during quoting.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Walnut Grove as in Willoughby or Fort Langley. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes is delivery — distance from our warehouse, alley access, anchor — never the per-item rate.
