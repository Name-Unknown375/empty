## Coastal wind, sunset light, and Tsawwassen operational realities

![Rows of white ceremony chairs facing a decorated altar with trees and water visible behind](/images/lifestyle/ceremony-chairs-2.jpg){.float-right}
*A bluff-top ceremony staged for sunset photography.*

### Coastal wind is the design constraint

The Strait of Georgia produces afternoon thermal patterns that inland Delta venues don't see. Ballasted weight kits at every leg, sidewalls year-round, and a marquee size adjusted for evening enclosure — even summer evenings cool fast on the bluff.

### Sand and saturated soil

Centennial Beach's direct-sand zones skew toward Resin Garden chairs (resin frame and seat handle saturated sand). Chiavari hardwood doesn't love sand contact. English Bluff and Tsawwassen Shores' grassy bluff-tops accept Chiavari fine, but the soil holds enough moisture that ballast outperforms staking.

### Sunset ceremony timing

Tsawwassen sunset weddings cluster in the 7:30–9:00 PM window from June through August. We dispatch the truck and crew to be set 90 minutes before ceremony start so the photographer has clean sightlines for golden hour and the ceremony begins at the planned time.

### Per-item pricing

The same on Centennial Beach as at Tsawwassen Mills. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — coastal anchor, beach access, distance — never the per-item rate.
