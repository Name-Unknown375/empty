## Delivery, setup, and what's actually on the quote

A Vancouver quote from us is itemised end-to-end. No fuel surcharges. No zone uplifts. No surprises at the truck.

### Delivery windows

We give you a one-hour delivery window when we book the date — not "morning of." Vancouver traffic is real, and Friday-afternoon load-ins and Sunday-morning teardowns get planned around it. We plan around the traffic so you don't pay for it.

### Crew setup

Our crew levels the tent on slope, ballasts the legs where staking isn't allowed (rooftops, paved lots, certain park surfaces), positions chairs and tables to your floor plan, and lays the dance floor on a level subfloor where the surface needs it. Mid-event re-staging — ceremony-to-reception flips on the same chair set — is included on single-tent bookings.

### Same-day load-in and load-out

Feasible for most Vancouver venues. The exception is permit-restricted park ceremonies where the access window is tight; on those, we flag a separate mobilization day on the quote so the timeline isn't a surprise.

## A 100-guest Vancouver wedding rental order

![Cocktail-hour highboy tables set up on an outdoor patio with guests in conversation in the background](/images/products/cocktail-tables.webp){.float-left}
*Highboy cocktail tables for the cocktail hour — covered, weighted, ready before doors open.*

A typical 100-guest Vancouver wedding — say a Saturday in July, ceremony in a park setting like Stanley Park, reception under marquee on a private lawn — looks like this on the rental side:

- 1 × **20×40 marquee tent** with bistro lighting and optional sidewalls
- 100 × **white Chiavari chairs** (80 staged for ceremony rows, then re-positioned at reception tables — same chairs, single delivery, our crew handles the flip)
- 12 × **5ft round tables** (8 guests per table = 96, plus 2 extras for kids and the cake/buffet lines)
- 8 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar, plus two for the buffet)
- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor**
- 2 × **highboy cocktail tables** for the cocktail hour

Our crew arrives around 7 AM for marquee setup, finishes by midday, and reposts late evening for teardown. Total on-site time across both visits: about 16 hours, all included in the quote.
