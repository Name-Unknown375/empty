## Foothill elevation, river wind, and Mission operational realities

### Foothill elevation changes the wind plan

Hatzic, Silverhill, and Cedar Valley properties climb the foothills behind Mission's town centre. Even modest elevation gain produces afternoon thermal patterns that valley-floor venues don't see. Engineered stakes paired with ballast on leeward corners is our default for unfamiliar foothill properties.

### Fraser River Heritage Park

The park's riverfront ceremony zones and pavilion areas are some of Mission's most-booked sites. River-adjacent installs need ballast (saturated soil holds stakes poorly), sidewalls year-round, and a marquee size adjusted for evening enclosure.

### Westminster Abbey-area setups

The Benedictine monastery's grounds and the surrounding foothill properties have a contemplative character that most Mission couples want to preserve in the rental setup — smaller-scale, lower-profile installs without aggressive lighting or a dance floor's visual footprint. Many Abbey-area weddings stay under 80 guests.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Hatzic foothill as in Mission town centre. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — elevation, river anchor, distance — never the per-item rate.

## Mission Raceway and community-event scale

Mission Raceway Park brings a different operational rhythm — motorsport events, charity functions, and community gatherings that often need 200+ Fanback folding chairs and 25+ banquet tables, no marquee. These setups ship on one truck and stage in 3–4 hours. Lead times here are typically 3–4 weeks rather than the 6–8 we recommend for peak-season weddings; community organizers usually have approved-vendor lists and faster procurement cycles.

## A 100-guest Mission wedding rental order

For a 100-guest Mission wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Hatzic foothill acreage with ceremony and reception running outdoors and the Coast Mountains in the photo backdrop — a typical order looks like:

- 1 × **20×60 marquee tent** with sidewalls (covered ceremony + reception in single footprint)
- 100 × **white Chiavari chairs** (ceremony rows, then re-staged at reception tables)
- 12 × **5ft round tables** for the reception
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor**
- 2 × **cocktail highboys** for the cocktail hour
- Bistro string lighting along the marquee ridge
- Engineered ground stakes paired with ballast (default for foothill grass)

Setup runs 5–6 hours; teardown 3–4. Both quoted up front. For smaller Westminster Abbey-area weddings (often under 80 guests, no dance floor), the order shrinks accordingly.
