## Inlet wind, forest canopy, and Port Moody operational realities

*Ceremony rows on grass with water behind. Shared lifestyle photo — not a named Port Moody lawn.*

### Inlet-exposed waterfront sites

Rocky Point, Shoreline Trail, and the Moody Centre waterfront all sit on Burrard Inlet exposure that inland Tri-Cities venues don't share. Ballasted weights and sidewalls year-round; we size up the marquee against forecast.

### Forested ceremony sites

Buntzen Lake's lakefront and Bert Flinn's wooded zones have tree cover that constrains marquee peak height. We pre-walk forest sites we haven't delivered to, or skip the marquee entirely for chair-only ceremonies under the canopy. Our 13' peak fits most but not all sites.

### Higher-elevation residential

Heritage Woods' elevation produces afternoon wind that lower Moody Centre doesn't see. We plan accordingly for any property above the Inlet's flat zone.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Heritage Woods as on Rocky Point. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — wind, anchor, elevation — never the per-item rate.

## Brewery district and Inlet-side restaurant patio events

Port Moody's Inlet-side brewery district and the Brewers Row corridor have grown into a steady stream of corporate events, milestone parties, and post-wedding receptions in the last few years. Most setups are smaller-footprint (20×20 popups, 1–2 highboys, bistro lighting for the patio) and run on 3–4 week lead times rather than the 6–8 weeks we recommend for full-scale weddings. Patio installs need ballast (no staking on pavement) and almost always sidewalls — Inlet wind funnels right down through the brewery district.

## A 100-guest Port Moody wedding rental order

For a 100-guest Port Moody wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Rocky Point area ceremony followed by an Inlet-facing reception — a typical order looks like:

- 1 × **20×60 marquee tent** with sidewalls (Inlet wind + evening enclosure)
- 100 × **white Chiavari chairs** (ceremony rows, then re-staged at reception tables)
- 12 × **5ft round tables** for the reception
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor**
- 2 × **cocktail highboys** for the cocktail hour
- Bistro string lighting along the marquee ridge
- Ballasted weight kits (Inlet default; engineered stakes for inland venues on grass)

Setup runs 5–6 hours; teardown 3–4. Both quoted up front. For other guest counts, the [50/100/150/200 rental checklist](/blog/party-rental-checklist-50-100-150-200-guests) maps the same configuration to your headcount.
