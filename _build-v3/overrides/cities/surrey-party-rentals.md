## What "local" actually changes about the booking

![A row of clean white Chiavari chairs lined up on grass at an outdoor wedding ceremony](/images/lifestyle/chiavari-setup.jpg){.float-right}
*Chiavari chairs staged for an outdoor Surrey ceremony.*

Three operational differences make Surrey our most flexible delivery zone.

### Same-week bookings

Tight timelines are more often workable in Surrey than anywhere else. Chair, table, and small-tent orders inside a 3–5 day window are usually feasible even in peak season — call before booking online and we'll tell you straight whether the trucks have room.

### Mid-event swaps

If a chair leg fails, a table breaks, or you suddenly need 20 more cocktail highboys at 5 PM Saturday, replacements can typically be on-site within 60 minutes. We don't make this promise for cities further out.

### Late-night teardowns

Saturday evening teardowns past 11 PM are easier in Surrey because the crew doesn't have a long return drive. Useful for late-running receptions where moving the teardown window isn't an option.

### Warehouse pickup

Pickup orders skip the delivery line item entirely. Useful for budget-conscious bookings — popup tents, chairs, banquet tables, and dance-floor sections are all pickup-eligible. Marquees require professional install and aren't on the pickup list.

## Beyond weddings: schools, sports banquets, and community events

Surrey has the largest school district in BC and the most active community-centre calendar in the Lower Mainland — which means a meaningful share of our Surrey deliveries aren't weddings at all. Grad ceremonies and after-grad events at school gymnasiums and field tents, sports banquets at Newton and Cloverdale halls, school fundraisers, and PAC family-day events are weekly bookings during the spring and fall.

Schools and community organizations get the same itemised quote and the same crew. The difference is volume: a school grad rental might be 200 Fanback chairs and 25 banquet tables, no marquee — a setup that ships on one truck and stages in under three hours.

## A 100-guest Surrey wedding rental order

For a 100-guest Surrey wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Cloverdale acreage with ceremony and reception under a single marquee — a typical order looks like:

- 1 × **20×60 marquee tent** with sidewalls (covered ceremony + reception)
- 100 × **white Chiavari chairs** (ceremony rows, then re-staged at the reception tables)
- 12 × **5ft round tables** for the reception
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor**
- 2 × **cocktail highboys** for the cocktail hour
- Bistro string lighting along the marquee ridge
- Engineered ground stakes (default for grass)

Setup runs 5–6 hours; teardown 3–4. Both quoted up front. For ceremony venues at Bear Creek Park or other Surrey parks, our [Surrey Parks event rental guide](/blog/surrey-parks-event-rental-guide) covers the venue-by-venue permit and footprint specifics.
