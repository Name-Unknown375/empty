## A wedding we set at Swaneset Bay

![White Chiavari chairs in ceremony rows and a white dance floor in the Swaneset Bay ballroom, golf course visible through the windows](/images/jobs/maple-ridge-swaneset-chiavari-ceremony.jpg)
*Chiavari rows and a portable dance floor at Swaneset Bay. The club is in Pitt Meadows; this was a Ridge Meadows wedding we set.*

Swaneset sits in [Pitt Meadows](/pitt-meadows-party-rentals), not city-of-Maple-Ridge limits. Maple Ridge couples still book it — we treat Ridge Meadows as one run. The ballroom is floor-to-ceiling glass over the golf course. We run white Chiavari in ceremony rows, then reset for dinner — the dance floor goes down on the carpet before guests arrive so the crew isn't moving panels during cocktail hour. Same chairs, both layouts: the [ceremony-to-reception flip playbook](/blog/tent-layout-ceremony-to-reception-flip) is the indoor version of what we do under a marquee.

The rest of Maple Ridge is acreage and park work: Alouette Lake wind by mid-afternoon, Golden Ears setbacks, Hammond and Silver Valley backyards. Delivery from the Surrey warehouse is 30–40 minutes. Tamara Durkin's 20×30 marquee (the review on this page) is the other typical Maple Ridge job — a private wedding, crew-installed, photo of the finished tent before we left the site. Footprint math for that size is in the [Lower Mainland tent size guide](/blog/tent-size-guide-lower-mainland-wedding).
