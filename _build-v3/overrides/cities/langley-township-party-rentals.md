## Vineyards, acreages, parks, and the Township's four operational rhythms

### Vineyard and berry-farm weddings

Krause Berry Farms, Township 7 Vineyards, Domaine de Chaberton, and the smaller agritourism properties between Brookswood and Aldergrove. Each venue's harvesting and tasting-room schedule shapes our delivery window — we coordinate the load-in directly with the venue's events team.

### Brookswood and Murrayville acreages

Single-family rural-residential properties with the standard backyard concerns plus the Township's open-field wind. Most acreages accept a 20×60 marquee comfortably; some accommodate two linked 20×40 tents for 200+ guest setups.

### Campbell Valley Regional Park

Open-air park ceremony zones with farmland-style afternoon wind. The Township and Metro Vancouver Regional Parks run separate permit processes; we know which zones cover which footprints. Equestrian-adjacent ceremonies often skip the tent entirely.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Brookswood as in Willoughby. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes is delivery — distance across the Township, anchor strategy, multi-venue — never the per-item rate.

## Township-wide events: weddings + community + corporate

The Township's geography supports a meaningful share of community-organization events outside the wedding category. Brookswood and Murrayville schools run grad ceremonies and field tents, agricultural fairs run at the Aldergrove area, and the Township's community centres host civic functions throughout the year. These setups typically need 3–4 weeks of lead time (faster than wedding procurement) and often run on Fanback chairs, banquet tables, and bistro lighting — no marquee, single-truck delivery.
