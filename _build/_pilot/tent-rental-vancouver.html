Vancouver venues we tent most weekends

Most Vancouver tent installations we deliver fall into four buckets, each with its own first question.

Park ceremonies — Stanley Park (Ceperley Meadow, the Shakespeare Garden, Third Beach), Queen Elizabeth Park (Quarry Gardens, the Bloedel lawn, Seasons in the Park patio), and VanDusen Botanical Garden’s outdoor sites. The first question is always permits: the couple pulls the Park Board permit, but we know which ceremony zones have approved tent footprints and which require a smaller setup. Our Stanley Park rental guide has the Park Board path written out in detail.

Rooftop and downtown weddings — Yaletown patios, Vancouver Convention Centre side rooms, and Coal Harbour event spaces. The first question is the freight elevator: width, weight, and interior height. We confirm those before quoting. Rooftops are always ballasted (never staked); buildings need to confirm their per-square-foot load rating.

Backyard receptions — Kitsilano, West End, Dunbar, and Mount Pleasant private homes. The first question is the property survey: setbacks from property lines, slope tolerance, sprinkler-head clearance, and where our truck can park without blocking the alley. Most Vancouver backyards take a 20×40 marquee comfortably; a few are 20×20-only.

Industrial-loft and East Vancouver events — Mount Pleasant, East Van warehouse spaces, and the Olympic Village. The first question is power: most lofts are wired for daytime use, not for dance-floor lighting and DJs at midnight. We bundle bistro string lights and (where needed) battery power stations on the same quote.

What our crew actually does on a Vancouver tent install

A Vancouver marquee install isn’t a tent and four stakes — it’s a sequence:

  1. Site walk before the quote (or before crew arrival for repeat venues), confirming surface type, anchor strategy, and access route.
  2. Stake or ballast matched to the surface. Grass takes engineered ground stakes. Pavement, stamped concrete, and pavers take ballasted weight kits at every leg — typically 250 kg per corner for a 20×40 marquee in Vancouver’s wind exposure. We never tent on artificial turf.
  3. Frame raise and canvas fit with the crew sized to the tent. A 20×40 needs three crew members; a 30×60 needs four. We don’t compress this — every Vancouver tent gets the right crew.
  4. Sidewalls if booked, plus heater placement, bistro string lighting, and floor demarcation if the dance floor is going inside the same tent.
  5. Pre-event walk with the couple or planner so any layout questions are answered before guest arrival.
  6. Teardown at the agreed time, usually late evening for Saturday weddings.

Tent sidewall rentals are an add-on that customers underestimate. They’re the difference between “rain plan” and “actual rain plan” between November and March, and they cut cross-breeze on hot August afternoons too.

A 100-guest Vancouver tent rental order

A typical 100-guest Vancouver wedding marquee order — say a Saturday in August, a Quarry Gardens ceremony, and a Yaletown reception — looks like this on the tent side:

  • 1 × 20×60 marquee tent (600 sq ft, seats 100 at 5ft round tables with a small dance area)
  • 4 × tent sidewalls (2 long sides, 2 short — full enclosure for evening / weather)
  • 8 × bistro string light strands across the marquee ridge
  • 1 × tent heater (October–April bookings; optional in summer)
  • Engineered ballast kit (this is a Yaletown rooftop or pavement install)

Setup typically takes 4–5 hours with our four-crew team; teardown is 2–3 hours. Both are quoted up front. Couples who want a covered ceremony AND reception under one tent should look at our ceremony-to-reception flip playbook — we can flip a 20×40 in 45 minutes with the right floor plan.

FAQ

Tent Rentals Questions — Vancouver

Can you install a marquee tent at Stanley Park?
Yes — we deliver and install tents at Stanley Park ceremony zones regularly. The Vancouver Park Board permit is the couple's responsibility (we're not allowed to pull it for you), but our crew coordinates the truck-only access window with the Park Board's events team directly. Two of our marquee footprints fit within the most-used ceremony approval zones — see our linked Stanley Park guide for the specifics.
What tent size do I need for a Vancouver wedding?
For a seated ceremony or reception, plan ~12 sq ft per guest with a dance floor, ~10 sq ft without. A 20×40 (800 sq ft) seats 60-65 at round tables with room for a head table; a 20×60 seats around 100. Our linked tent size guide has the math for 50, 100, 150, and 200-guest setups so you can match the venue footprint before you book.
Can you tent on a Yaletown rooftop?
Sometimes — it depends on the building. We need three things confirmed before we quote: the freight elevator's interior dimensions and weight rating, the rooftop's load-per-square-foot rating (usually in the building's spec sheet), and whether the building manager will be on-site Saturday morning. Most Yaletown rooftops we've worked accept a 20×20 or smaller marquee with ballasted weights — never staked installation.
Do you install tents in November through March?
Yes, with sidewalls and a tent heater on the quote. Our marquees are engineered for Lower Mainland winter weather — high-peak aluminium frames, solid sidewalls, and tie-down hardware rated for sustained wind and rain. The sidewalls are an add-on but we strongly recommend them between November and March. The 125% Cancellation Protection covers our crew's no-show; if you need to roll a date for a forecast we waive the change fee when called before crew dispatch.
How much lead time do you need for a Vancouver marquee tent?
For peak-season weddings (May–September), 6-8 weeks is the comfortable window — earlier if your date is a Saturday in July or August. Same-week is feasible for chair, table, and 10×10 popup orders if our trucks aren't already booked. Marquee tents 20×40 and larger need installer scheduling that's hard to compress under 7 days.
