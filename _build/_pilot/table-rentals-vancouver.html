Which Vancouver table you need at which point in the event

Three tables, three jobs. Vancouver event floor plans usually need all three.

5ft round tables ($13.50/day, seats 8) — the classic wedding-reception configuration. Eight guests, room for centerpieces, fits two-deep into most marquee footprints (a 20×40 holds 6 rounds; a 20×60 holds 12). The reception workhorse. We size every Vancouver wedding around these unless the couple has explicitly chosen long banquet-style seating.

6ft rectangular banquet tables ($10.95/day, seats 6-8) — the single most versatile SKU in event rentals. Family-style dining rows, buffet stations, gift tables, DJ booths, bar setups, registration desks, head tables. Under 75 lb so they re-stage quickly mid-event. If you can only book one type of table, book this one.

Cocktail highboy tables ($15.50/day, standing) — 42-inch standing-height rounds for cocktail hour, networking events, and pre-dinner mingling. Plan ~1 highboy per 10–12 standing guests. Spandex covers available through our linen partners.

For sizing — what counts to actually book at 50, 100, 150, or 200 guests — our rental checklist walks the math, including the back-of-house tables most floor plans forget.

Floor-plan staging and Vancouver venue access realities

Where chairs and tents care most about surface and weather, tables care most about access and floor plan. The Vancouver-specific patterns we plan around:

  1. Park ceremonies have minimal tables — usually just a registration table, a guest book table, and maybe a small refreshment table. Chairs are the count that matters; tables are accent pieces.
  2. Yaletown rooftops have weight and elevator constraints — we confirm freight-elevator interior dimensions and weight ratings before quoting any 8-foot banquet table. The 6ft banquet at <75 lb fits nearly every freight elevator we’ve seen; the 8ft is occasionally tight.
  3. Convention Centre and downtown corporate spaces use loading-dock booking systems — table delivery is timed with the dock window, which closes earlier than the event start. We schedule around it; you don’t manage it.
  4. Backyard weddings — Kitsilano, West End, Dunbar properties usually have grass and a level patio. The crew chooses surface placement based on table type: highboys go on the patio, banquets and rounds go on the grass (we level them).
  5. Mid-event flips — for Queen Elizabeth Park Quarry Gardens and similar ceremony-to-reception venues, the table layout transforms during cocktail hour. We bring extra crew to handle the re-stage in under an hour.

We don’t add a per-table setup charge when delivery and setup are on the quote. Every table is positioned to your floor plan during the same delivery window.

A 100-guest Vancouver table rental order

For a 100-guest Vancouver wedding with a marquee reception and a separate cocktail-hour space, a typical table order looks like:

  • 12 × 5ft round tables (8 guests per round = 96, with 2 extra rounds for kids’ table and overflow)
  • 6 × 6ft banquet tables (head table linking 2, gift, cake, DJ, registration)
  • 2 × 6ft banquet tables for the buffet line (or head-table extension if buffet runs through the venue’s catering line)
  • 3 × cocktail highboys for the pre-dinner cocktail hour

Total quote line items on the table side: 23 tables, all positioned to your floor plan during a single delivery window. Combined with chairs, marquee, and (often) a dance floor, this is one truck, one quote, one crew.

FAQ

Table Rentals Questions — Vancouver

How many tables do I need for a 100-guest Vancouver wedding?
For seated dinner at 5ft rounds, plan ~12 rounds (8 guests per round = 96; round up for kids and split parties). Add 5–8 banquet tables for head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, and buffet. Add 2–3 cocktail highboys for the pre-dinner cocktail hour. Our linked rental checklist has the same math worked out for 50, 150, and 200 guests.
Can you set up tables on a Yaletown rooftop or grass surface?
Yes — both. Our 6ft banquet tables and 5ft rounds weigh under 75 lb each, so freight elevators and stair-only buildings aren't a problem if we're given the access details up front. On grass (Stanley Park, Queen Elizabeth Park, VanDusen lawns) we level each table to your floor plan during setup; uneven ground is what an extra 30 minutes of staging time is for.
Do you supply linens, runners, or chargers?
We don't stock linens directly, but we coordinate through our preferred Vancouver linen partners and bundle them onto the same delivery window if you want. Most Vancouver couples arrange linens independently with a specialist; we'll happily share recommendations during the quote. Chargers, runners, and place settings sit with the catering provider.
Can you re-stage tables mid-event for a Vancouver flip?
Yes — for venues like Queen Elizabeth Park's Quarry Gardens or any single-tent ceremony-to-reception flip, our crew handles the table re-stage during the cocktail hour. The 6ft banquet tables fold flat and re-position quickly; round tables re-stack to the truck while we re-stage to dinner configuration. We've flipped Vancouver tents in 45 minutes.
Are tables included in your tent rental, or quoted separately?
Quoted separately, but on the same order. Our quoting process bundles tents, chairs, tables, and any add-ons (lighting, dance floor, sidewalls) onto a single line-itemed quote with one delivery window and one teardown. Most Vancouver couples book tent + chair + table together rather than splitting across vendors.
