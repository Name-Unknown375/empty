The four kinds of Burnaby events we deliver

Most weekends in season we’re delivering to one of four event categories in Burnaby, and each has its own first-question pattern.

Heritage venue weddings — Hart House Restaurant on Deer Lake, Burnaby Village Museum, and the Shadbolt Centre’s outdoor terraces. These are the Burnaby weddings most couples picture: lakefront ceremony, historic backdrop, formal reception. The first question is the venue’s own load-in window (some Hart House Saturdays only allow vendor access between 9 AM and noon). We schedule around it.

Corporate events at Brentwood and Metrotown — office-plaza receptions, tenant appreciation parties, product launches, and the occasional rooftop summer event. The first question is power and access: most office plazas have controlled freight elevators and after-5pm timing constraints. We bundle bistro string lights, marquee tents, and (where needed) battery power stations onto the same quote.

Backyard receptions in Deer Lake, Edmonds, and North Burnaby — single-family detached homes hosting weddings, milestone birthdays, and graduations. The first question is the property survey: setbacks from property lines, slope tolerance, sprinkler-head clearance, and where the truck can stop. Most Burnaby backyards accept a 20×40 marquee comfortably.

Metrotown rooftop birthdays and engagement parties — high-rise condo terraces with limited freight-elevator dimensions and weight-bearing constraints. The first question is the freight elevator: width, weight rating, and whether the building manager will be on-site. Tables and Chiavari chairs ship freely; marquees are case-by-case based on the rooftop’s structural rating.

Loading docks, condo elevators, and Burnaby venue access

Burnaby venue access is more varied than most Lower Mainland cities:

  1. Hart House Restaurant — Saturday afternoon delivery window only; we book the slot with the events team during quoting.
  2. Burnaby Village Museum — historic site with seasonal access restrictions; events typically run on the museum’s calendar, not yours.
  3. Shadbolt Centre for the Arts — multiple performance and event spaces; outdoor terraces accept marquee setups; indoor theatre lobby is a popular cocktail-style corporate space.
  4. Deer Lake Park — multiple ceremony zones (lakefront, meadow, south lot) each with different vehicle access and tent footprint allowances. We know which zones fit our 20×20, 20×40, and 20×60 marquees.
  5. Brentwood and Metrotown office plazas — controlled freight elevators, after-5pm timing constraints, and security-desk vendor sign-in. We confirm all three before quoting.
  6. Detached-home neighbourhoods (Deer Lake, North Burnaby, Edmonds) — standard backyard setups; the variable is alley access for the truck.

We don’t add a Burnaby-zone fee to the quote. Delivery shows up as a single line item that covers the round-trip and the crew time.

A 100-guest Burnaby wedding rental order

For a 100-guest Burnaby wedding — say a Saturday in August at Hart House on Deer Lake, with a ceremony on the lakefront lawn and reception inside the heritage building — a typical rental order looks like:

  • 1 × 20×40 marquee tent (covered ceremony in case of rain; reception is indoors at Hart House)
  • 100 × white Chiavari chairs (split: ceremony rows on the lawn, then re-staged at the reception)
  • 12 × 5ft round tables for the reception (Hart House’s indoor floor plan)
  • 6 × 6ft banquet tables (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
  • 1 × 12×16 portable dance floor
  • 2 × cocktail highboys for the cocktail hour on the terrace
  • Bistro string lighting on the lakefront marquee

Setup takes our crew 4–5 hours; teardown 2–3. Both quoted up front. For sizing variations on smaller (50 guest) or larger (150-200 guest) Burnaby setups, our 50/100/150/200 rental checklist maps the same configuration to your headcount.

For ceremony-to-reception flips at venues that share a single tent (less common in Burnaby than at Vancouver park venues, but it happens for Deer Lake Park bookings), see our ceremony-to-reception flip playbook — we can flip a 20×40 in 45 minutes with the right floor plan.

FAQ

Party Rental Questions — Burnaby

Can you set up at Deer Lake Park, Hart House, or the Shadbolt Centre?
Yes — all three are regular Burnaby venues for our crew. Deer Lake Park has multiple ceremony sites with their own setback rules (the lakefront lawn vs. the meadow vs. the south lot). Hart House has a fixed Saturday delivery window we coordinate with their events team. The Shadbolt Centre's outdoor terraces accept marquee tents for arts-organization events; the indoor theatre lobby works well for chair-and-cocktail-table corporate functions.
Do you handle Brentwood and Metrotown rooftop condo events?
Yes — both are common Burnaby bookings. We confirm the building's freight elevator dimensions and weight rating before quoting, and we always ballast (never stake) on rooftops. Most Brentwood Town Centre and Metrotown buildings we've worked with accept our 6ft banquet and 5ft round tables; some accept a 20×20 marquee with ballasted weights. The building manager's Saturday availability is the variable that decides delivery-day timing.
What's the Burnaby corporate event lead time?
Corporate bookings (Brentwood office plazas, Metrotown commercial spaces, Shadbolt Centre tenant events) typically need 3–4 weeks of lead time — less than weddings because corporate clients usually have approved vendor lists and faster procurement cycles. Same-week bookings are sometimes feasible for chair, table, and 10×10 popup orders if our trucks aren't already booked.
How much advance booking for a Burnaby summer wedding?
For peak-season Burnaby weddings (May–September), 6–8 weeks is the comfortable window. Saturdays in July and August book out earliest. Heritage venues like Hart House and Burnaby Village Museum often have their own venue-side booking lead times that exceed ours — confirm both calendars before locking equipment quantities.
Can you bundle Christmas light installation with my Burnaby event rental?
Yes — Burnaby is one of our 14 Christmas light installation cities. The two services are quoted separately because Christmas runs October through January and event rentals run year-round, but if you book both with us we coordinate scheduling so neither steps on the other. Free off-season storage is included on every Christmas light install.
What does the delivery and setup fee cover?
When you add delivery and setup to your order, our crew handles round-trip transport, professional setup of tents and dance floors, takedown, and pickup. Chairs and tables are stacked and placed where you want them. Delivery and setup are quoted up front with no hidden charges or fuel surcharges — the price you see at checkout is the price you pay.
Can you set up a tent on grass, gravel, or a paved driveway?
Yes to all three. Our marquee tents use stake-down installation on grass and ballasted (weighted) installation on pavement, gravel, and stamped concrete. Tell us the surface when you book and we'll send the right hardware. We do not tent on artificial turf.
What happens if the weather is bad or I need to cancel?
Our 125% Cancellation Protection means if we ever cancel your booking, you receive 125% of your deposit back. If you need to reschedule because of weather, we'll roll your deposit forward to a new date at no charge — as long as we're notified before crew dispatch. Cancellations initiated by the customer are subject to our standard deposit terms.
