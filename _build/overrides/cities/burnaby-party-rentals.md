## The Burnaby logistics that change the quote

Burnaby venue access varies more than most cities — and that variation is what shapes the quote.

### Heritage and lakefront venues

![Cocktail-hour highboy tables staged outdoors with guests in conversation behind](/images/lifestyle/cocktail-hour-1.jpg){.float-right}
*Cocktail highboys staged for a reception.*

Hart House, Burnaby Village Museum, the Shadbolt Centre, and Deer Lake Park all run their own programming calendars. Vendor access is venue-coordinated; we book the load-in window with the events team during quoting so the date isn't a surprise. Deer Lake Park alone has multiple ceremony zones with different vehicle-access lanes — we know which ones fit a 20×60 marquee and which top out at a 20×40.

### Brentwood and Metrotown high-rise events

Rooftop and tenant events at Brentwood Town Centre and Metrotown buildings need three things confirmed up front: freight-elevator dimensions, freight-elevator weight rating, and the building manager's Saturday availability. Marquees on rooftops are case-by-case based on the rooftop's structural rating; tables and chairs ship freely.

### Detached-home neighbourhoods

Backyards in North Burnaby, the Deer Lake area, and Edmonds run the standard pattern: setbacks, slope, sprinkler heads, and where the truck can stop without blocking the alley. Most accept a 20×40 comfortably.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Brentwood as in any neighbourhood we serve. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, elevator access, slope — never the per-item rate.

## A 100-guest Burnaby wedding rental order

For a 100-guest Burnaby wedding — say a Saturday in August at Deer Lake Park's lakefront lawn, with ceremony and reception both running under a single tent — a typical rental order looks like:

- 1 × **20×60 marquee tent** with sidewalls (covered ceremony + reception)
- 100 × **white Chiavari chairs** (ceremony rows, then re-positioned at reception tables — same chairs, our crew handles the flip)
- 12 × **5ft round tables** (8 guests per table = 96, plus 2 extras)
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor**
- 2 × **cocktail highboys** for the cocktail hour
- Bistro string lighting along the marquee ridge

Setup runs 5–6 hours; teardown 3–4. Both quoted up front. This is a single-tent ceremony-to-reception flip — our [ceremony-to-reception flip playbook](/blog/tent-layout-ceremony-to-reception-flip) covers the layout that lets the same chairs run both phases.
