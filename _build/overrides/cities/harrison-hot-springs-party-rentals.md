## Destination delivery, multi-day staging, and Harrison realities

### Multi-day staging is the destination norm

Harrison weddings often run Friday–Sunday — rehearsal dinner, ceremony/reception, farewell brunch — at multiple venues across the village. We can stage the truck overnight at the lead venue rather than running the round-trip back twice. Overnight staging factors into the delivery line up front, not as a surprise add-on.

### Harrison Resort vs. lakefront private properties

Resort weddings run on the Harrison Hot Springs Resort's calendar and vendor protocol — we book through their events team. Lakefront private properties (rented homes, Sasquatch Park-adjacent properties) are more flexible on timing but need a pre-event site walk for any property we haven't delivered to.

### Lake exposure and beach permits

Direct-sand ceremonies at Harrison Beach require village permits. Resin Garden chairs replace Chiavari for sand contact; ballast replaces stakes on saturated lakefront soil; sidewalls go up year-round for any open-water exposure.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Harrison Lake property as in our Surrey warehouse. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, overnight staging, multi-venue — never the per-item rate.

## A 100-guest Harrison Hot Springs destination wedding rental order

For a 100-guest Harrison destination wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Harrison Lake-adjacent property with ceremony on the beach and reception under marquee on the lawn — a typical order looks like:

- 1 × **20×60 marquee tent** with sidewalls (covered reception with full enclosure)
- 100 × **white Chiavari chairs** for the reception
- 100 × **Resin Garden chairs** for the beach ceremony (Chiavari doesn't suit direct sand)
- 12 × **5ft round tables** for the reception
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor**
- 2 × **cocktail highboys** for the cocktail hour
- Bistro string lighting along the marquee ridge plus Edison lights at the cocktail hour
- Ballasted weight kits (lakefront default)

Setup runs 6–7 hours with the larger crew destination weddings need; teardown 4–5. Both quoted up front. For Harrison Hot Springs Resort weddings (where the venue's interior covers the reception footprint), the marquee comes off the order entirely.
