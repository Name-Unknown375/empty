## Steep driveways, hairpin roads, and North Shore access

![A clean white marquee tent set up at a backyard wedding with the side walls open and chairs visible inside](/images/lifestyle/tent-setup.jpg){.float-right}
*A 20×40 marquee staged on a mid-grade North Van lawn.*

North Van delivery isn't urban delivery. Four operational realities shape the quote.

### Bridge + traffic

We plan delivery windows around the Ironworkers Memorial crossing and Lonsdale traffic patterns. Friday afternoon and Sunday morning teardowns get the widest windows.

### Driveway grade and width

Our delivery trucks handle most North Van driveways, but unusually steep or narrow lots need staging at the road. We confirm access during quoting — never on event day.

### Overhead branches and clearance

North Shore yards have more tree cover than most Lower Mainland properties. We measure marquee peak clearance before quoting; the [backyard tent sizing playbook](/blog/backyard-wedding-tent-sizing-bc) covers the measurements we ask for.

### Mountain weather

Elevation-driven afternoon wind is the constant. Sidewalls go up year-round on any North Van mountain-adjacent venue.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Lynn Valley acreage as in any North Van neighbourhood. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, grade, access — never the per-item rate.

## Lonsdale waterfront and Quay-area events

The other half of our North Van calendar is waterfront — restaurant patio events, Lonsdale Quay-area weddings, and corporate gatherings along the Burrard Inlet. The operational pattern is different from mountain installs: smaller footprints (a 20×20 or 20×30 typically clears the patio), faster setup, and a stronger emphasis on cocktail-hour highboys and bistro lighting than full-scale dinner seating. Wind exposure is meaningful at the water's edge — sidewalls go up year-round, even on summer evenings.

## A 100-guest North Vancouver wedding rental order

For a 100-guest North Vancouver wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Lynn Valley acreage with ceremony on the lawn under tree cover and reception under a marquee — a typical order looks like:

- 1 × **20×60 marquee tent** with sidewalls (covered reception; ceremony is open-air under the trees)
- 100 × **white Chiavari chairs** (ceremony rows under canopy, then re-staged at reception tables)
- 12 × **5ft round tables** for the reception
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor**
- 2 × **cocktail highboys** for the cocktail hour
