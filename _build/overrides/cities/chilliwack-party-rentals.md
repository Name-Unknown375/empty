## Lake exposure, mountain wind, and Chilliwack operational realities

### Cultus Lake wind plan

Cultus Lake's afternoon thermal is meaningful — sidewalls year-round and ballasted weights for any beach-adjacent ceremony. We size the marquee one step up from nominal headcount because evening enclosure with windbreak matters more than tight footprint here.

### Mount Cheam-facing acreage installs

Sardis, Promontory, Vedder Crossing, and Cheam-facing properties all see mid-afternoon wind that flat valley-floor venues don't. Engineered stakes paired with ballast on the leeward corners is the default for unfamiliar properties.

### Bridal Veil Falls forest sites

Forest-canopy ceremonies skip the marquee entirely — open-air Chiavari rows under the trees. We pre-walk forest sites we haven't delivered to, and we don't promise marquee installs in zones with overhead-branch constraints we haven't measured.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Promontory hilltop as on Cultus Lake. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, lake permits, anchor strategy — never the per-item rate.

## Eastern-valley lead times and overnight staging

Chilliwack is one of our longer delivery routes, and that shapes scheduling. For weddings with multi-day setup needs (rehearsal-dinner Friday + ceremony/reception Saturday + brunch Sunday), we sometimes stage the truck overnight at the venue rather than running the round-trip back to Surrey twice. Overnight staging factors into the delivery line, not as a surprise add-on. For acreage weddings 60+ minutes out, this is often the more cost-effective option.

## A 100-guest Chilliwack wedding rental order

For a 100-guest Chilliwack wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Sardis acreage with ceremony and reception running outdoors and Mount Cheam in the photo backdrop — a typical order looks like:

- 1 × **20×60 marquee tent** with sidewalls (covered ceremony + reception in single footprint)
- 100 × **white Chiavari chairs** (ceremony rows, then re-staged at reception tables)
- 12 × **5ft round tables** for the reception
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor**
- 2 × **cocktail highboys** for the cocktail hour
- Bistro string lighting along the marquee ridge
- Engineered ground stakes paired with ballast (default for Cheam-facing properties)

Setup runs 5–6 hours; teardown 3–4. Both quoted up front. For Cultus Lake beach-adjacent ceremonies, the marquee comes off the order entirely (chair-only), and Resin Garden chairs replace the Chiavari count for direct-sand zones.
