### Short delivery window

New West's compact footprint means same-week chair, table, and small-tent orders are usually feasible — even in peak season. We can absorb tight timelines that further-out cities can't.

### Heritage-venue access constraints

Queen's Park's pavilions and Irving House have heritage-protected sightlines. Most heritage venues skip the marquee entirely and run on smaller-footprint setups: Chiavari chairs, banquet tables, and bistro lighting handle the typical heritage wedding.

### Pier Park wind plan

Fraser River exposure is the constant. Ballasted weight kits, sidewalls year-round, and a slightly larger marquee than nominal headcount.

### Queensborough late-night access

Industrial-loft event spaces in Queensborough often run late, with teardowns past midnight. Queensborough is close enough to our route that late teardowns don't blow up our crew calendar.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Sapperton as in Uptown. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, access, anchor — never the per-item rate.

## Queensborough industrial-loft conversions

Queensborough's converted industrial spaces are growing as event venues for weddings, milestone birthdays, and corporate functions — and they need a different setup pattern than heritage venues. The first question is power and access: most lofts are wired for daytime use, not for dance-floor lighting and DJ infrastructure at midnight. We bundle bistro string lights, cocktail highboys, and (where needed) battery power stations onto the same quote. Loft weddings typically run 60–100 guests with no marquee, full chair-and-table setup, and a 12×12 dance floor.

## A 100-guest New Westminster wedding rental order

For a 100-guest New Westminster wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Queen's Park ceremony followed by a Queensborough loft reception — a typical order looks like:

- 1 × **20×40 marquee tent** (covered ceremony at Queen's Park; reception is indoors at the loft)
- 100 × **white Chiavari chairs** (ceremony rows, then re-staged at reception tables)
- 12 × **5ft round tables** for the reception
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor**
- 2 × **cocktail highboys** for the cocktail hour

Setup runs 4–5 hours; teardown 2–3. Both quoted up front. For ceremony-only or reception-only configurations, the order shrinks accordingly. The [50/100/150/200 rental checklist](/blog/party-rental-checklist-50-100-150-200-guests) maps the same configuration to your headcount.
