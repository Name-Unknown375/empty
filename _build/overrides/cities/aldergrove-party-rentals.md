### Rural-residential acreage installs

Bertrand Creek, County Line Diversion, and North Aldergrove properties have driveway approaches measured in kilometres rather than metres. We factor the truck-stage distance into the crew-time line — and we confirm gate codes, livestock considerations, and overhead utility lines before the crew dispatches.

### Greater Vancouver Zoo events

The Zoo's corporate-event programming has grown into a regular slice of our Aldergrove calendar. Conservation organization galas, corporate retreats, family-day events, and team-building functions all run through the Zoo's events team. Most Zoo setups skip the marquee and run on banquet seating, Chiavari chairs, and bistro lighting.

### Aldergrove Regional Park

Open-air park ceremony zones with the same farmland-style wind that Township acreage installs encounter. Sidewalls help cut the cross-breeze; ballast outperforms staking on irrigated park grass.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Bertrand Creek acreage as at Aldergrove Village. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — driveway distance, gate access, anchor strategy — never the per-item rate.

## Border-area events and Sumas Mountain spillover

Aldergrove's east edge bumps against the Abbotsford border, and a meaningful share of weddings booked as "Aldergrove" actually happen on properties that straddle the line — Sumas Mountain-facing acreages, properties along the County Line. Our crew works both sides on a single quote without a different rate; the delivery line covers the round-trip whether the address is on the Township or City of Abbotsford side.

## A 100-guest Aldergrove rural wedding rental order

For a 100-guest Aldergrove wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Bertrand Creek acreage with ceremony and reception running outdoors — a typical order looks like:

- 1 × **20×60 marquee tent** with sidewalls (covered ceremony + reception in single footprint)
- 100 × **white Chiavari chairs** (ceremony rows, then re-staged at reception tables)
- 12 × **5ft round tables** for the reception
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor**
- 2 × **cocktail highboys** for the cocktail hour
- Bistro string lighting along the marquee ridge
- Engineered ground stakes (default for grass; ballast where irrigation present)

Setup runs 5–6 hours; teardown 3–4. Both quoted up front. For Greater Vancouver Zoo corporate events (typically 80–150 attendees, no marquee), the order shifts to banquet tables, Chiavari chairs, cocktail highboys, and lighting.
