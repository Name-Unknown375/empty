## Three operational patterns specific to Richmond

![Cocktail-hour highboy tables set up at an outdoor reception with guests in conversation in the background](/images/lifestyle/cocktail-hour-1.jpg){.float-right}
*Cocktail highboys staged for a Richmond cocktail hour.*

### Cultural-wedding timing precision

Multi-stage ceremonies often start at 8 AM or earlier — tea ceremonies typically run before brunch. We dispatch the truck to be on-site 90 minutes before your earliest milestone, with the chairs set, aisle-spaced, and angled before family arrival.

### Steveston wind exposure

Garry Point and the Steveston dyke trails sit on open water with afternoon wind that's meaningfully different from Vancouver-side or inland Richmond venues. Sidewalls year-round, heavier anchor strategy, and a slightly larger marquee than headcount strictly requires.

### Multi-venue events

Richmond cultural weddings often span two venues — ceremony at one site, reception at another. One quote, two delivery windows on the same day, same crew across both. We coordinate the timing directly with whoever's running point on your day.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Steveston as in central Richmond. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, multi-venue, wind-anchor — never the per-item rate.

## Olympic Oval and Richmond corporate events

Richmond's other operational rhythm is corporate — galas, product launches, conference functions, and charity events at the Olympic Oval and the larger downtown venues. The pattern is faster than weddings: corporate clients usually have approved-vendor lists and shorter procurement cycles, so 3–4 weeks of lead time is typical instead of the 6–8 we recommend for peak-season Saturdays. Most large-venue corporate setups skip the marquee entirely (the building's interior is the venue) and run on banquet seating, Chiavari chairs, cocktail highboys, and bistro lighting for ambience.

## A 100-guest Richmond wedding rental order

For a 100-guest Richmond wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Steveston-area outdoor venue with ceremony and reception both running on-site — a typical order looks like:

- 1 × **20×60 marquee tent** with sidewalls (Steveston wind + evening enclosure)
- 100 × **white Chiavari chairs** (ceremony rows, then re-staged at reception tables)
- 12 × **5ft round tables** for the reception
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, family, gift, cake, DJ, bar)
- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor**
- 2 × **cocktail highboys** for the cocktail hour
- Bistro string lighting along the marquee ridge
- Ballasted weight kits (Steveston-area; engineered stakes elsewhere on grass)

Setup runs 5–6 hours; teardown 3–4. Both quoted up front.

For multi-stage cultural weddings the chair count typically scales (200–300 guest weddings are common), and the table side picks up additional banquet tables for larger family seating.
