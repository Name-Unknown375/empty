### 200 Street business corridor

The growing business park along 200 Street produces a steady rhythm of tenant events, corporate retreats, product launches, and seasonal client appreciation gatherings. Lead times here are typically shorter than weddings (3–4 weeks rather than 6–8); corporate clients have approved-vendor lists and faster procurement cycles.

### Park & Ride and transit-area events

Carvolth Exchange's transit hub occasionally hosts community events, civic functions, and TransLink-coordinated activations. These setups need clear vendor sign-in, controlled access during peak commute hours, and ballasted setups on the paved exchange surfaces.

### Northwest Willoughby Heights residential

The northwest corner of Willoughby Heights extends into Carvolth, and the residential character here carries the same new-build pattern as the rest of Willoughby — smaller backyard footprints, townhouse common areas, and strata-managed event approval processes.

### Per-item pricing

The same in the 200 Street business park as on a residential lot. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — building access, parking restrictions, after-hours timing — never the per-item rate.

## Same-week and same-day corporate additions

Corporate clients regularly need last-minute additions to existing setups — extra cocktail highboys when the head count grows, additional banquet tables when a vendor pivot happens, replacement chairs when something breaks. Carvolth's proximity to our warehouse makes these turnaround windows realistic in ways they aren't for further-out cities. We can typically get replacement or additional inventory on-site within 60–90 minutes during business hours.

## A 100-guest Carvolth corporate event rental order

For a 100-attendee Carvolth corporate event — say a Tuesday in October at a 200 Street business park venue with afternoon presentation seating transitioning into an evening cocktail reception — a typical order looks like:

- 100 × **white Chiavari chairs** (presentation seating, then re-positioned for cocktail-style standing during the reception)
- 12 × **5ft round tables** for cocktail-hour seating clusters
- 4 × **6ft banquet tables** (registration, beverage stations, food displays)
- 8 × **cocktail highboys** for the standing reception zones
- Bistro string lighting (where the venue allows)

Setup runs 2–3 hours; teardown 1–2. Both quoted up front. For Meridian Farm Market events (typically smaller, 50–80 attendees), the order shrinks accordingly.
