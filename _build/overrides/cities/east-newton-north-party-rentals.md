## A 200-guest East Newton multi-stage wedding rental order

For a 200-guest East Newton multi-stage wedding — say a Saturday in August with morning ceremony, afternoon reception, and evening dinner all running on the same large lot or community centre — a typical order looks like:

- 1 × **30×60 marquee tent** with sidewalls (covered reception + dinner)
- 200 × **Fanback folding chairs** (ceremony rows; morning and afternoon timing)
- 200 × **white Chiavari chairs** (re-positioned at reception and dinner tables)
- 25 × **5ft round tables** for dinner seating
- 12 × **6ft banquet tables** (head, family, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar, food displays)
- 1 × **16×20 portable dance floor**
- 4 × **cocktail highboys** for the cocktail-hour transition
- Bistro string lighting along the marquee ridge plus Edison lights at the entrance
- Engineered ground stakes (default for grass)

Setup runs 6–7 hours with the larger crew multi-stage events need; teardown 4–5. Both quoted up front.
