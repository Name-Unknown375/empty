## Ocean, mountain, and the bench in between

West Van delivery isn't North Van delivery. Four operational realities shape the quote.

### Bridge crossing and Taylor Way

Lions Gate is the default route to Ambleside, Dundarave, and the lower bench; Ironworkers becomes the better call for British Properties peak-hour deliveries. Taylor Way's climb to the upper bench is meaningful on a loaded truck — we factor it into the delivery window.

### Terraced lawns and irregular grade

The British Properties hillside doesn't have flat lawns. Every estate setup gets graded before quoting, and the dance floor in particular gets subfloor panels to level the surface. Our [backyard tent sizing playbook](/blog/backyard-wedding-tent-sizing-bc) covers what to measure before you call us.

### Tidal windows for beach setups

Whytecliff and Ambleside ceremonies are timed to tide, not just to schedule. The install crew arrives at the low-tide window, finishes before guest arrival, and tears down once the reception has moved away from the waterline.

### Ferry traffic at Horseshoe Bay

The Bowen Island and Sunshine Coast sailings shape the road pattern around the terminal. We schedule deliveries around the gaps, not into them.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Caulfeild estate as in a Dundarave waterfront condo's rooftop. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, grade, tide, ferry queue — never the per-item rate.

## Whytecliff, Lighthouse Park, and the western edge

The western end of West Vancouver has the most photographed ocean ceremonies on the North Shore — and the most operationally specific install conditions we handle. Whytecliff requires permit-desk coordination with the District of West Vancouver, cart access from the upper lot down to the rocky lower beach, and timing that accounts for the tide cycle and the cellular-dead-spot at the cove. Lighthouse Park is similar in spirit — old-growth canopy, narrow trail access, and small ceremony footprints (under 50 guests is typical). Both venues reward early planning more than any other West Van site we serve.
