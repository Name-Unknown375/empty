## Vineyards, berry farms, and Langley acreage logistics

![Rows of white chairs and a wedding ceremony setup on lush grass with greenery framing the scene](/images/lifestyle/ceremony-chairs-3.jpg){.float-right}
*A vineyard-row ceremony staged on Langley grass.*

Langley acreage and vineyard installs differ from urban setups in three operational ways.

### Engineered stakes are the default

Stakes hold meaningfully better than ballasted weights on irrigated agricultural land. We confirm sprinkler infrastructure and underground utilities before staking — never on event day.

### Wind comes from a different direction in the open valley

Langley's mid-afternoon thermal pattern produces gust loads that flat urban ground doesn't. Sidewalls help cut the cross-breeze, and we recommend them year-round on any open-field installation.

### Vineyard staging is venue-coordinated

The venue's irrigation, harvesting, and tasting-room patterns determine when our truck can stage equipment without interfering with their operations. We coordinate the window directly during quoting.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Township 7 lawn as in any Langley neighbourhood. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, surface, multi-venue — never the per-item rate.
