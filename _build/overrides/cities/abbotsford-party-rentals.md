## Acreage installs: surface, slope, and Fraser Valley wind

![A long banquet table set up under a marquee at sunset with chairs and lighting](/images/lifestyle/tent-chairs-tables.jpg){.float-right}
*A reception under marquee on Fraser Valley grass.*

Abbotsford acreage installs differ from urban setups in three operational ways.

### Engineered stakes are the default

Stakes hold meaningfully better than ballasted weights on topsoil. On acreages with known underground irrigation, septic, or buried utilities we switch to ballast after confirming the site map. We don't guess.

### Wind ratings matter more than season

Sumas Mountain's elevation and the open Fraser Valley both produce afternoon gusts that flat urban ground doesn't. Sidewalls year-round are our recommendation for higher-elevation properties; the wind cuts the same direction in July as in October.

### Pre-event site walk is part of the quote

For any acreage we haven't delivered to, the [backyard tent sizing playbook](/blog/backyard-wedding-tent-sizing-bc) describes the measurements we ask for in advance. Slope tolerance, sprinkler clearance, alley access, and overhead utility lines are all part of the pre-quote checklist.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Sumas Mountain acreage as in any Abbotsford neighbourhood. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes is delivery — distance, slope, surface — never the per-item rate.

## Tradex and Fraser Valley corporate events

Tradex Exhibition Centre is a different operational pattern than acreages. The venue runs its own loading-dock booking system and vendor sign-in protocols, both of which supersede ours — we book delivery slots through the events team during quoting, and our crew checks in at the dock instead of staging at the gate.

Most Tradex corporate setups skip the marquee entirely (the building's interior is the venue) and run on banquet tables, cocktail highboys, bistro lighting for ambience, and a portable dance floor for evening events. Lead time is typically shorter than weddings — corporate clients usually have approved-vendor lists and faster procurement cycles, so 3–4 weeks is comfortable instead of the 6–8 we recommend for peak-season Saturdays.
