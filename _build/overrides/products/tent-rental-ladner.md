## Westham Island farm weddings — the marquee anchor

![A clean white marquee tent set against a vibrant blue sky with fluffy clouds](/images/lifestyle/tent-golden-hour-2.jpg)
*A 20×40 marquee staged on a Westham Island farm — agritourism backdrop.*

Westham Island farm weddings define the Ladner marquee calendar. Most are 80–120 guests under a 20×40 to 20×60 marquee on the farm lawn or farm-edge clearing. The bridge-crossing delivery and the farm operations during the wedding-season weekends shape install timing more than any other Ladner variable.

Install pattern:

- Cross the single-lane bridge in the morning install window (we coordinate around peak agritourism visitor hours).
- Stage the marquee close to the farm-edge clearing.
- Engineered stakes for grass; supplemental ballast where the farm-edge surface transitions to gravel or compacted equipment paths.
- Sidewalls go up at install — open Boundary Bay wind reaches Westham Island in the afternoon.

Most Westham Island marquees are 20×40; the larger farm receptions with covered indoor-and-outdoor flow take a 20×60 with the open side facing the farmhouse.

## Ladner Village heritage and 48 Avenue

Ladner Village's heritage residential streets and 48 Avenue restaurant row have tight truck access — shorter delivery windows, pre-stage close to the property line. Most Ladner Village heritage backyards accept a 20×30 to 20×40 marquee comfortably. Side-passage access caps install crew window length.

The marquee itself doesn't change between an open Westham Island farm and a tight Ladner Village backyard. What changes is the install procedure and how the crew positions equipment around side passages.

## Port Guichon and Holly — waterfront residential

Port Guichon's waterfront residential streets accept standard delivery patterns. Boundary Bay-side afternoon onshore wind shapes the anchor strategy — stakes plus supplemental ballast at the bay-facing corners, sidewalls year-round.

Most Port Guichon residence weddings are 80–120 guests with a 20×40 or 20×60 marquee on the back lawn. The view across the bay is the photographic anchor at most of these installs.

## George C. Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary

Reifel Sanctuary's ceremony zones run on permits with strict setback rules. Most accept 20×20 cocktail-style marquees only; full reception tents rarely fit. The standard pattern: ceremony chairs only at Reifel (the marsh-adjacent canopy provides cover), with the reception marquee installed at an adjacent Westham Island farm or Ladner Village venue.

## Millennium Park and community events

Millennium Park occasionally books supplemental marquees for community-scale events and larger seated-row functions. 20×20 to 20×40 footprints; park-permit coordination during quoting.

## East Ladner — newer residential

East Ladner's newer residential streets accept standard delivery patterns with friendly truck access. Most East Ladner backyards accept a 20×40 to 20×60 comfortably.
