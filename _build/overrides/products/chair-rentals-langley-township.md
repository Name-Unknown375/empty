## Vineyard, berry-farm, park, and Events Centre patterns

![Rows of white ceremony chairs at an outdoor wedding setup](/images/lifestyle/ceremony-chairs-3.jpg){.float-right}
*Ceremony chairs on Township grass.*

### Township 7 and Domaine de Chaberton — vineyard scale

Township 7 and Domaine de Chaberton drive the largest single Township chair orders. Vineyard weddings here regularly hit 200 guests with two-chair-line configurations: Resin Garden for the 200 outdoor ceremony rows in the vines, Chiavari for the 200 reception tables under marquee on the lawn. Truck access on the vineyard roads is ample; we coordinate the staging window with the harvesting and tasting-room schedule.

### Krause Berry Farms — agritourism weddings

Krause Berry Farms runs a meaningful share of the Township's summer wedding calendar. Berry-farm chair work has its own pattern: chairs stage on dew-prone grass before the morning fog burns off, and the dust-and-pollen reality of a working farm means individual chair wipe-downs after install. Resin Garden is the only outdoor pick here.

### Campbell Valley Regional Park

Campbell Valley's ceremony zones run on park-permit windows with strict setback rules. Resin Garden for the forest-canopy and meadow zones; Chiavari indoors at the adjacent venues if the reception flips. Truck access is at the parking lot, with hand-carry to the specific ceremony zone for the smaller setback configurations.

### Langley Events Centre — corporate at scale

Langley Events Centre's corporate galas, sports banquets, and community events run on the largest single-event chair counts in the Township. 300+ Fanback folding chairs is a routine order; Chiavari for head-table and dignitary seating ships alongside. Loading-dock access at the LEC is ample.

### Willoughby, Walnut Grove, Brookswood, Murrayville — residential

Standard residential backyard chair patterns. Willoughby's newer developments accept any chair configuration; Walnut Grove's wider lots accommodate larger ceremony rows comfortably; Brookswood's mature-tree backyards occasionally need a smaller chair row given lot constraints.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Campbell Valley acreage as at the Events Centre. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance from our warehouse, after-hours timing — never the per-chair rate.
