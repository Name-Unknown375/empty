## What our Vancouver chair lineup covers

![A clean white Chiavari chair photographed against a hedge — the wedding-reception standard](/images/products/chiavari-chairs-stack.jpg)
*A clean Chiavari — individually wiped before it loads.*

Three chair lines that cover almost every Vancouver event:

- **[White Chiavari](/product-white-chiavari-chair) ($8.50/day)** — the wedding-reception standard. Lacquered hardwood frame, ivory cushion, stacks 8 high. Best for indoor and covered-patio receptions, heritage venues, and any photographed ceremony where the formal frame is the aesthetic priority.
- **[Fanback folding](/product-fanback-garden-chair) ($3.25/day)** — the workhorse. Steel frame, folds flat, stacks compactly. Right for ceremony rows where photos prioritize the couple, school events, and any high-headcount Vancouver booking.
- **[Resin Garden](/product-resin-garden-chair) ($5.50/day)** — the outdoor-resilient pick. Resin frame and seat, more substantial visual than Fanback but more forgiving than Chiavari to weather and damp grass. The right call for shoulder-season outdoor ceremonies (October–March) and any Vancouver outdoor wedding where surface moisture is the variable.

> Mixing lines is free. Chiavari for the reception, Fanback for the ceremony rows — one quote, one delivery.

## Park, rooftop, backyard — Vancouver chair patterns

![Rows of white ceremony chairs facing an altar at an outdoor wedding](/images/lifestyle/ceremony-chairs-1.jpg){.float-right}
*White Chiavari ceremony rows staged on a Vancouver lawn.*

### Park ceremonies

Stanley Park, Queen Elizabeth Park, and VanDusen all accept any of the three chair lines — the choice is about photo aesthetic and forecast. Chiavari is the most-photographed pick for dry summer afternoons; Resin Garden handles wet-forecast bookings better.

### Rooftop and high-rise events

Yaletown and downtown rooftop ceremonies and receptions skew toward Chiavari for the formal frame and the controlled indoor-outdoor transition. Stacking 8 high keeps the freight-elevator trips manageable.

### Backyard receptions

West End and Kitsilano backyard weddings often pair Fanback folding for ceremony rows with Chiavari at the reception tables — common at higher headcounts (150+ guests) where the per-chair cost on the ceremony rows matters.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Yaletown as in any Vancouver neighbourhood. Our [Chiavari chair](/product-white-chiavari-chair) is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, freight elevator, slope — never the per-chair rate.

## Same-day flips and mid-event re-staging

Many Vancouver weddings run a single-tent ceremony-to-reception flip with the same chairs handling both phases. Our crew arrives 90 minutes before ceremony, sets the rows, and re-stages the chairs around the reception tables in the 30–45 minute cocktail-hour window. Mid-event re-staging is included on single-tent bookings; it's not an add-on line.
