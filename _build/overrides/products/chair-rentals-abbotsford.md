Tradex hosts the region's largest single-event seating configurations. 500+ Fanback folding chairs is a routine order; the loading-dock access is ample and we coordinate the delivery window around the venue's primary programming. Head-table Chiavari ships alongside.

Mill Lake Park's lakeside ceremony zone runs on city event permits with venue-coordinated delivery windows. Resin Garden for the lakeside ceremony; Chiavari indoors if the reception flips to a hotel or restaurant in central Abbotsford.

### Clayburn Village heritage

Clayburn Village's heritage streets run smaller, more intimate weddings (40–80 guests) with Chiavari indoors at the heritage venues. Truck access through the village is tight; we use shorter delivery windows.

### Sumas Mountain hillside

Sumas Mountain acreages run rural-road delivery with longer driveways. Most Sumas weddings are 100–150 guests with the full chair-line mix.
