## Yorkson, Bedford Landing, Gloucester — Walnut Grove marquee installs

![A clean white marquee tent set against a vibrant blue sky with fluffy clouds](/images/lifestyle/tent-golden-hour-2.jpg)
*A 20×60 marquee staged on a Walnut Grove residential lawn.*

The marquee calendar in Walnut Grove splits across three residential patterns:

- **Yorkson-border newer residential** — wider lot frontage, friendly truck access, most lots accept a 20×60 comfortably. Standard residential install template.
- **Bedford Landing-adjacent** — newer streets, mixed Walnut Grove and Fort Langley addresses, Chiavari-dominant reception aesthetic.
- **Gloucester rural-edge** — larger lots, longer driveway approaches, accept 20×60 and occasional 30×60 marquees for the 100–130 guest weddings.

The marquee itself doesn't change between these. What changes is the headcount the lot accommodates and the access pattern the truck takes.

## Derby Reach Regional Park

Derby Reach's forest-canopy ceremony zones rarely accept marquee installs — GVRD park-permit setbacks limit footprints to 20×20 cocktail tents at most. The standard Walnut Grove pattern: ceremony chairs-only at the park (the forest canopy provides cover), with the reception marquee installed at the adjacent Bedford Landing-adjacent or Yorkson residence.

## Walnut Grove Community Park and Secondary

Walnut Grove Community Park and the Secondary School zone occasionally book supplemental marquees for community-scale events. 20×20 to 20×40 footprints; park-permit coordination during quoting.

## Walnut Grove core residential

The established Walnut Grove residential core has narrower lot frontage than Yorkson but still accepts a 20×40 to 20×60 marquee at most addresses. Mature-tree backyards constrain marquee placement at some lots — we measure overhead-branch clearance during the pre-quote walk.

- 4 × **tent sidewalls** (full enclosure for evening)
- 8 × **bistro string-light strands** across the marquee ridge
