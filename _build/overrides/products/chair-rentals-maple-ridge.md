## Forest, lake, acreage — Maple Ridge surface considerations

![A clean white Chiavari chair photographed against a hedge](/images/products/chiavari-chairs-stack.jpg){.float-right}
*A Chiavari ready to load — individually wiped before delivery.*

### Forest-floor sites (Golden Ears)

Uneven ground, root systems, damp soil. Resin Garden travels well to these sites and handles the surface cleanly. Chiavari hardwood legs don't love saturated forest floor.

### Lakefront sites (Alouette Lake)

Saturated grass and morning dew. Resin Garden again — Chiavari's lacquered legs don't love sustained moisture from afternoon thermal off the lake.

### Acreage backyards

Hammond, Silver Valley, Albion. Standard mowed grass plus the elevation-driven afternoon wind. Chiavari for evening receptions under marquee cover; Fanback for ceremony rows when guest count is high.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Hammond as on Alouette Lake. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — surface, distance, multi-venue — never the per-chair rate.
