Best for Harrison Hot Springs Resort indoor and covered receptions, Harrison Mills heritage venues, Lakeshore residence lawn receptions under marquee, and any Harrison booking where the photos prioritize formal seating against the lake-and-mountain backdrops.

Right for the Resort's larger outdoor courtyard ceremonies, Harrison community-venue events, and any high-headcount destination booking where Chiavari budget would be prohibitive.

Right for Harrison Beach lakefront ceremonies, Sasquatch Provincial Park forest-canopy weddings, Harrison Lagoon outdoor ceremony zones, and any Harrison booking where the morning lake mist or forest dampness is the surface variable.

> Mixing lines is free. Destination weddings often need the full chair-line range across the event arc; we ship all three lines on a single Harrison delivery rather than splitting into multiple corridor runs.

## Resort, Beach, Sasquatch — Harrison chair patterns

![Rows of white ceremony chairs at an outdoor wedding setup](/images/lifestyle/ceremony-chairs-3.jpg){.float-right}
*Lakefront ceremony chairs staged at Harrison Hot Springs.*

### Harrison Hot Springs Resort

The Resort drives most of the destination calendar. Most Resort weddings are 80–150 guests with Chiavari for the covered or indoor reception and Resin Garden for any outdoor ceremony portion. We coordinate the install window with the Resort's events team during quoting; loading-dock access is ample, and the Resort's adjacent grounds accommodate marquee installs where the wedding needs an additional outdoor footprint.

### Harrison Beach lakefront

Harrison Beach's lakefront ceremony zones are the most-photographed destination outdoor venue in the Eastern Fraser Valley. Resin Garden every time for the beach-adjacent grass and the afternoon onshore breeze. Most beach ceremonies are 60–120 guest configurations; the reception flips to the Resort or a lakefront residence.

### Sasquatch Provincial Park forest ceremonies

Sasquatch Park's forest-canopy ceremony zones run on park-permit windows with strict setback rules. Resin Garden for the forest-floor surface — the only Lower Mainland chair line that tolerates damp duff and uneven ground at the Sasquatch ceremony scale. Hand-carry from the parking lot to the specific ceremony zone.

### Harrison Mills heritage and Lakeshore residential

Harrison Mills heritage venues and Lakeshore residence weddings run smaller (60–100 guests typical) with Chiavari-dominant configurations. Standard truck access on the Lakeshore residential streets; tighter access at the Harrison Mills heritage core.

### Harrison Lagoon and outdoor community

Harrison Lagoon and Harrison community-venue events round out the calendar with occasional outdoor cocktail-hour seating and ceremony rows. Fanback folding ships in volume for the community-scale events; Resin Garden for the lagoon-adjacent ceremonies.

### Per-item pricing

The same on Harrison Beach as at the Resort. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The destination corridor delivery is the line item that changes, not the per-chair rate.
