## What our North Vancouver chair lineup covers

*A North Shore ceremony — water, trees, and clean white seating.*

Three chair lines that cover almost every North Vancouver event:

- **[White Chiavari](/product-white-chiavari-chair) ($8.50/day)** — the wedding-reception standard. Lacquered hardwood frame, ivory cushion, stacks 8 high. Best for indoor and covered-patio receptions, Lonsdale-area restaurants, and any photographed ceremony where the formal frame is the aesthetic priority.
- **[Fanback folding](/product-fanback-garden-chair) ($3.25/day)** — the workhorse. Steel frame, folds flat, stacks compactly. Right for high-headcount ceremony rows, school events, and budget-conscious bookings.
- **[Resin Garden](/product-resin-garden-chair) ($5.50/day)** — the outdoor-resilient pick. Resin frame and seat, more substantial visual than Fanback but more forgiving than Chiavari to weather, damp grass, and forest floor. The right call for Lynn Canyon forest ceremonies, mountain-adjacent bookings, and any October–April outdoor North Van wedding.

> The surface chooses the chair before the photograph does. We don't book Chiavari onto saturated forest floor.

## Forest, mountain, waterfront — North Van chair patterns

![A clean white Chiavari chair photographed against a hedge](/images/products/chiavari-chairs-stack.jpg){.float-right}
*A Chiavari ready for the reception — individually wiped before loading.*

### Forest-floor ceremonies

Lynn Canyon, Capilano Suspension Bridge Park, and the wooded zones along the North Shore favour Resin Garden chairs. The forest floor is uneven and the soil holds moisture; Chiavari hardwood doesn't love either condition.

### Mountain-elevation ceremonies

Grouse Mountain and Mount Seymour ceremonies sit higher than they look on a map. Wind exposure is real, and morning dew on the elevated turf affects chair choice. Resin Garden again — and we recommend ballasted weight backup on the chair-side decor (signs, flowers) at any mountain venue.

### Waterfront ceremonies

Lonsdale Quay-area weddings and waterfront restaurant patios accept Chiavari fine — the surface is concrete or controlled patio rather than damp grass. Wind exposure here is moderate; sidewall infrastructure isn't usually needed for chair-only setups.

### Per-item pricing

The same on Grouse Mountain as in Edgemont. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — grade, mountain access, distance — never the per-chair rate.
