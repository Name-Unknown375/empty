### Heritage and Deer Lake venues

Hart House, Burnaby Village Museum, the Shadbolt Centre, and Deer Lake Park all run their own programming calendars. Vendor access is venue-coordinated. Most heritage venues skip the marquee entirely; outdoor zones at Deer Lake Park accept 20×40 and 20×60 footprints depending on the specific ceremony site.

### Brentwood and Metrotown rooftops

High-rise rooftop installs need three things confirmed before quoting: freight-elevator dimensions, weight rating, and the building manager's Saturday availability. Ballast at every leg, sidewalls year-round, and a marquee size adjusted for the rooftop's structural capacity rather than nominal headcount.

### Detached-home backyards

Standard backyard pattern: setbacks, slope, sprinkler heads, and where the truck can stop without blocking the alley. Most North Burnaby and Deer Lake-area lots accept a 20×40 comfortably.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Brentwood as in any Burnaby neighbourhood. Our 20×40 marquee is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — elevator, anchor strategy, after-hours timing — never the tent rate.

## A 100-guest Burnaby tent rental order

For a 100-guest Burnaby wedding — say a Saturday in August at Deer Lake Park's lakefront lawn, ceremony and reception under a single tent — a typical tent-side order looks like:

- 1 × **20×60 marquee tent** (covered ceremony + reception in single footprint)
- 4 × **tent sidewalls** (full enclosure for evening)
- 8 × **bistro string-light strands** across the marquee ridge
- 1 × **tent heater** (October–April bookings; optional in summer)
