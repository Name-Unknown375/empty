## Rocky Point, Shoreline Trail, Buntzen — Port Moody chair patterns

### Rocky Point Park and Shoreline Trail waterfront

Rocky Point Park and the Shoreline Trail run on tight permit windows and Inlet-edge wind exposure. Resin Garden is the standard. Vehicle access is via the park gates with venue-coordinated timing; we pre-stage equipment near the ceremony zone to minimize the carry distance from the truck.

### Buntzen Lake forest ceremonies

Buntzen Lake's forested ceremony zones are damp, uneven, and accessed via the parking lot. Resin Garden every time; we hand-carry chairs the final stretch when the ceremony zone is past the trailhead.

### Heritage Mountain hillside backyards

Heritage Mountain weddings are typically smaller (50–100 guests) and held at hillside acreage residences. Newer streets accept the standard delivery truck without issue.

### Inlet Centre, Moody Centre, civic

Port Moody Arts Centre and Inlet Centre civic venues run on dock-controlled schedules. Most Inlet Centre weddings lean Chiavari for indoor receptions; civic events at Moody Centre run Fanback folding for high-headcount registrations.

### Anmore-border acreage

Anmore-border weddings off Sunnyside Road use rural roads with ample truck access. Larger acreage receptions (100–150 guests) take the full chair-line mix: Resin Garden outdoor ceremony, Chiavari indoor reception under a marquee.

### Per-item pricing

The same on Heritage Mountain as on Buntzen Lake. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — hillside access, after-hours timing — never the per-chair rate.
