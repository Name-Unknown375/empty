## What our Burnaby table lineup covers

![A formal round dinner table with full place settings, candles, and a floral centerpiece](/images/lifestyle/round-table-dinner-1.jpg)
*A 5ft round set for 8 — the Burnaby wedding-reception standard.*

Three tables, three jobs. Burnaby event floor plans usually need all three:

- **[5ft round tables](/product-round-table-5ft) ($13.50/day, seats 8)** — the classic wedding-reception configuration. Eight guests, room for centerpieces, fits two-deep into most marquee footprints (a 20×40 holds 6 rounds; a 20×60 holds 12).
- **[6ft rectangular banquet tables](/product-banquet-table-6ft) ($10.95/day, seats 6–8)** — the single most versatile SKU we ship. Family-style dining rows, buffet stations, gift tables, DJ booths, bar setups, registration desks, head tables. Under 75 lb so they re-stage quickly mid-event.
- **[Cocktail highboy tables](/product-cocktail-table) ($15.50/day, standing)** — 42-inch standing-height rounds for cocktail hour, networking events, and pre-dinner mingling. Plan ~1 highboy per 10–12 standing guests.

> Tables care most about access and floor plan — we don't add a per-table setup charge when delivery is on the quote.

## Burnaby venue access and floor-plan staging

*Cocktail highboys staged for a Brentwood corporate event.*

### Heritage venues (Hart House, Burnaby Village Museum)

Smaller table counts (often under 60 guests indoors), heritage-protected sightlines that constrain layout. We coordinate with the venue's events team during quoting on placement and access.

### Brentwood and Metrotown corporate

Office plazas and rooftop venues with controlled freight elevators. Our 6ft banquet at <75 lb fits nearly every freight elevator; the 8ft is occasionally tight. We confirm dimensions and weight rating before quoting larger tables.

### Deer Lake and North Burnaby backyards

Standard backyard pattern: grass and a level patio. Highboys on the patio, banquets and rounds on the grass — we level them during the staging window.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Brentwood as on Deer Lake. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — elevator, anchor, after-hours timing — never the per-table rate.

## Mid-event flips and ceremony-to-reception staging

Many Burnaby weddings run a single-tent ceremony-to-reception flip — same chairs, same marquee, with our crew rearranging the table layout in 30–45 minutes during the cocktail-hour window. Banquet tables fold flat and re-position quickly; rounds re-stack to the truck while the dinner configuration goes up. This is included on single-tent bookings, not an add-on line.

## A 100-guest Burnaby table rental order

For a 100-guest Burnaby wedding with a marquee reception and a separate cocktail-hour space, a typical table-side order looks like:

- 12 × **5ft round tables** (8 guests per round = 96, with 2 extras for kids and overflow)
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head linking 2, gift, cake, DJ, registration)
- 2 × **6ft banquet tables** for the buffet line
- 3 × **cocktail highboys** for the pre-dinner cocktail hour

Total: 23 tables, all positioned to your floor plan during a single delivery window. For other guest counts, the [50/100/150/200 rental checklist](/blog/party-rental-checklist-50-100-150-200-guests) maps the table mix to your headcount.
