## What our West Vancouver table lineup covers

![A formal round dinner table with full place settings, candles, and a floral centerpiece](/images/lifestyle/round-table-dinner-1.jpg)
*A 5ft round set for 8 — the West Van estate-reception standard.*

Three tables, three jobs. West Van floor plans typically need all three:

- **[5ft round tables](/product-round-table-5ft) ($13.50/day, seats 8)** — the classic wedding-reception configuration. Eight guests, room for centerpieces, fits two-deep into a 20×40 (6 rounds) or two and a half deep into a 20×60 (12 rounds).
- **[6ft rectangular banquet tables](/product-banquet-table-6ft) ($10.95/day, seats 6–8)** — the most versatile SKU. Head tables, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar, buffet. Under 75 lb so they re-stage quickly mid-event.
- **[Cocktail highboy tables](/product-cocktail-table) ($15.50/day, standing)** — 42-inch standing-height rounds. The Ambleside seawall and Dundarave waterfront crowds run heavier on highboys than most West Van bookings — pre-dinner cocktail hour on the terrace is the format. Plan ~1 highboy per 10–12 standing guests.

## West Van venue patterns and floor-plan staging

![Cocktail highboy tables at an outdoor reception with guests in conversation](/images/lifestyle/cocktail-hour-1.jpg){.float-right}
*Highboys for a Dundarave waterfront pre-dinner hour.*

### British Properties estate receptions

Terraced lawns and irregular grade. Round tables get levelled during the staging window; we shim where the slope is mild and lay subfloor panels where it isn't. Most estate receptions run 100–150 guests on 12–18 rounds.

### Ambleside and Dundarave waterfront

Heavier cocktail-hour configurations than other West Van venues. The seawall and waterfront-home decks reward a longer mingling phase before dinner, which means 4–6 highboys for a 100-guest booking rather than the standard 2–3. The 8ft banquet works for the buffet but not always for the head table — sightlines matter when the ocean's behind you.

### Horseshoe Bay restaurants and waterfront homes

Narrow service entrances and ferry-timed deliveries. The 6ft banquet at <75 lb fits through nearly every restaurant service door; the 8ft is occasionally tight. We confirm access dimensions during quoting.

### Park Royal corporate

Office plaza and mid-rise venues with controlled freight elevators. Smaller cocktail-driven configurations than estate receptions; highboys and 6ft banquets for registration drive most quotes.

### Per-item pricing

The same on the Ambleside seawall as in British Properties. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. What changes is delivery — bridge timing, terraced-lawn levelling, ferry queue — never the per-table rate.

## Salt-air and waterfront table considerations

The single venue-specific note we share with West Van waterfront bookings: salt-air exposure accelerates linen aging if the reception runs all day in direct ocean breeze. Our linen partners recommend the heavier polyester blends rather than the lighter cotton-touch lines for exposed Ambleside and Dundarave bookings — they hold up better through a five-hour reception in salt spray. Indoors and inland (British Properties, Caulfeild interior, Park Royal) the lighter linens are fine. We flag this in the quote when the venue is a waterfront site.

## A 100-guest West Vancouver table rental order

For a 100-guest West Van wedding with a British Properties estate reception (the most common West Van pattern), a typical table-side order looks like:

- 12 × **5ft round tables** (96 dinner guests at 8 per round, with 2 extras for kids and overflow)
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head linking 2, gift, cake, DJ, registration)
- 2 × **6ft banquet tables** for the buffet line
- 3 × **cocktail highboys** for the pre-dinner hour on the terrace

For a Dundarave or Ambleside waterfront reception, swap to 4–5 highboys for a longer cocktail phase. For other guest counts, the [50/100/150/200 rental checklist](/blog/party-rental-checklist-50-100-150-200-guests) maps the table mix to your headcount.
