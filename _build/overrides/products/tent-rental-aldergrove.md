Rural-residential backyard weddings drive the Aldergrove marquee calendar. Most are 60–120 guest configurations under a 20×40 or 20×60 marquee on the residential lawn. North Aldergrove and the County Line Diversion area offer the largest lots and accept the larger marquees comfortably.

Standard install pattern:

- 20×40 marquee for 60–80 guest weddings, 20×60 for 100–120 guests
- Engineered ground stakes for grass; supplemental ballast on side-yard hardscape segments
- Sidewalls year-round on open-field installs
- Bistro string lighting along the ridge

Aldergrove Village's central residential streets accept standard delivery patterns. Most village backyards accept a 20×30 to 20×40 marquee comfortably; lots in the older village core constrain footprint to 20×30 or smaller.
