Three chair lines that cover almost every Richmond event:

Best for Brighouse hotel ballroom receptions, Olympic Oval gala seating, indoor restaurant weddings along No. 3 Road, and any Richmond venue where the photos prioritize formal seating.
- **[Fanback folding](/product-fanback-garden-chair) ($3.25/day)** — the workhorse. Steel frame, folds flat, stacks compactly. Resin frame and seat, more substantial visual than Fanback but more forgiving than Chiavari to salt air, river dampness, and coastal humidity. The right call for Garry Point Park beach ceremonies, Steveston Heritage Village waterfront weddings, and Minoru Park outdoor events.

## Steveston, Brighouse, Olympic Oval — Richmond chair patterns

![A row of cocktail-hour highboy tables at an outdoor reception with guests in conversation](/images/lifestyle/cocktail-hour-1.jpg){.float-right}
*Cocktail highboys staged for a Brighouse corporate event.*

### Steveston and Garry Point waterfront

Steveston Heritage Fishing Village and Garry Point Park run salt-air, dampness, and afternoon onshore wind — every detail matters for chair selection. Resin Garden is the standard for these waterfront ceremonies. Vehicle access is straightforward through the village; truck-stop windows around fish-shop deliveries get coordinated during quoting.

### Brighouse and Richmond Centre — banquet and hotel weddings

Brighouse hosts most of Richmond's hotel-ballroom weddings (Sheraton, Pacific Gateway, Marriott, Hilton). Banquet-style weddings of 250–400 guests are routine — Chiavari for the head and family tables, Fanback folding for the broader floor when the configuration runs full theatre-style. Loading-dock access at most Brighouse hotels is controlled and we book the window during quoting.

### Olympic Oval and River Rock — large corporate and gala events

The Olympic Oval handles 500–1000 guest galas and large corporate events. Freight access is ample but the loading schedule is tightly programmed. River Rock Show Theatre runs on its own dock calendar and we book against their event programming, not against ours.

### Hamilton, Thompson, Seafair backyards

Detached-home Richmond backyards are flat (the whole city is delta-flat) and large. Sprinkler heads and irrigation runs matter for stake decisions; most Hamilton and Seafair lots accept any chair configuration without anchoring concerns.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Steveston as in Brighouse. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — venue dock access, after-hours timing, distance — never the per-chair rate.
