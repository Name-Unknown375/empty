## What our Coquitlam table lineup covers

![A formal round dinner table with full place settings, candles, and a floral centerpiece](/images/lifestyle/round-table-dinner-1.jpg)
*A 5ft round set for 8 — the Coquitlam wedding-reception standard.*

Three tables, three jobs. Coquitlam event floor plans usually need all three:

- **[5ft round tables](/product-round-table-5ft) ($13.50/day, seats 8)** — the classic wedding-reception configuration. Eight guests, room for centerpieces, fits two-deep into most marquee footprints (a 20×40 holds 6 rounds; a 20×60 holds 12). Standard for Westwood Plateau clubhouse weddings.
- **[6ft rectangular banquet tables](/product-banquet-table-6ft) ($10.95/day, seats 6–8)** — the single most versatile SKU we ship. Family-style dining rows, buffet stations, gift tables, DJ booths, bar setups, registration desks at Town Centre corporate events. Under 75 lb so they re-stage quickly mid-event.
- **[Cocktail highboy tables](/product-cocktail-table) ($15.50/day, standing)** — 42-inch standing-height rounds for cocktail hour, networking events, and pre-dinner mingling. Plan ~1 highboy per 10–12 standing guests.

> Tables care most about access and floor plan — we don't add a per-table setup charge when delivery is on the quote.

## Coquitlam venue access and floor-plan staging

![A row of cocktail-hour highboy tables at an outdoor reception with guests in conversation behind](/images/lifestyle/cocktail-hour-1.jpg){.float-right}
*Cocktail highboys staged for a Town Centre corporate event.*

### Westwood Plateau Golf — clubhouse and ceremony lawn

The most-booked upscale wedding venue in Coquitlam. Clubhouse loading dock for indoor reception tables, cart-path access for ceremony-lawn tables. Most Westwood weddings run 100–150 guests with the full table mix (rounds + banquets + highboys).

### Lafarge Lake and Town Centre civic

Lafarge Lake's lakeside zone runs city event permits with venue-coordinated delivery windows. Town Centre Park accommodates larger civic gatherings with controlled access; corporate events here lean cocktail highboys and registration banquets over rounds.

### Burke Mountain hillside backyards

Standard delivery truck handles most Burke Mountain streets; the older switchbacks at the very top occasionally need smaller-truck staging. Most Burke Mountain backyards run smaller table counts (50–100 guests) with simpler banquet-and-rounds configurations.

### Minnekhada forest ceremonies

Forest-zone ceremonies are typically table-light — most run hand-passed cocktail hour rather than seated dinner. When seated dinner is part of the program, we hand-carry banquet tables from the parking lot to the ceremony zone.

### Maillardville heritage-residential

Tight street parking and narrow side passages constrain delivery. We use shorter delivery windows and pre-stage closer to the property line; lots themselves accept any standard table configuration.

### Per-item pricing

The same on Burke Mountain as in Maillardville. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — hillside access, after-hours timing, distance — never the per-table rate.

## Mid-event flips and ceremony-to-reception staging

Many Coquitlam weddings run a single-tent ceremony-to-reception flip — same chairs, same marquee, with our crew rearranging the table layout in 30–45 minutes during the cocktail-hour window. Banquet tables fold flat and re-position quickly; rounds re-stack to the truck while the dinner configuration goes up. This is included on single-tent bookings, not an add-on line.

## A 100-guest Coquitlam table rental order

For a 100-guest Coquitlam wedding with a marquee reception and a separate cocktail-hour space, a typical table-side order looks like:

- 12 × **5ft round tables** (8 guests per round = 96, with 2 extras for kids and overflow)
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head linking 2, gift, cake, DJ, registration)
- 2 × **6ft banquet tables** for the buffet line
- 3 × **cocktail highboys** for the pre-dinner cocktail hour
