## Coquitlam tent install patterns we cover

![Cocktail-hour highboy tables set up at an outdoor reception with guests in conversation behind](/images/lifestyle/cocktail-hour-1.jpg){.float-right}
*Cocktail highboys staged for a Town Centre corporate event.*

### Lafarge Lake and Town Centre civic

Lafarge Lake's lakeside lawn and Town Centre Park run on city event permits with specific setback rules and venue-coordinated delivery windows. Stakes-plus-ballast is standard for the lakeside zone given variable inlet wind. Most civic-scale marquees here run 20×40 to 20×60.

### Westwood Plateau Golf — slope-aware ceremony installs

Westwood's ceremony lawn is graded for golf and the slope changes tent placement options. We walk the site during quoting to confirm the marquee position; for steeper grades we adjust the footprint or add supplemental anchor.

### Burke Mountain hillside acreage

Hillside acreage along the upper Burke streets often requires engineered slope-staking, supplemental downhill ballast, or — for the steepest sites — a smaller marquee than the headcount would otherwise suggest. We don't squeeze a 20×60 onto a slope that calls for two 20×30s.

### Minnekhada and Mundy forest ceremonies

Forest-zone ceremonies need permit coordination, hand-carry delivery from the parking lot, and uneven-ground anchor strategies. Smaller marquee footprints (20×20, 20×30) are standard for the forest sites; larger footprints stay on the open meadow.

### Maillardville, Eagle Ridge, Town Centre backyards

Standard backyard pattern: setbacks, slope, sprinkler heads, and where the truck can stop without blocking the laneway. Maillardville's heritage streets are tight on truck access; we use shorter delivery windows and pre-stage equipment closer to the property line.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Maillardville as on Burke Mountain. Our 20×40 marquee is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — slope, anchor strategy, after-hours timing — never the tent rate.
