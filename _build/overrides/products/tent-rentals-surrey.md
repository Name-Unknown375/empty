## South Surrey peninsula — the marquee calendar core

South Surrey acreage weddings are the largest single share of our Surrey marquee bookings. Crescent Beach, Hazelmere Valley, Elgin, and the broader peninsula run on rural-road truck access with firm lawn or graded acreage as the surface.

The standard install is a 20×60 marquee with sidewalls, engineered ground stakes, bistro string lighting along the ridge, and (October–April) a tent heater. Setup runs 4–5 hours; teardown is 2–3. For larger weddings (150–200 guests), 30×60 marquee on a single footprint or linked 20×40s for split ceremony/reception zones.

Hazelmere afternoon wind off the peninsula is reliable enough that we recommend sidewalls year-round on open-field installs here. The marquee itself doesn't change between a Cloverdale install and a Hazelmere install — what changes is anchor count.

Cloverdale Rodeo Grounds and Stetson Bowl run some of the largest event footprints in Surrey. For Cloverdale Rodeo–adjacent corporate galas and the Surrey Fair grounds-style events, the marquee is one piece of a much larger order: dance floor for the central tent, bistro lighting strands across the ridge, sidewalls for noise control and weather, and the chairs and tables that go inside.

## Bear Creek Park, Surrey Civic Plaza, and ceremony permits

Bear Creek Park's ceremony zones take 20×40 and 20×60 footprints depending on the permit zone. Civic Plaza hardscape installs cap at 20×40 in most configurations — coordinated with the City's events team during quoting.

Park-permit ceremony marquees are typically smaller than acreage receptions (50–100 guest range). We've worked the Bear Creek calendar enough to know the standard install windows and which zones accept which footprints.

## Newton, Whalley, Guildford, Fleetwood — backyard residential

Standard residential backyard pattern: setbacks, slope, sprinkler heads, and where the truck can stop without blocking the laneway. Most Surrey lots accept a 20×40 comfortably; larger lots (1/4-acre+) accommodate 20×60. We walk the lot during quoting for backyards we haven't installed at.
