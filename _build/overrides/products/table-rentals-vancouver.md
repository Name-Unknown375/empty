## What our Vancouver table lineup covers

![A formal round dinner table with full place settings, candles, and a floral centerpiece set for a wedding reception](/images/lifestyle/round-table-dinner-1.jpg)
*A 5ft round set for 8 — the Vancouver wedding-reception standard.*

Three tables, three jobs. Vancouver event floor plans usually need all three:

- **[5ft round tables](/product-round-table-5ft) ($13.50/day, seats 8)** — the classic wedding-reception configuration. Eight guests, room for centerpieces, fits two-deep into most marquee footprints (a 20×40 holds 6 rounds; a 20×60 holds 12). The reception workhorse.
- **[6ft rectangular banquet tables](/product-banquet-table-6ft) ($10.95/day, seats 6–8)** — the single most versatile SKU we ship. Family-style dining rows, buffet stations, gift tables, DJ booths, bar setups, registration desks, head tables. Under 75 lb so they re-stage quickly mid-event.
- **[Cocktail highboy tables](/product-cocktail-table) ($15.50/day, standing)** — 42-inch standing-height rounds for cocktail hour, networking events, and pre-dinner mingling. Plan ~1 highboy per 10–12 standing guests.

> Tables care most about access and floor plan — and we don't add a per-table setup charge when delivery is on the quote.

## Floor-plan staging and Vancouver venue access

![A row of cocktail-hour highboy tables at an outdoor reception with guests in conversation behind](/images/lifestyle/cocktail-hour-1.jpg){.float-right}
*Cocktail highboys staged for a Vancouver reception cocktail hour.*

### Park ceremonies

Minimal tables — usually just a registration table, a guest book table, and maybe a small refreshment table. Chairs are the count that matters; tables are accent pieces here.

### Yaletown rooftops

Weight and elevator constraints. We confirm freight-elevator interior dimensions and weight ratings before quoting any 8ft banquet table. The 6ft banquet at <75 lb fits nearly every freight elevator we've seen; the 8ft is occasionally tight.

### Convention Centre and downtown corporate

Loading-dock booking systems. Table delivery is timed with the dock window, which closes earlier than the event start. We schedule around it; you don't manage it.

### Backyard weddings

Kitsilano, West End, Dunbar properties usually have grass and a level patio. Highboys go on the patio, banquets and rounds go on the grass — we level them.

### Per-item pricing

The same in Yaletown as in Kitsilano. Our [5ft round table](/product-round-table-5ft) is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, freight elevator, slope — never the per-table rate.

## A 100-guest Vancouver table rental order

For a 100-guest Vancouver wedding with a marquee reception and a separate cocktail-hour space, a typical table-side order looks like:

- 12 × **5ft round tables** (8 guests per round = 96, with 2 extras for kids and overflow)
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head linking 2, gift, cake, DJ, registration)
- 2 × **6ft banquet tables** for the buffet line
- 3 × **cocktail highboys** for the pre-dinner cocktail hour

Total: 23 tables, all positioned to your floor plan during a single delivery window. Combined with chairs, marquee, and (often) a dance floor, this is one truck, one quote, one crew.
