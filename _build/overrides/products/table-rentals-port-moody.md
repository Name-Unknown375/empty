## What our Port Moody table lineup covers

![A formal round dinner table with full place settings, candles, and a floral centerpiece](/images/lifestyle/round-table-dinner-1.jpg)
*A 5ft round set for 8 — the Port Moody wedding-reception standard.*

Three tables, three jobs. Port Moody event floor plans usually need all three:

- **[5ft round tables](/product-round-table-5ft) ($13.50/day, seats 8)** — the classic wedding-reception configuration. Eight guests, room for centerpieces, fits two-deep into most marquee footprints (a 20×40 holds 6 rounds; a 20×60 holds 12). Standard for Anmore-border acreage marquee receptions.
- **[6ft rectangular banquet tables](/product-banquet-table-6ft) ($10.95/day, seats 6–8)** — the single most versatile SKU we ship. Family-style dining rows, buffet stations, gift tables, DJ booths, bar setups, registration desks at Inlet Centre civic events. Under 75 lb so they re-stage quickly mid-event.
- **[Cocktail highboy tables](/product-cocktail-table) ($15.50/day, standing)** — 42-inch standing-height rounds for cocktail hour, networking events, and pre-dinner mingling. Plan ~1 highboy per 10–12 standing guests.

> Tables care most about access and floor plan — we don't add a per-table setup charge when delivery is on the quote.

### Rocky Point Park and Shoreline Trail — waterfront

Wind-aware. Banquet bases over highboy bistros where the site is exposed. Permit-coordinated delivery windows; we pre-stage near the ceremony zone to minimize the carry from the truck.

### Bert Flinn Park and Buntzen Lake

Bert Flinn Park accommodates larger table counts; Buntzen Lake's forest zones stay smaller and require hand-carry from the parking lot.

### Heritage Mountain backyards

Smaller weddings (50–100 guests) with simpler banquet runs. Most Heritage Mountain bookings run 8 rounds + 4 banquets + 2 highboys.

### Anmore-border acreage

Full table mix — 12+ rounds, 6–8 banquets, 3–4 highboys. Rural-road truck access; we pre-stage near the ceremony zone for longer driveways.

### Inlet Centre, Moody Centre, Arts Centre civic

Loading-dock access at Port Moody Arts Centre and Inlet Centre venues runs on tight schedules we book during quoting. Civic events lean cocktail highboys and registration banquets over rounds.

### Per-item pricing

The same on Heritage Mountain as at Rocky Point. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — never the per-table rate.

## Mid-event flips and ceremony-to-reception staging

Many Port Moody weddings run a single-tent ceremony-to-reception flip — same chairs, same marquee, with our crew rearranging the table layout in 30–45 minutes during the cocktail-hour window. Banquet tables fold flat and re-position quickly; rounds re-stack to the truck while the dinner configuration goes up. This is included on single-tent bookings, not an add-on line.

## A 100-guest Port Moody table rental order

For a 100-guest Port Moody wedding with a marquee reception and a separate cocktail-hour space, a typical table-side order looks like:

- 12 × **5ft round tables** (8 guests per round = 96, with 2 extras for kids and overflow)
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head linking 2, gift, cake, DJ, registration)
- 2 × **6ft banquet tables** for the buffet line
- 3 × **cocktail highboys** for the pre-dinner cocktail hour
