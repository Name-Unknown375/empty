## What our Abbotsford table lineup covers

![A formal round dinner table with full place settings, candles, and a floral centerpiece](/images/lifestyle/round-table-dinner-1.jpg)
*A 5ft round set for 8 — the Abbotsford wedding-reception standard.*

Three tables, three jobs. Abbotsford event floor plans usually need all three:

- **[5ft round tables](/product-round-table-5ft) ($13.50/day, seats 8)** — the classic wedding-reception configuration. Eight guests, room for centerpieces, fits two-deep into most marquee footprints (a 20×40 holds 6 rounds; a 20×60 holds 12). Standard for vineyard and farm marquee receptions.
- **[6ft rectangular banquet tables](/product-banquet-table-6ft) ($10.95/day, seats 6–8)** — the single most versatile SKU we ship. Family-style dining rows in vineyard barns, buffet stations, gift tables, DJ booths, registration desks at Tradex galas. Under 75 lb so they re-stage quickly mid-event. Linked banquets are a vineyard-barn favourite for farm-table head configurations.
- **[Cocktail highboy tables](/product-cocktail-table) ($15.50/day, standing)** — 42-inch standing-height rounds for cocktail hour, networking events, and pre-dinner mingling. Plan ~1 highboy per 10–12 standing guests. Tradex corporate events often run 30+ highboys.

> Tables care most about access and floor plan — we don't add a per-table setup charge when delivery is on the quote.

## Abbotsford venue access and floor-plan staging

### Vineyards and farms — Mount Lehman, Matsqui

Standard rounds-and-banquets mix, often with linked banquets for farm-table head configurations. Truck access is ample on rural roads; we stage tables in the barn or under the marquee during the delivery window.

### Tradex Exhibition Centre — gala-scale corporate

Tradex galas run the largest single-event table counts on our calendar — 50+ banquet registration tables and 30+ cocktail highboys is routine. Loading dock access is ample.

### Mill Lake Park and civic

Mill Lake Park requires city event-permit coordination. Standard table mix; smaller civic gatherings usually run banquet-and-cocktail-highboy configurations rather than rounds.

### Clayburn Village heritage

Smaller, more intimate weddings (60–100 guests). Tight heritage streets constrain delivery; we use shorter delivery windows and pre-stage closer to the property line.

### Sumas Mountain and McKee acreage

Standard residential backyard patterns with longer driveways. Most acreage weddings take the full table mix at 100–200 guests.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a vineyard as at Tradex. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — never the per-table rate.

## Mid-event flips and ceremony-to-reception staging

Many Abbotsford vineyard and farm weddings run a single-tent ceremony-to-reception flip — same chairs, same marquee, with our crew rearranging the table layout in 30–45 minutes during the cocktail-hour window. Banquet tables fold flat and re-position quickly; rounds re-stack to the truck while the dinner configuration goes up. This is included on single-tent bookings, not an add-on line.

## A 100-guest Abbotsford table rental order

For a 100-guest Abbotsford wedding with a vineyard barn reception and a separate cocktail-hour space, a typical table-side order looks like:

- 12 × **5ft round tables** (8 guests per round = 96, with 2 extras for kids and overflow)
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head linking 2 in farm-table configuration, gift, cake, DJ, registration)
- 2 × **6ft banquet tables** for the buffet line
- 3 × **cocktail highboys** for the pre-dinner cocktail hour

Total: 23 tables, all positioned to your floor plan during a single delivery window. For Tradex galas (250+ guests), the table count and mix shift toward banquet-and-highboy configurations rather than rounds.
