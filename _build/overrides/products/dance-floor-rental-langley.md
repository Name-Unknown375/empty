## What our Langley dance floor lineup covers

![A wedding marquee with guests dancing at night under festive lighting](/images/lifestyle/dance-floor-3.jpg)
*A Langley vineyard reception in full swing.*

Three floor sizes that cover almost every Langley vineyard, acreage, and corporate event:

- **12×12 portable dance floor** — 60–80 guests, smaller acreage backyards, intimate vineyard receptions.
- **12×16 portable dance floor** — the workhorse. 100-guest vineyard weddings, most marquee setups across Brookswood and Murrayville.
- **16×20 portable dance floor** — 150–200 guests (more common at Langley vineyards than urban weddings), larger acreage receptions.
- **Subfloor panels** — engineered platforms that level grass, farm soil, and sloped lawns beneath the dance floor. Almost always required for Langley installs.
- **Edge ramps** — included on every floor install. Trip-hazard safety, not aesthetic.

> Vineyard grass holds more moisture than urban lawns. Subfloor isn't an upsell here — it's how the floor stays tight at midnight.

Township 7, Krause, Domaine de Chaberton. Subfloor on every install — the lawn near tasting rooms holds enough irrigation moisture that staking subfloor is non-negotiable. We coordinate the install window with the venue's harvest and tasting-room schedule.

### Acreage installs

Brookswood, Murrayville. Mowed grass with the standard Langley afternoon thermal wind. Subfloor levels the surface; we confirm slope during the pre-quote walk for unfamiliar properties.

### Langley Events Centre corporate

Indoor floors at LEC accept the dance floor directly with edge ramps only — no subfloor. Setup is faster (45–60 minutes for a 12×16) than vineyard installs.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Township 7 lawn as in Brookswood. Our 12×16 portable dance floor is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery and subfloor — surface, slope, multi-venue — never the per-floor rate.

## A 100-guest Langley dance floor rental order

For a 100-guest Langley vineyard wedding at Township 7 with marquee reception, a typical dance floor order looks like:

- 1 × **12×16 portable dance floor** (the standard for 100 guests)
- Subfloor panels (level the vineyard lawn beneath the floor)
- Edge ramps on all four sides
- Crew install (90–120 minutes for floor + subfloor)
