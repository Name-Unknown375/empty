## Surface, canopy, and the Maple Ridge anchor strategy

![Rows of white ceremony chairs facing a decorated altar with trees and water in the background](/images/lifestyle/ceremony-chairs-2.jpg){.float-right}
*A forest-canopy ceremony staged for a Maple Ridge wedding.*

### Forest-canopy ceremonies

Golden Ears and the wooded Maple Ridge acreages have overhead-branch constraints that flat urban yards don't. Most forest-floor zones skip the marquee entirely (chair-only ceremonies); meadow and clearing zones accommodate 20×20 or 20×40 footprints. Our 13' peak fits most clearings but not all — we measure during the pre-quote walk.

### Alouette Lake shoreline installs

Lakefront sites need ballast (saturated soil holds stakes poorly), sidewalls year-round (afternoon thermal off the lake is meaningful), and morning-fog tolerance for early ceremonies — sidewalls help with that too.

### Hammond and Silver Valley acreages

Engineered stakes paired with ballast on the leeward corners is the default. We confirm overhead branches, driveway grade, and septic/sprinkler maps for any acreage we haven't installed at — slope tolerance is 5% on grass with no engineered platform.

### Per-item pricing

The same on a Silver Valley acreage as in Hammond. Our 20×40 marquee is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, anchor strategy, surface — never the tent rate.

## Whonnock and Albion rural-residential

Whonnock and Albion's larger lots accommodate a 20×60 marquee comfortably and many properties have room for two linked 20×40 tents for larger headcounts. Driveway approach measured in hundreds of metres rather than tens; we factor truck-stage distance into the crew-time line. Gate codes and livestock considerations get confirmed during quoting, not on event day.
