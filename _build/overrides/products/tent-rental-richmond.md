## Richmond tent install patterns we cover

### Garry Point Park and Steveston waterfront

Salt air, afternoon onshore wind, and tight permit setbacks. Stakes plus ballast every time, sidewalls recommended year-round, marquee size adjusted to fit the specific permit zone rather than the nominal headcount. We've installed enough Garry Point marquees to know which zones tolerate a 20×60 versus where a 20×40 is the cap.

### Olympic Oval and River Rock — large-scale events

Olympic Oval freight access is ample; River Rock and adjacent hotels run on dock-controlled schedules. Most large Richmond galas split into linked marquees rather than one mega-tent for both anchor stability and faster install timing.

### Brighouse hotel-grounds and corporate

Hotel-grounds installs at Brighouse properties (Sheraton, Pacific Gateway, etc.) run controlled access via the loading dock. Hardscape footprints mean full ballast every time; sidewalls common to control noise and lighting bleed into hotel rooms.

### Hamilton, Thompson, Seafair backyards

Standard backyard pattern: setbacks, sprinkler heads, irrigation runs, and where the truck can stop without blocking the laneway. Richmond lots are flat and large — most Hamilton and Seafair backyards accept a 20×60 comfortably.

### Per-item pricing

The same at Garry Point as on a Hamilton acreage. Our 20×40 marquee is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — anchor strategy, after-hours timing, dock coordination — never the tent rate.
