## Township 7, Domaine de Chaberton — vineyard scale

![A clean white marquee tent set against a vibrant blue sky with fluffy clouds](/images/lifestyle/tent-golden-hour-2.jpg)
*A 20×40 marquee on open Township grass — vineyard reception standard.*

Township 7 Vineyards and Domaine de Chaberton drive a meaningful share of the Township marquee calendar. Vineyard weddings here regularly hit 150–200 guests under one tent — 20×60 or 30×60 marquees are routine. The ceremony is in the rows or on the lawn (chairs-only, no marquee — vine canopy provides the cover); the marquee is at the reception lawn or barn-adjacent flat area.

Install windows work around the venue's harvesting and tasting-room schedule. We don't drive across irrigation lines or stage equipment in active vineyard rows — we walk the site with the venue's events team during quoting to identify the staging path.

Engineered ground stakes are the default anchor on vineyard lawn. Where irrigation infrastructure is dense, we pair stakes with ballast or switch to ballast-only.

## Krause Berry Farms and agritourism

Krause Berry Farms agritourism weddings have their own staging rhythm — the farm runs as a working business through the wedding season, and our delivery window coordinates with their farm operations and tasting-room hours. Berry-farm working soil is firm in summer (good for stakes), softer in shoulder seasons.

Most Krause weddings are 100–150 guest configurations with a 20×60 or 30×40 marquee on the lawn-side of the property. Bistro lighting along the ridge is standard; the marquee opens onto the farm-store and tasting-area zones during cocktail hour.

## Campbell Valley Regional Park

Campbell Valley's ceremony zones run on park-permit windows with strict setback rules. Most ceremony zones accept 20×20 or 20×30 footprints; full reception marquees rarely fit within the permitted setbacks. The standard pattern: smaller ceremony marquee at the park, reception marquee at the adjacent reception venue (a Township acreage, a Brookswood backyard, or a covered indoor venue).

## Langley Events Centre — supplemental and corporate

Langley Events Centre's outdoor zones occasionally book supplemental marquees for cocktail-hour cover, registration overflow, or sponsor activations adjacent to corporate galas. 20×20 to 20×40 footprints are the standard. Hardscape installs use full ballast; lawn sections use stakes plus supplemental ballast.

## Willoughby, Walnut Grove, Brookswood, Murrayville — residential

Standard residential backyard pattern across the Township's residential subregions. Willoughby and Walnut Grove's newer developments offer wider lot frontage and accept 20×60 marquees comfortably. Brookswood's mature-tree backyards constrain marquee placement — we measure overhead-branch clearance during the pre-quote walk and may cap at 20×40 where the canopy is dense. Murrayville mixes both patterns.

## Open-valley wind and sidewall strategy

Township mid-afternoon thermal patterns produce gust loads that flat urban ground doesn't. Open-field installs (vineyard rows, berry-farm fields, acreage lawns, larger backyards) get sidewalls year-round — not optional, not "if it rains." Marquee size goes one tier above the strict headcount requirement on exposed Township sites to absorb gusts.
