## Lafarge Lake, Westwood Plateau, Burke Mountain — Coquitlam chair patterns

### Lafarge Lake and Town Centre civic ceremonies

Lafarge Lake's stage and lakeside lawn host most of Coquitlam's civic weddings and small-to-medium corporate gatherings. Resin Garden is the standard for the lakeside ceremony zone (damp grass, lake dew); Chiavari for indoor venues at Town Centre. We've worked the Town Centre Park calendar enough to know the venue-coordinated delivery windows around their programmed events.

### Westwood Plateau Golf — upscale weddings

Westwood Plateau's clubhouse and ceremony lawn are among Coquitlam's most-booked upscale wedding venues. Cart-path access for ceremony-lawn deliveries; standard freight access at the clubhouse. Most Westwood weddings book Chiavari for the formal aesthetic, with Resin Garden as a backup for shoulder-season ceremonies when the lawn is damp.

### Burke Mountain — hillside backyards and ceremonies

Burke Mountain weddings are typically smaller (50–100 guests) and held at private acreage residences along the upper slopes. Standard delivery truck handles the newer streets; the older switchbacks at the very top occasionally need smaller-truck staging. We confirm the route during quoting.

### Minnekhada and forest ceremonies

Minnekhada Regional Park's forest ceremony zones run on permits with strict setback rules. Resin Garden is the only pick for the forest floor — Chiavari's lacquered finish doesn't tolerate damp duff and uneven ground. Truck access is at the parking lot; we hand-carry chairs the final stretch when the ceremony zone is past the trailhead.

### Maillardville and Eagle Ridge — heritage backyards

Maillardville's heritage-residential streets are tight (street parking only, narrow side passages), but most lots accept any chair configuration. Eagle Ridge runs newer detached-home backyards with standard delivery patterns.

### Per-item pricing

The same on Burke Mountain as in Maillardville. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — hillside access, after-hours timing, distance — never the per-chair rate.
