*A Chiavari ready to load — individually wiped, inspected, stacked.*

Three chair lines cover almost every Chilliwack event:

- **[White Chiavari](/product-white-chiavari-chair) ($8.50/day)** — the wedding-reception standard. Lacquered hardwood frame, ivory cushion, stacks 8 high. Best for Sardis and Vedder Crossing indoor receptions, Promontory acreage receptions under marquee, Heritage Park indoor venues, and any Chilliwack booking where the photos prioritize formal seating.
- **[Fanback folding](/product-fanback-garden-chair) ($3.25/day)** — the workhorse. Steel frame, folds flat, stacks compactly. Right for Chilliwack Heritage Park large-venue events, school events at Chilliwack campuses, community-scale corporate gatherings, and any high-headcount booking.
- **[Resin Garden](/product-resin-garden-chair) ($5.50/day)** — the outdoor-resilient pick. Right for Cultus Lake shoreline ceremonies, Bridal Veil Falls Provincial Park ceremonies, Mount Cheam-view acreage ceremonies on damp grass, and any Chilliwack booking where the morning surface is dew-prone.

## Cultus Lake, Heritage Park, Rosedale acreage — Chilliwack chair patterns

![Rows of white ceremony chairs at an outdoor wedding setup](/images/lifestyle/ceremony-chairs-3.jpg){.float-right}
*Ceremony chairs staged at a Chilliwack acreage.*

### Cultus Lake shoreline and beach

Cultus Lake's beach and shoreline ceremony zones are the most-photographed Chilliwack outdoor wedding sites. Resin Garden every time — the lakefront grass and beach sand don't reward Chiavari. Most Cultus weddings flip the reception to an indoor venue or a marquee on the adjacent acreage; Chiavari for that phase.

### Chilliwack Heritage Park — community scale

Chilliwack Heritage Park hosts community gatherings, larger corporate events, and the occasional 200+ guest wedding. Fanback folding ships in volume; Chiavari for head-table and feature seating. Loading-dock access at the Park is ample, but the 90-minute delivery means we install the day before for any Saturday event.

### Promontory, Rosedale, Hatzic Valley — acreage and view weddings

Eastern Fraser Valley acreages with Mount Cheam views drive the upscale Chilliwack wedding calendar. Most are 100–150 guests with two-chair-line configurations. Truck access is rural-road and ample.

### Sardis and Vedder Crossing — indoor and restaurant

Sardis restaurant weddings and Vedder Crossing community-venue receptions skew Chiavari-only. Most are 60–100 guests with indoor seating that doesn't see the outdoor variable.

### Bridal Veil Falls and provincial-park ceremonies

Bridal Veil Falls Provincial Park's ceremony zones run on permits with strict setback rules. Resin Garden for the spray-adjacent zones. Hand-carry delivery from the parking lot.

### Per-item pricing

The same on Cultus Lake as in Sardis. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop — the Highway 1 corridor delivery is the line item that changes, not the per-chair rate.
