## What our White Rock table lineup covers

![A formal round dinner table with full place settings, candles, and a floral centerpiece](/images/lifestyle/round-table-dinner-1.jpg)
*A 5ft round set for 8 — the White Rock peninsula wedding-reception standard.*

Three tables, three jobs. White Rock event floor plans usually need all three:

- **[5ft round tables](/product-round-table-5ft) ($13.50/day, seats 8)** — the classic wedding-reception configuration. Eight guests, room for centerpieces, fits two-deep into most marquee footprints (a 20×40 holds 6 rounds; a 20×60 holds 12). Standard for Semiahmoo peninsula marquee receptions.
- **[6ft rectangular banquet tables](/product-banquet-table-6ft) ($10.95/day, seats 6–8)** — the single most versatile table we ship. Family-style dining rows, buffet stations, gift tables, DJ booths, bar setups. Under 75 lb so they re-stage quickly mid-event. Heavier base than highboys — the right pick for exposed beach setups when seated dinner is part of the program.
- **[Cocktail highboy tables](/product-cocktail-table) ($15.50/day, standing)** — 42-inch standing-height rounds for cocktail hour, networking events, and pre-dinner mingling. Plan ~1 highboy per 10–12 standing guests. Standard for Marine Drive restaurant pre-dinner staging.

> Tables care most about access and floor plan — we don't add a per-table setup charge when delivery is on the quote.

## White Rock venue access and floor-plan staging

![A row of cocktail-hour highboy tables at an outdoor reception with guests in conversation behind](/images/lifestyle/cocktail-hour-1.jpg){.float-right}
*Cocktail highboys staged for a Marine Drive event.*

### White Rock Pier and Marine Drive — wind-aware

Tight permit windows, exposed wind. Banquet bases over highboy bistros where the site is fully exposed; cocktail highboys staged under tent canopy or weighted with sandbags. We don't recommend full seated-dinner table programs on the open beach.

### West Beach and East Beach — cocktail hour

Beach-sand setups skew toward highboy-and-banquet cocktail-hour configurations rather than full seated dinner. Sandbagged bases on any exposed setup.

### Semiahmoo Park and peninsula acreage

Full table mix — 12+ rounds, 6–8 banquets, 3–4 highboys — common for 100–150-guest peninsula weddings. Rural-road truck access; we pre-stage near the marquee for longer driveways.

### Marine Drive restaurants — cocktail hour staging

Most Marine Drive restaurant weddings have the seated-dinner tables provided by the venue; our delivery is typically cocktail-hour highboys staged outside the entrance. Curbside delivery on Marine Drive is coordinated with city event scheduling.

### Uptown, Centennial Park area, Hillside backyards

Smaller weddings (50–100 guests) with simpler banquet runs. Hillside delivery is straightforward.

### Per-item pricing

The same on the pier as on the peninsula. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — never the per-table rate.

## Mid-event flips and ceremony-to-reception staging

Many White Rock peninsula weddings run a single-tent ceremony-to-reception flip — same chairs, same marquee, with our crew rearranging the table layout in 30–45 minutes during the cocktail-hour window. Banquet tables fold flat and re-position quickly; rounds re-stack to the truck while the dinner configuration goes up. This is included on single-tent bookings, not an add-on line.

## A 100-guest White Rock table rental order

For a 100-guest White Rock wedding with a Semiahmoo peninsula marquee reception and a separate cocktail-hour space, a typical table-side order looks like:

- 12 × **5ft round tables** (8 guests per round = 96, with 2 extras for kids and overflow)
- 6 × **6ft banquet tables** (head linking 2, gift, cake, DJ, registration)
- 2 × **6ft banquet tables** for the buffet line
- 3 × **cocktail highboys** for the pre-dinner cocktail hour

Total: 23 tables, all positioned to your floor plan during a single delivery window. For beach-and-Marine-Drive cocktail-hour-into-restaurant configurations, the order shifts to highboy-heavy.
