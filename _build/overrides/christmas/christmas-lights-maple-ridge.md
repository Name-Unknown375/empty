## What our Maple Ridge Christmas crew brings to your roofline

We're a small enough team that the person who quotes your install is on the roof during install day. We're large enough that we own every part of the service — design, install, maintenance, takedown, storage — and don't sub-contract.

On the truck for a typical Maple Ridge install:

- **Commercial-grade LED strands** — 5+ season durability, rated for Fraser Valley wind exposure
- **Warm white C9 LED roofline** (the most-booked Maple Ridge aesthetic)
- **Mini-light tree and pathway accents** for sheltered acreage yards
- **Outbuilding and gate-area accent strands** for multi-structure rural properties
- **Forest-canopy clearance kit** for Whonnock and Albion forested rural-residential
- **WCB coverage and roof certification** for two- and three-storey homes
- **Acreage-scale install crew** — 250–400 linear foot installs are routine

> Acreage installs aren't urban installs. Bigger scope, longer crew time, more linear footage — and the per-foot rate stays the same.

## Maple Ridge install patterns

### Hammond and Silver Valley acreages

Single-family rural-residential properties with extended rooflines, multiple outbuildings, and significant front-of-house landscape. Most acreages need 250–400 linear feet of roofline plus tree, gate-area, and pathway accents. Pricing $9–$12/ft for typical Premium-package installs.

### Albion and Whonnock rural-residential

Properties with mature trees, longer driveway approaches, and forest-canopy clearance variables. We measure overhead-branch clearance during the design walk; ladder-access constraints sometimes push us to schedule install windows earlier in November before deciduous canopy thickens with weather.

### Kanaka Creek-adjacent

Rural-residential properties near Kanaka Creek Regional Park. Often combine residential rooflines with naturalized landscape — minimal tree wraps, focus on roofline lighting and front-yard pathway accents.

### Maple Ridge town centre and Haney residential

More urban-character residential closer to the town centre. Most run the Starter or Classic package — warm-white C9 along the main roofline with optional tree accents. $8.50–$10/ft pricing.

## Free off-season storage and the year-2 math

Year one, you pay for design, install, lights, takedown, and storage as a fixed package. For acreage installs especially, year-2 pricing drops 20–30% because the linear-footage materials are the biggest year-one cost and they're already paid for.

This is why most Maple Ridge acreage properties book us multi-year. The math compounds.
