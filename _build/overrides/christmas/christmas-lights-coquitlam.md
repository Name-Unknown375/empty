## Coquitlam install patterns

### Burke Mountain and Westwood Plateau

Higher-elevation residential with elevation-driven afternoon wind. Our crews use sustained-wind-rated install hardware and commercial-grade LED strands rather than delicate mini-lights on exposed rooflines. November install windows narrow earlier here than in lower Coquitlam — we book mountain installs first when peak season arrives. Most run the Premium package given the property scale.

### Town Centre commercial and civic

Lafarge Lake-area commercial buildings, Town Centre Park-adjacent retail, civic and community-centre exteriors. Commercial installs need building or City of Coquitlam coordination on access windows; we book the slot and confirm permits during quoting. Most run on warm-white C9 or alternating-pattern designs.

### Maillardville and Eagle Ridge established residential

Older homes with detailed trim and complex rooflines. Most run the Classic package — warm-white C9 along the rooflines plus accent strands on porches, columns, and front-yard trees. Maillardville's heritage character (some properties have French-village architecture details) responds well to clip-style installs (no staples, no nails).

### Town Centre condo balconies

Town Centre high-rise condos increasingly book balcony installs — string lights along the railing, mini-lights along the planter line. We coordinate freight-elevator access with building management; install non-damaging mounting (no drilling).

## Free off-season storage and the year-2 math

Year one, you pay for design, install, lights, takedown, and storage as a fixed package. Year two, the math gets meaningfully better — your strands are already in our warehouse. For Burke Mountain installs especially, the sustained-wind-rated commercial-grade LEDs are a significant year-one cost; year-2 pricing typically drops 20–30% because those materials are already paid for.
