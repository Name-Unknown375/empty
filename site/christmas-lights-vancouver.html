What our Vancouver Christmas crew brings to your roofline

We’re a small enough team that the person who quotes your install is on the roof during install day. We’re large enough that we own every part of the service — design, install, maintenance, takedown, storage — and don’t sub-contract.

On the truck for a typical Vancouver install:

  • Commercial-grade LED strands — 5+ season durability, not big-box consumer lights
  • Warm white C9 LED roofline (the standard Vancouver heritage aesthetic)
  • Colored or warm-white mini-light options for trees, bushes, and pathway accents
  • Clip-style install hardware — no staples, no nails, no trim damage
  • WCB coverage and roof certification for our crew on two- and three-storey homes
  • Portable power assessments during the design walk if existing outlets won’t carry the load

One quote, one crew, one warehouse. Same hands install in November and take down in January.

Why Vancouver homes book the install differently than other cities

Heritage West Side and Shaughnessy

Older homes with detailed trim, wrap-around porches, and complex rooflines — they read best with warm-white C9 along the roofline plus accent strands on porch columns and fascia. Most West Side installs run the Classic or Premium package given the linear footage involved.

Downtown and Yaletown commercial

Storefront facades, restaurant patios, hotel exteriors. Commercial installs need building-management coordination on access windows; we book the slot and confirm permits with the building during quoting. Most commercial installs run on warm-white C9 or alternating-pattern installs depending on brand aesthetic.

Kitsilano and West End residential

Single-family detached homes with the standard residential install pattern. Lots accept the full Classic package (~150 linear feet of roofline plus tree/bush accents). Pricing lands in the $8.50–$10 per linear foot range for most Kitsilano installs.

Mount Pleasant and East Vancouver

Modern homes with simpler rooflines. Often the Starter package fits — clean warm-white C9 along the main roofline, no tree or bush accent. $8.50/ft pricing for most Starter installs.

Free off-season storage and the year-2 math

The first season you book with us, you pay for the design, install, lights, takedown, and storage as a fixed package. Year two, the math gets meaningfully better — your strands are already in our warehouse, labeled with your name and your roof’s section map. The install crew arrives on day one with the same lights, the same plan, and the same install pattern. No buy-new cost. No design re-walk unless your house has changed. Most Vancouver clients see year-2 pricing 15–25% lower than year-1 because the materials are already paid for.

This is the reason most Christmas-light installers in Vancouver lose customers after one season. We don’t.

A typical Vancouver residential install

For a two-storey Vancouver home with rooflines, two trees in the front yard, and a pathway from the sidewalk to the door, a typical Classic package install looks like:

  • ~150 linear feet of warm-white C9 LED along the main and gable rooflines
  • ~80 linear feet of mini-lights wrapping the two front-yard trees

Setup runs 4–6 hours depending on roof complexity; takedown 2–3 hours. Both quoted up front. The Premium package adds bush wraps, larger architectural accents, and timer integration.

Vancouver Christmas Light FAQ

How much does Christmas light installation cost in Vancouver?
Our Vancouver installations run $8.50–$12 per linear foot, depending on the package (Starter, Classic, or Premium). The price covers commercial-grade LED strands, the on-site design walk-through, install, season-long maintenance, takedown, and free off-season storage in our climate-controlled warehouse. Fixed-price quote within 24 hours of your free consultation.
Do you install lights on heritage West Side homes?
Yes — heritage homes are some of our favourite Vancouver installs. We work clip-style installs (no staples, no nails) that respect the trim and don't damage character properties. Our crews are certified on two- and three-storey rooflines and carry full WCB coverage. Most heritage installs run the Classic or Premium package.
When do you install in Vancouver — and when's the deadline to book?
Vancouver installs run mid-October through early December, with takedown happening between January 5 and 31. Bookings for the upcoming season open in August; the comfortable booking window is September–early October. Last-minute December installs are sometimes possible but rarely for premium packages — call us early.
What happens to my lights after the season?
We come back, take it all down, label your strands by section, and store them in our climate-controlled warehouse until next October. There's no storage fee — it's part of the package. Year two onward, you skip the buy-new step entirely; we just bring back what's already labeled with your name on it.
What if a strand fails during the season?
We're on-site within 24 to 48 hours to replace it — no charge. Most installers make you wait until spring or charge an emergency-service fee. We don't. Same-day fix is sometimes possible during business hours; call 778-990-7983.
Can you do commercial Christmas light installations downtown?
Yes — downtown Vancouver commercial installs (storefront facades, restaurant patios, hotel exteriors, condo strata common areas) are routine bookings. Commercial installs typically need building-management coordination on access windows; we book the install slot and confirm permits with the building during quoting.
