How It Works

Your Christmas Light Install — Step by Step

From first consultation to January takedown, we handle every step. You never touch a light.

1

Free Design Consultation

We visit your property, walk the rooflines, talk about style preferences (warm-white vs color, classic vs modern), and take measurements for power planning.

2

Custom Design & Fixed-Price Quote

We send a fixed-price package quote with a written install plan. For Premium clients we include a rendered 3D mockup so you can preview the finished look.

3

Professional Installation

WCB-covered crews arrive in October or early November, install in a single visit, and leave your property cleaner than we found it. Most homes are complete in 2–4 hours.

4

Season-Long Maintenance

If a strand fails mid-December, we're on-site within 24–48 hours to replace it — no charge. We also do a proactive mid-season check for Premium and Classic packages.

5

Takedown & Free Storage

We return in January (or earlier if you prefer) for a clean takedown. Your lights are labeled, packed, and stored in our climate-controlled warehouse at no extra cost — ready for next November.

FAQ

Christmas Light Installation Questions

How does your Christmas light service work from start to finish?

Five steps: (1) You book a free design consultation. (2) We visit, measure, and send a fixed-price quote with a written install plan. (3) Our WCB-covered crew installs in October or November. (4) We maintain the display all season — 24-to-48-hour response if a strand fails. (5) We take everything down in January and store your lights for free in our warehouse until next November.

What sets Forever apart from other Christmas light installers in the Lower Mainland?

Three things: fixed-price packages (no hourly surprises, no separate storage fee), commercial-grade LEDs that last 5+ seasons (vs. the 1–2 seasons typical of big-box lights), and our same-day/48-hour fix guarantee if anything fails during December. We're also backed by 190+ five-star Google reviews from our party rental side — a track record most specialist installers can't match.

Do you service residential and commercial properties?

Both. We install for private homes (single-story to 3-story), strata complexes and townhouse rows, storefronts and office towers, retirement residences, and community centres. Commercial and strata clients make up roughly 20% of our annual installs. Commercial installs can be scheduled for after-hours or overnight windows to avoid disrupting tenants.

What's the difference between your Starter, Classic, and Premium packages?

Starter is a single-story roofline with warm-white C9 LEDs — clean, classic, efficient. Classic adds one accent (tree wrap, bushes, or pathway) and works well for two-story homes. Premium is the full display — roofline + trees + bushes + pathway + door frame, with a 3D mockup before install, priority 24-hour maintenance, and smart Wi-Fi controls included. All packages include install, maintenance, takedown, and free storage.

Can I bundle Christmas lights with my corporate holiday party rentals?

Yes — we're one of the only providers in the Lower Mainland who can handle both the outdoor lighting and the indoor event rentals (tents, tables, Chiavari chairs, dance floors) on a single quote and a single invoice. For corporate clients we've installed storefront lighting while also delivering the tent and tables for the company's holiday party. Ask about a bundled package at your design consultation.

What areas of the Lower Mainland do you serve?

Our Christmas light installation service covers 14 cities across Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley: Surrey, Langley, Vancouver, Burnaby (our Tier 1 cities), plus Abbotsford, Coquitlam, Delta, Langley Township, Maple Ridge, New Westminster, North Vancouver, Port Moody, Richmond, and White Rock. For cities outside this list we offer priority-booking or by-quote service depending on travel distance — contact us and we'll check.
