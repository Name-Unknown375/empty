Surrey’s three install patterns

Cloverdale and South Surrey acreage rooflines

Acreage homes have meaningfully more linear footage than urban rooflines — 200–400 feet is routine, 500+ for the larger Cloverdale properties. Pricing scales linearly: a 300-foot acreage install at $9/ft lands at $2,700 for the lights alone, plus $300–$600 for trees and pathway accents. We confirm gate codes, livestock considerations, and overhead utility lines before the install crew dispatches. Most acreages run the Premium package given the scale.

King George Boulevard commercial corridor

Storefront facades, restaurant exteriors, plaza entrances along the King George corridor and Scott Road. Commercial installs typically need after-5pm or weekend install timing; we coordinate access with the property manager during quoting. Most commercial installs run warm-white C9 or alternating-pattern designs.

Newton, Fleetwood, Guildford residential

Detached single-family homes with the standard residential install pattern. Most lots accept the Starter or Classic package — warm-white C9 along the main roofline with optional tree and bush accents. $8.50–$10/ft pricing for most.

Schools and community-centre exteriors

Surrey’s school district and community centres run a meaningful share of our late-season Christmas installs — community building exteriors, Newton-area community centre entrances, school facades for grad-season events that overlap into December. Lead times are typically shorter (3–4 weeks) than residential procurement.

Free off-season storage and the year-2 math

Year one, you pay for design, install, lights, takedown, and storage as a fixed package. Year two, the math gets meaningfully better — your strands are already in our warehouse in Surrey, labeled with your section map. The install crew arrives on day one with the same lights and the same plan. Most Surrey clients see year-2 pricing 15–25% lower than year-1 because the materials are already paid for.

FAQ

Christmas Light Questions — Surrey

How much does Christmas light installation cost in Surrey?
Our Surrey installations run $8.50–$12 per linear foot. Local pricing is local pricing — same per-foot rate as anywhere else we serve, no Surrey-zone discount, no Surrey-zone uplift. Price covers commercial-grade LED strands, the on-site design walk-through, install, season-long maintenance, takedown, and free off-season storage.
Do you install Christmas lights on Cloverdale or South Surrey acreages?
Yes — Cloverdale, South Surrey, and the rural-residential properties south of 40th Avenue are some of our most-photogenic Surrey installs. Acreage rooflines often run 200–400 linear feet of lighting; pricing scales with the linear footage. We confirm gate codes, livestock considerations, and overhead utility lines before the install crew dispatches.
How fast can you do a Surrey Christmas light install on short notice?
More flexibly than any other city we serve. Surrey is our shortest delivery route, which means same-week residential installs are sometimes feasible inside a 5–7 day window if our installer crew has capacity. Mid-season fixes (a strand fails, you want to add a section) are often on-site within 60–90 minutes during business hours.
Can you do mid-season fixes in Surrey?
Yes — and this is where being local matters. If a strand fails, lights go out on a section, or you suddenly want an extension, we can typically have a crew on-site within 60–90 minutes for any Surrey property during business hours. We don't make this promise for cities further out.
What if I want to add lights mid-season?
Surrey customers add sections mid-season more often than further-out cities. Common requests: an extra tree wrap before a holiday party, pathway lights for a December event, accent strands on a structure we didn't light in November. We add the section, charge by the linear foot, and store it with the rest of your install at season end.
What happens to my lights after the season?
We come back, take it all down, label your strands by section, and store them in our climate-controlled warehouse until next October. No storage fee — it's part of the package. Year two onward, you skip the buy-new step entirely.
