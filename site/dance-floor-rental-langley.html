What our Langley dance floor lineup covers

A Langley vineyard reception in full swing.

Three floor sizes that cover almost every Langley vineyard, acreage, and corporate event:

  • 12×12 portable dance floor — 60–80 guests, smaller acreage backyards, intimate vineyard receptions.
  • 12×16 portable dance floor — the workhorse. 100-guest vineyard weddings, most marquee setups across Brookswood and Murrayville.
  • 16×20 portable dance floor — 150–200 guests (more common at Langley vineyards than urban weddings), larger acreage receptions.
  • Subfloor panels — engineered platforms that level grass, farm soil, and sloped lawns beneath the dance floor. Almost always required for Langley installs.
  • Edge ramps — included on every floor install. Trip-hazard safety, not aesthetic.

Vineyard grass holds more moisture than urban lawns. Subfloor isn’t an upsell here — it’s how the floor stays tight at midnight.

Langley dance floor install patterns we cover

A 16×20 dance floor in use at a Langley vineyard wedding.

Vineyard receptions

Township 7, Krause, Domaine de Chaberton. Subfloor on every install — the lawn near tasting rooms holds enough irrigation moisture that staking subfloor is non-negotiable. We coordinate the install window with the venue’s harvest and tasting-room schedule.

Acreage installs

Brookswood, Murrayville. Mowed grass with the standard Langley afternoon thermal wind. Subfloor levels the surface; we confirm slope during the pre-quote walk for unfamiliar properties.

Langley Events Centre corporate

Indoor floors at LEC accept the dance floor directly with edge ramps only — no subfloor. Setup is faster (45–60 minutes for a 12×16) than vineyard installs.

Per-item pricing

The same on a Township 7 lawn as in Brookswood. Our 12×16 portable dance floor is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery and subfloor — surface, slope, multi-venue — never the per-floor rate.

High-volume vineyard floors and 200-guest weddings

Langley vineyard weddings hit 200 guests more often than urban Vancouver weddings, and the dance floor scales accordingly — 16×20 with full subfloor on the marquee-adjacent lawn. We carry the volume in stock, and the install runs 2.5–3 hours total (subfloor + floor + ramps) with the crew sized for the larger order.

A 100-guest Langley dance floor rental order

For a 100-guest Langley vineyard wedding at Township 7 with marquee reception, a typical dance floor order looks like:

  • 1 × 12×16 portable dance floor (the standard for 100 guests)
  • Subfloor panels (level the vineyard lawn beneath the floor)
  • Edge ramps on all four sides
  • Crew install (90–120 minutes for floor + subfloor)

For 200-guest vineyard weddings, the floor scales to 16×20 with corresponding subfloor. The 50/100/150/200 rental checklist maps the dance floor sizing alongside the rest of the rental list.

FAQ

Dance Floor Rentals Questions — Langley

What's included on a Langley dance floor rental quote?
Every Langley dance floor quote we send is itemised: panel count, subfloor (almost always required on Langley grass), edge ramps, delivery, professional crew install, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Fraser Valley uplifts, no surprises at the truck.
What size dance floor for a 100-guest Langley vineyard wedding?
12×16 is the standard for 100 guests — about 30–40% will be on the floor at any moment. For 60–80 guests, 12×12. For 150–200 guests (more common at Langley vineyards than urban Vancouver weddings), 16×20.
Do you install dance floors on vineyard lawn or farm soil?
Yes — vineyard and farm-soil installs always include engineered subfloor panels under the dance floor. The soil holds enough moisture and unevenness that subfloor is non-negotiable for a tight, gap-free surface. Subfloor adds 90–120 minutes to setup and gets quoted as a separate line.
Can you install a dance floor on a Brookswood or Murrayville acreage?
Yes — acreage installs are some of our most-frequent Langley dance floor bookings. Most properties have plenty of footprint for a 16×20 floor under marquee. We confirm slope and surface during the pre-quote walk; severely sloped lawns may need raised subfloor on one side to level the floor.
How fast can you deliver a Langley dance floor?
Langley is one of our shortest delivery routes, which means same-week 12×12 and 12×16 orders are usually feasible. Larger 16×20+ floors with full subfloor still need 7+ days for crew sizing and panel staging.
