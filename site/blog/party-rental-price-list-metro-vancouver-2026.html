01The short answer

Here is what party rentals cost in Metro Vancouver in 2026: chairs run $3.25 to $8.50 per day, tables $10.95 to $15.50 per day, tents $75 (a 10×10 popup) to $1,890 (our 30×60 marquee), the checkered dance floor is $800 per event, and silent battery power starts at $110 per weekend. Those aren't "starting at" teasers — they're the same numbers sitting in our booking system today. Twenty-two years of quoting has convinced us that a published price sheet saves everyone a week of email.

One thing before you compare us to anyone else: read the units. We price three ways. Chairs, tables, popup tents, heaters, and lights are per day. Marquee tents and the dance floor are per event — one price covers install, your event, and takedown, even when that spans a full weekend. Power stations and Starlink are priced per 2-day weekend. And if you bundle a tent, tables, and chairs into one of our event packages, the bundle prices at 10% below the à-la-carte totals on this page.

Key Takeaways

If you only read this section

  • Cheapest seat in the fleet is $3.25/day (fanback folding); the reception-standard Chiavari is $8.50/day.
  • Marquee tents run $550–$1,890 per event, solid sidewalls included — not per day.
  • Most price-sheet confusion is units. Check whether a number is /day, /event, or /weekend before comparing vendors.
  • Packages knock 10% off the à-la-carte total for the same gear.
  • No price on this page includes delivery — that's quoted separately by postal code and order size.

08What's not on this sheet

A price list that pretends to be the whole invoice is lying to you. Four things sit outside the numbers above:

  • Delivery and setup. Quoted separately, calculated from your postal code and order size — a popup tent to Cloverdale and a 30×60 build in North Vancouver are not the same job, so we don't pretend one flat fee covers both. Pickup from our Surrey warehouse is available on many items at no charge.
  • Damage waiver. Industry standard is 7–10% of the rental subtotal; ours is 8%, added to the invoice unless you opt out in writing — waive it and the replacement cost of damaged gear is on you.
  • Insurance certificates. If your venue requires a Public Liability Insurance certificate naming them, budget $75–$150. Most park venues ask for one.
  • Linens and taxes. Linens are coordinated through our partners and quoted with your order; GST/PST apply to everything.

When you compare vendors, compare the out-the-door number — a cheaper headline price with a vague delivery line usually isn't cheaper.

10Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent chairs in Vancouver?
Fanback folding chairs are $3.25 per chair per day, resin garden chairs are $5.50, and white Chiavari chairs are $8.50. For 100 guests that's $325 in fanbacks or $850 in Chiavaris. Delivery is quoted separately based on your postal code and order size.

What does a 20×40 tent cost to rent in Metro Vancouver?
Our 20×40 marquee is $1,100 per event — one price that covers install, your event, and takedown, with solid sidewalls included. It seats 72–80 guests at 5ft round tables and fits a 12×12 dance floor. Delivery and setup are quoted separately by location.

How much does a dance floor rental cost?
Our black-and-white checkered dance floor is $800 per event, in sizes from 12×12 up to 20×20, with the levelling subfloor included — it installs over grass, carpet, or pavement. Plan roughly 4.5 square feet per active dancer.

Are delivery and damage waiver included in these prices?
No. Delivery is quoted separately based on your postal code and order size. The damage waiver runs 7–10% of the rental subtotal (ours is 8%, opt-out in writing), and if your venue requires a Public Liability Insurance certificate, budget $75–$150.
