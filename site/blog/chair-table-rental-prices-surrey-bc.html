If you're shopping chair and table rentals in Surrey, you've probably seen prices ranging from $1.25 to $9.50 per chair and $8 to $18 per table — and you've probably wondered which numbers reflect reality. 02Chiavari vs. fanback vs. resin garden — what to pick

Three chair categories dominate Surrey weddings. The right pick depends on your photography priority, formality, and budget.

  • Resin garden / fanback chairs — the BC outdoor-wedding workhorse. White, lightweight, elegant enough for ceremonies and receptions, weatherproof. Our fanback garden chair is our most-rented chair across Surrey, Langley, and the Fraser Valley.
  • Chiavari chairs — the formal upgrade. Solid wood (or aluminum) frame, padded seat cushion, gold/silver/white finish options. The right call for weddings where chairs feature prominently in photos. Our white Chiavari with cushion is the most popular Chiavari finish in BC.
  • Folding chairs — the budget option. Functional, not photogenic. Right for casual events, kids' parties, corporate setups where the chairs aren't the focal point.
  • Bistro/patio chairs — for cocktail-style or lounge-style setups. Less common at full receptions.

03Banquet vs. round vs. cocktail — what each is for

Tables divide into three families based on event style:

  • Round tables (48", 60", 72") — wedding-default. 60" rounds seat 8 comfortably; 72" rounds seat 10. Better sight lines for toasts than long banquets.
  • Banquet tables (6ft, 8ft) — long rectangles. 6ft seats 6, 8ft seats 8. The right call for family-style dining, modern long-table receptions, and any event where the head-table look is important.
  • Cocktail tables — high-tops for standing cocktail hour. 30" diameter, 42" tall. Plan one cocktail table per 8–10 guests at standing receptions.

Most Lower Mainland weddings book 5ft (60") round tables for the seated reception plus 1–3 banquet tables for the head table, gift table, and bar. See our rental checklist by guest count for the standard table mix at common headcounts.

03bHarvest, farmhouse, and specialty tables

The rustic-farmhouse aesthetic has been the dominant BC wedding style since 2022, and harvest tables — solid wood, often cross-legged or X-base, 8ft long — are the centrepiece. They're specialty inventory in the Lower Mainland; not every rental company stocks them.

  • Standard harvest tables (8ft pine or fir, X-base) — $65–$95 per table per day. Seats 8.
  • Distressed/whitewashed harvest tables — $80–$110 per day. Same dimensions, different finish.
  • Cross-back wooden chairs (paired with harvest tables) — $5.50–$7.50 per chair, between Chiavari and resin pricing.
  • Farm-style benches — $40–$60 per bench. Each seats 4 (replaces 4 chairs at half the line-item cost, but less comfortable for a 4-hour reception).

Harvest setups skip linens (the wood is the look) — saving $10–$15 per table on linen rental. The trade-off is you can't hide setup imperfections under a tablecloth, so the surface needs to be ready for photos.

04Delivery and pickup fees in Surrey

Delivery is rarely included in the per-chair price. Standard structure across Surrey-area companies:

  • Customer pickup from warehouse: free (most companies). You're responsible for transport, loading, and return.
  • Delivery within Surrey + adjacent (Langley, White Rock, Delta, Cloverdale): $45–$95 round trip for orders under $500; often included on orders over $1,000.
  • Delivery to extended zone (Vancouver, Richmond, Burnaby): $95–$175 round trip.
  • Stairs, narrow access, complex sites: labour surcharge $25–$95 added.
  • After-hours pickup: $250+ surcharge.

05Bundling chairs with tables (and linens) to save

Almost every Surrey-area company offers package discounts when you book chairs, tables, and linens together — typically 5–15% off the line-item subtotal. The math becomes important at 100+ guests. A typical 100-guest wedding chair-table-linen package vs. á la carte:

  • 100 fanback chairs á la carte: $475
  • 13 × 60" rounds á la carte: $182
  • 13 white linens (60" round size) á la carte: $156
  • Á la carte total: $813
  • Bundled package: $695 (~$118 savings, ~14%)

If you're booking from one company anyway, ask explicitly for the bundled price. Most quotes default to á la carte unless you ask.

FAQFrequently asked questions

How much do Chiavari chairs cost to rent in Surrey?
Chiavari chairs with cushion run $7.00–$9.50 per chair across Surrey-area rental companies, with weekend rates of $8.50–$11.00. Gold and silver Chiavari finishes typically sit at the higher end of that range. Quantity discounts kick in at 50+ chairs.

What's the cheapest chair to rent for a wedding?
Folding chairs ($1.25–$1.95 per chair) are the budget option. They're functional but not photogenic — the right call for casual events or when chairs aren't the focal point. For weddings, fanback or resin garden chairs ($3.50–$4.95) are the budget-conscious sweet spot.

Do you deliver chairs and tables to Vancouver from Surrey?
Yes — Vancouver is in our extended delivery zone. Surcharge is typically $95–$175 round trip on top of the rental subtotal. Deliveries to Burnaby, Richmond, and the North Shore fall in the same zone. See the Surrey delivery zones section above for full breakdown.

How much does a 60-inch round table cost to rent?
$10–$14 per table per day, $11–$15 for a 3-day weekend rental. 60" rounds seat 8 comfortably and are the wedding-default size. 48" rounds are cheaper ($8–$11) and seat 6; 72" rounds are pricier ($13–$17) and seat 10.

Are harvest or farmhouse-style tables available in BC?
Yes, though specialty inventory. Farmhouse harvest tables (8ft solid-wood, cross-leg) typically run $65–$95 per table per day in the Lower Mainland — significantly more than standard banquet tables. Inventory is limited; book 2+ months ahead for summer dates.

How many chairs should I rent for 100 guests?
110 chairs — always 10% over your guest count to cover breakage, ceremony-flip flexibility, and late additions. For larger weddings (150+) bump to 15% extra. Chairs are the one rental category we never recommend cutting close to actual guest count.
