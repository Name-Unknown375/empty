Battery & Power Station Rentals Questions — Maple Ridge

Can I run a tent heater off an EcoFlow at a Maple Ridge event?
A 1500 W ceramic heater pulls roughly 1500 Wh per hour — a Delta 3 Max (2048 Wh) gives you about 75 minutes of heat, and a Delta 3 Ultra (3072 Wh) about 2 hours. For all-evening heat in Maple Ridge we recommend our propane tent heaters; for short cocktail-hour warming or AV+light loads, the EcoFlows are silent and indoor-safe.
Is this safe to run inside a marquee tent or indoor venue in Maple Ridge?
Yes — EcoFlow power stations have zero emissions, near-silent operation (~30 dB in standby), and no fuel storage. They're rated for indoor use and we routinely deploy them inside enclosed marquees, banquet halls, and historic Maple Ridge venues that ban gas generators.
How long can I keep the rental for a Maple Ridge multi-day event?
Every battery has two rate tiers: a 2-day weekend rate (Friday delivery, Sunday pickup) and a weekly rate (5–7 day window) for festivals, week-long activations, or production schedules. Both include delivery, on-site walkthrough, and pickup in Maple Ridge.
Do I need the 220 W solar panel for a one-day event?
Usually no — a fully charged Delta 3 Max or Ultra will power most one-day Maple Ridge events on its own. The solar panel earns its keep on multi-day activations (festivals, week-long pop-ups) where you can't get to a wall outlet between days.
Can the Delta 3 Ultra really power a whole reception in Maple Ridge?
For lighting, AV, DJ, and small kitchen appliances — yes. With extra batteries it scales to 11 kWh, which covers a typical 5-hour reception with bistro lights, a sound system, and a coffee/dessert bar. For HVAC or large catering kitchens we still recommend grid power; we'll spec the right unit when you book.
Do you deliver party rentals to Maple Ridge?
Yes — we deliver and set up throughout Maple Ridge, including Hammond, Silver Valley, and Albion. Standard Maple Ridge delivery runs just 30-40 minutes. Delivery and setup are available as add-on services — we'll quote them up front with no surprise fees at the venue.
Have you worked at venues in Maple Ridge like Golden Ears Provincial Park?
Yes — we regularly deliver rentals to Golden Ears Provincial Park, Alouette Lake, and Kanaka Creek Regional Park and dozens of private residences across Maple Ridge. We know the setback rules for most public parks in Maple Ridge and can advise on what permits (if any) your venue requires for tents.
