FAQ

Power Station Rental Questions

Are EcoFlow power stations safe to run inside a marquee tent or indoor venue?

Yes — EcoFlow power stations have zero emissions, near-silent operation (~30 dB in standby), and no fuel storage. They're rated for indoor use and we routinely deploy them inside enclosed marquees, banquet halls, and historic venues that ban gas generators.

Which EcoFlow Delta model do I need for my event?

Delta 2 (1024 Wh, 1800 W AC) handles a DJ booth, lighting, and phone charging at a backyard wedding. Delta 3 Max (2048 Wh, 3000 W AC) is the event workhorse — espresso bars, food-truck POS, shoulder-season heaters. Delta 3 Ultra (3072 Wh, 3600 W AC, expandable to 11 kWh) carries a full reception's lighting + AV. We'll spec the right unit when you book.

What's the rental minimum and how does pricing work?

Two rate tiers per power station: a 2-day weekend rate (the minimum — Friday delivery, Sunday pickup) and a weekly rate (5–7 day window) for festivals, week-long activations, or production schedules. Both include delivery, on-site walkthrough, and pickup throughout Metro Vancouver.

Can I run a tent heater off an EcoFlow?

A 1500 W ceramic heater pulls roughly 1500 Wh per hour — a Delta 3 Max (2048 Wh) gives you about 75 minutes of heat, a Delta 3 Ultra (3072 Wh) about 2 hours. For all-evening heat we recommend our propane tent heaters; for short cocktail-hour warming or AV+light loads, the EcoFlows are silent and indoor-safe.

Do I need the 220 W solar panel for a one-day event?

Usually no — a fully charged Delta 3 Max or Ultra will power most one-day events on its own. The solar panel earns its keep on multi-day activations (festivals, week-long pop-ups) where you can't get to a wall outlet between days.

What's your cancellation policy?

We offer a 125% cancellation guarantee: if we ever cancel on you for any reason, we return 125% of every dollar paid. For client-initiated cancellations, your deposit is held as a credit toward a rescheduled date. See our full terms at checkout.
