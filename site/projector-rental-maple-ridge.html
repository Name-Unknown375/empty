Projector & Screen Rentals Questions — Maple Ridge

Can I use the projector outdoors during the day in Maple Ridge?
At 5000 lumens the ViewSonic LS740HD is one of the brighter event projectors out there, but no projector beats direct sunlight. For Maple Ridge outdoor screenings we recommend starting after dusk, or projecting inside a shaded marquee tent during the day. In a lit indoor room — a conference hall, banquet room, or boardroom — it holds a clear image with the lights on.
What's included with the projector rental?
The base rental is the ViewSonic LS740HD projector plus HDMI cabling. The 100″ screen with floor stand and a projector/laptop stand are available as add-ons — you can add both to the same booking and we'll deliver everything together. Audio isn't included in the kit; most Maple Ridge events run sound from a laptop, a portable speaker, or our other AV gear, and we're happy to advise on the right setup.
Do you set up the projector and screen in Maple Ridge?
Yes. Our crew delivers to your Maple Ridge venue, assembles and positions the 100″ screen and stand, places the projector, and confirms a clean, level image before we leave. We collect everything after your event. If you'd rather pick up and self-install, that's an option too — just let us know when you book.
Can I run the projector off-grid for a backyard movie night?
Absolutely — this is one of our favourite Maple Ridge combos. The projector pairs with our EcoFlow power stations so you can run a full backyard or park movie night with no wall outlet, and with one of our marquee tents you get a rain-proof screening either way. Book the projector, screen, and power station on one order and we deliver it all in a single window.
How far in advance should I book a projector in Maple Ridge?
We carry limited projector and screen stock, so summer weekends and the December corporate-party season book up first. For a Maple Ridge event we recommend reserving 2–3 weeks ahead when you can; that said, it's always worth checking our live availability calendar for last-minute dates.
Do you deliver party rentals to Maple Ridge?
Yes — we deliver and set up throughout Maple Ridge, including Hammond, Silver Valley, and Albion. Standard Maple Ridge delivery runs just 30-40 minutes. Delivery and setup are available as add-on services — we'll quote them up front with no surprise fees at the venue.
Have you worked at venues in Maple Ridge like Golden Ears Provincial Park?
Yes — we regularly deliver rentals to Golden Ears Provincial Park, Alouette Lake, and Kanaka Creek Regional Park and dozens of private residences across Maple Ridge. We know the setback rules for most public parks in Maple Ridge and can advise on what permits (if any) your venue requires for tents.
