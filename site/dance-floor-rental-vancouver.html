What our Vancouver dance floor lineup covers

A reception in full swing under a Vancouver marquee.

Three floor sizes that cover almost every Vancouver wedding and corporate booking:

  • 12×12 portable dance floor — 60–80 guests, smaller backyard receptions, intimate venues. The smallest floor that still feels like a real floor (not a rug).
  • 12×16 portable dance floor — the workhorse. 100-guest weddings, most marquee setups, most rooftop installs. The size that suits a 20×60 marquee best.
  • 16×20 portable dance floor — 150–200 guests, larger acreage and heritage-venue receptions. Edge ramps and full subfloor on grass installations.
  • Subfloor panels — engineered platforms that level grass, sloped lawns, gravel, and uneven patios beneath the dance floor. Adds 90–120 minutes to setup; quoted separately.
  • Edge ramps — included on every floor install where guests step from the surrounding floor onto the dance floor. Trip-hazard safety, not aesthetic.

The floor goes down level. Heels don’t catch panel seams. We don’t ship a floor that bounces under load.

Vancouver dance floor install patterns we cover

A reception dance floor in motion.

Marquee installs on grass

Most Vancouver tent receptions need subfloor under the dance floor — grass settles under heel impact, and uneven turf shows up immediately when the music starts. Subfloor adds 90–120 minutes; we factor it into the quote up front.

Rooftop installs

Yaletown and downtown rooftops accept dance floors with appropriate weight distribution. Panels at <60 lb each fit nearly every freight elevator. Ballast for any tent component goes up; the floor itself sits flat on the rooftop surface.

Heritage venue receptions

Hart House, Anvil Centre, and other heritage venues with existing flooring accept the dance floor directly with edge ramps only — no subfloor. Setup is faster (45–60 minutes for a 12×16) than grass installs.

Per-item pricing

The same in Yaletown as in Kitsilano. Our 12×16 portable dance floor is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery and subfloor — surface, freight elevator, slope — never the per-floor rate.

Subfloor on grass and the level-floor guarantee

Most Vancouver outdoor receptions use grass as the foundation, and most grass surfaces aren’t level enough to support a tight, gap-free dance floor without engineered subfloor underneath. We install subfloor on every grass-installed dance floor we quote — not as an upsell, but as the only way to deliver a floor that holds together under 80–100 dancing guests at midnight. The added setup time gets quoted up front, and the floor we hand over is the one we’d dance on ourselves.

A 100-guest Vancouver dance floor rental order

For a 100-guest Vancouver wedding under marquee on grass, a typical dance floor order looks like:

  • 1 × 12×16 portable dance floor (the standard for 100 guests)
  • Subfloor panels (level the grass beneath the floor)
  • Edge ramps on all four sides
  • Crew install (90–120 minutes for floor + subfloor)

For a heritage-venue reception with existing flooring, the same 12×16 ships without subfloor, and the install drops to 45–60 minutes. For 60–80 guest backyard weddings, swap to a 12×12; for 150–200 guest receptions, a 16×20 with full subfloor on grass.

FAQ

Dance Floor Rentals Questions — Vancouver

What's included on a Vancouver dance floor rental quote?
Every Vancouver dance floor quote we send is itemised: panel count, subfloor where the surface needs it, edge ramps, delivery, professional crew install, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Vancouver-zone uplifts, no surprises at the truck.
What size dance floor for a 100-guest Vancouver wedding?
12×16 is the standard for 100 guests — about 30–40% will be on the floor at any moment, which is the comfort target. For 60–80 guests, 12×12 works. For 150+ guests or weddings with a strong dance culture, 16×20.
Can you install a dance floor on grass or uneven surfaces?
Yes — we install over grass, sloped lawns, gravel, and uneven patios using engineered subfloor panels that level the surface beneath the dance floor. Subfloor adds setup time (90–120 minutes typically) and gets quoted as a separate line. On level pavement or indoor flooring, the dance floor goes down directly with edge ramps only.
Do you install dance floors on Yaletown rooftops?
Yes — rooftop dance floor installs are common Vancouver bookings. We confirm freight-elevator dimensions, weight rating, and rooftop structural capacity before quoting. Panels are <60 lb each so they fit nearly every freight elevator we've seen.
How fast can you deliver a Vancouver dance floor on short notice?
Same-week is usually feasible for 12×12 and 12×16 floors if our trucks aren't already booked. Larger 16×20+ floors with subfloor need 7+ days for crew sizing and panel staging. Call before booking online and we'll tell you straight.
