Where the install pattern changes

A 20×40 with sidewalls open on a mid-bench West Van property.

Ocean-side installs (Ambleside, Dundarave, waterfront homes)

Howe Sound inflow wind shapes everything. Sidewalls year-round. Ballast at every leg paired with stakes where the surface accepts them. Marquee sized one tier above the strict headcount requirement to absorb gusts.

British Properties estate lawns

Terraced grade is the operational reality. Most estates accept a 20×60 comfortably on the main lawn; the dance floor portion typically needs subfloor levelling. We grade-check during quoting and budget the levelling time before the install starts.

Cypress Park and mountain-adjacent venues

Elevation-driven afternoon wind is predictable from late morning. Same doubled anchor strategy as ocean-side installs, plus we recommend booking the install for the morning shift to finish before the afternoon wind pattern builds.

Whytecliff and Lighthouse Park

Tight venues. Full marquees rarely work here; 10×10 popups for the officiant or cake-table cover are the common pattern. Reception happens at an adjacent estate or waterfront home, and we move the full marquee setup there.

Per-item pricing

The same in Caulfeild as in Horseshoe Bay. Our 20×40 marquee is one rate, full stop. The line that changes is delivery and anchor — ballast count, ferry timing, cart access — never the tent rate.

Ocean-side anchor strategy and the wind plan

A West Van waterfront marquee is structurally a Burnaby marquee with twice the anchor weight and full sidewalls. The marquee itself doesn’t change; what changes is the install procedure. Every leg gets ballast (concrete weights, not water-fillable, because water leaks and concrete doesn’t). Engineered stakes pair with ballast wherever the surface accepts them — sandy lawn, grass, even some packed gravel. Sidewalls go up at install, not “if it rains” — Howe Sound weather changes in fifteen minutes and we don’t bring sidewalls back to install mid-reception. The Lower Mainland tent size guide covers the footprint math; the anchor strategy is what’s specific to West Van.

A 100-guest West Vancouver tent rental order

For a 100-guest West Van wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Dundarave waterfront home, ceremony on the lawn followed by reception under the marquee — a typical tent-side order looks like:

  • 1 × 20×60 marquee tent (covered reception on the lawn)
  • 4 × tent sidewalls (full enclosure for evening, Howe Sound wind)
  • 8 × bistro string-light strands across the marquee ridge
  • 1 × tent heater (shoulder-season default; August evenings still cool at the waterfront)
  • Engineered stakes paired with ballast at every leg (default for ocean-adjacent grass)

Setup runs 5–6 hours for a 20×60 + sidewall + doubled anchor configuration; teardown is 3–4. Both quoted up front. The 50/100/150/200 rental checklist maps marquee sizing across headcounts. FAQ

Tent Rentals Questions — West Vancouver

What's included on a West Vancouver tent rental quote?
Every West Van tent quote we send is itemised: the marquee, sidewalls, doubled anchor kit, lighting add-ons, delivery, professional crew install, and applicable taxes. No bridge surcharge, no upper-bench uplift, no surprises at the truck.
Can you install a marquee at Whytecliff Park or Lighthouse Park?
Both venues are tight on marquee installs. Whytecliff's lower beach won't take a full marquee — the rock surface and tidal exposure rule out anchoring. Most Whytecliff weddings book a 10×10 popup for the officiant station and shelter the cake table, with the ceremony itself uncovered. Lighthouse Park's old-growth canopy and trail access typically rule out anything larger than a 10×10. For full marquee receptions tied to either venue, the reception happens at an adjacent estate or waterfront home.
What tent size for a British Properties estate wedding?
Depends on guest count and lawn footprint. The typical British Properties estate lawn accepts a 20×60 marquee for 100 guests; larger upper-bench properties take 30×60 for 150–200. The terraced grade matters more than the square footage — we measure slope and may pair the marquee with subfloor under the dance floor portion.
Do you account for ocean wind on Ambleside or Dundarave waterfront marquees?
Yes — Howe Sound inflow wind is the constant we plan around for any ocean-side install. Sidewalls go up year-round, every leg gets ballast paired with stakes (engineered to whichever the surface accepts), and we recommend sizing a tier larger than headcount strictly requires so the footprint absorbs gusts rather than catching them. Reception evenings on Ambleside often run cooler than the forecast — heaters get quoted as a default for shoulder-season bookings.
How fast can you deliver a West Vancouver marquee on short notice?
Same-week is feasible for chair, table, and 10×10 popup orders. Marquee tents 20×40 and larger need 7+ days of installer scheduling, especially for West Van where venue coordination (Whytecliff permit desk, British Properties HOA notice, Horseshoe Bay ferry-timing) adds setup steps. Call before booking online and we'll tell you straight whether the timeline works.
