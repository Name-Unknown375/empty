2-day weekend minimum · delivery, setup & pickup available across Metro Vancouver

The Starlink Standard Actuated kit is the connectivity workhorse for Lower Mainland events that grid internet can't reach. Self-aligning rectangular dish (the dish tilts itself to find satellites — no manual aiming), Gen 3 WiFi router, 15 m of weather-rated cable, AC power supply, and a hard storage case. The Roam (Canada + USA) subscription comes pre-activated, so you plug it in and within about 5 minutes you have 100–200 Mbps down, 10–20 Mbps up, and 25–50 ms latency — fast enough for Zoom calls, 4K livestreams, contactless payment terminals, DJ streaming, and dozens of guest WiFi connections simultaneously. Power draw is about 50–75 W continuous (~150 W peak in cold weather when the dish melts snow), which means a single Delta 3 Max power station carries it for a full event with capacity to spare. We deliver, walk through setup, and confirm speeds before leaving site.

  • ✓ Self-aligning Standard Actuated dish — places + tilts itself in 5 minutes
  • ✓ 100–200 Mbps down · 25–50 ms latency — Zoom, livestream, POS, DJ streaming
  • ✓ Roam (Canada + USA) subscription pre-activated — no provisioning hassle
  • ✓ All-in-one kit: dish, router, cables, power supply, storage case
  • ✓ Pairs cleanly with Delta 3 Max for fully off-grid event connectivity
