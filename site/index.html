FAQ

Party Rental Questions

Quick answers on delivery, pricing, and booking party rentals across Metro Vancouver.

Do you offer party rentals near me?
We deliver party rentals across 28 communities in Metro Vancouver and the Fraser Valley — including Surrey, Vancouver, Burnaby, Langley, Coquitlam, Richmond, North Vancouver, Abbotsford, Maple Ridge, Delta, and New Westminster. Enter your event date and postal code when you book online and we'll confirm delivery to your address, or you're welcome to pick up from our Surrey warehouse.
What party rental supplies do you offer?
Our core inventory covers everything a celebration needs: marquee and popup tents, Chiavari, fanback and resin garden chairs, banquet, round and cocktail tables, and black-and-white dance floors. We also rent tent heaters, bistro string lights, sidewalls, EcoFlow battery power stations, Starlink internet, projectors, and staffed carnival games. Browse the full catalogue on our booking page.
Do you do birthday party rentals?
Yes — birthday parties are one of our most common bookings. A popup or marquee tent, a set of tables and chairs, and a dance floor turn any backyard into a venue. Our backyard packages bundle the essentials for 50, 100, or 150 guests at 10% off, and you can add carnival games or extras to match the theme.
How much do party rentals cost?
Pricing depends on the items and your guest count. As a guide, chairs start around $3–$4 each per day, tables from about $10–$15, and marquee tents from roughly $400 depending on size. Bundling a tent, tables, and chairs into a package saves 10%, and paying in full within 24 hours of your quote saves another 10%. Build an order online for an instant, itemised total — delivery and setup are quoted up front with no hidden fees.
How far in advance should I book party rentals?
For weddings and large summer events we recommend booking four to eight weeks ahead, since peak-season weekends from May through September fill quickly. Smaller backyard parties can often be arranged on shorter notice. A 25% deposit secures your booking, backed by our 125% Cancellation Protection.
Do you deliver and set up, or is it pickup only?
Both. Add delivery and setup at checkout and our crew handles round-trip transport, professional installation of tents and dance floors, takedown, and pickup. Smaller items like chairs, tables, and popup tents can also be picked up from our Surrey warehouse to save on delivery.
