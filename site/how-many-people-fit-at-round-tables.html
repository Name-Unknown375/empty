How Many People Fit at a Round Table?

A 5-foot (60") round table seats 8 guests comfortably — and 10 max if you drop to family-style place settings. Round tables give better sight lines than rectangles and are the wedding-reception default. We deliver 5 ft rounds across Metro Vancouver from $13.50 / day, and our free layout planner lets you test table spacing before you book.

How many people can sit at a 5 foot round table?

Eight for a plated dinner with full place settings; ten if guests are passing platters or circulating. Here's how every round size we see in the field compares:

5ft round table seats how many? The chair math

Round-table seating capacity is a function of edge length, not surface area. The circumference of a 60-inch round is π × 60 ≈ 188 inches. Divide by 24 inches per guest (the catering-school standard for elbow room with a full place setting) and you get 7.8 — so 8 guests. The same math on a 72-inch round gives 226 inches ÷ 24 = 9.4 — so 9 to 10 guests, depending on stemware count.

The "tight" numbers (10 on a 5-footer, 12 on a 6-footer) drop the per-guest edge to 19 inches — close to a folding-chair width with no breathing room. We see this configuration at cocktail-style receptions where guests are circulating, at family-style dinners where place settings are simpler, and on Mother's Day brunch when the maître d' is squeezing two extra seats onto a banquette.

The minimum we recommend for a plated wedding dinner with stemware, charger plates, and centrepieces? Eight at the 60-inch round, ten at the 72-inch round. Anything tighter and your photographer can't get a clean overhead shot of the table.

Rounds vs rectangles — what wedding planners actually choose

The reflex answer is "rounds, always." The honest answer is more nuanced. Round tables win on sight lines — every guest at the table can see every other guest without leaning around a centrepiece. Conversation is also better, because nobody is stuck at the end of a long table talking to one person.

Rectangular tables win on square-foot efficiency. A 6-foot rectangle seats 6 plated guests in 15 square feet (the table footprint plus chair clearance), or 4 square feet per guest. A 5-foot round seats 8 plated guests in 28 square feet (with chair clearance), or 3.5 square feet per guest — but the round shape leaves dead space at the corners that rectangles don't. In a venue measured to the inch, rectangles fit more guests.

The most common wedding configuration we deliver: 5-foot rounds for guest seating, 6- or 8-foot rectangles for the head table. The head table reads as the "focal" table from a distance, and the rounds give everyone else better conversation.

FAQ

What chairs work with a 5 ft round table?
Any 18-inch-wide chair fits 8 around a 5-foot round. The wedding-rental standard is white Chiavari chairs — clean lines, good for photos, dressy enough for plated service. Fanback folding chairs are the budget-friendly upgrade from white plastic. For garden ceremonies followed by a tented reception, resin garden chairs read elegant on both surfaces.
Do you have 6 ft round tables for rent?
Our standard inventory is the 5-foot round, which is the most-requested size by Lower Mainland wedding clients. For 6-foot rounds on a specific date, contact us directly — we can usually source them through our partner network within 2 weeks of your event.
Do you deliver round tables across Metro Vancouver?
Yes — round tables are a staple of our wedding deliveries in Vancouver and Richmond, and we also deliver to Surrey, Langley, Burnaby, North Vancouver, Abbotsford, Maple Ridge, Coquitlam, Delta, White Rock, New Westminster, Pitt Meadows, Mission, Chilliwack, Harrison Hot Springs, Fort Langley, and 15+ other Lower Mainland cities. Delivery and setup are quoted up front based on your venue address.
Can I rent rounds and rectangles on the same order?
Yes — most wedding orders mix 5-foot rounds for guest seating with one or two 6-foot or 8-foot rectangles for the head table and the gift / cake table. There's no minimum per SKU. Our online booking tool lets you build the mix and see the total before you confirm.
Are the tables wiped down between rentals?
Every table is wiped, inspected for wobbles or surface damage, and re-stickered with our QA tag before loading on the truck. If a table arrives at your venue with any defect, our delivery crew swaps it from truck stock or we run a replacement within 60 minutes — standard SOP across our fleet.
