Cup Toss Carnival Game Rental

Base rate $650 / 4-hour rental · staff included
Additional hours: +$150 / hr

Cup toss is the tabletop carnival game — think beer pong, but G-rated and scored. A 3×4 grid of numbered cups (1, 2, 3, 5, and a single 10-point centre cup) sits on a 6 ft banquet-style table. Players toss pingpong balls to land in cups for the corresponding score. Three balls per turn. Your $650 / 4hr rate covers a trained attendant to run the booth — they keep score, replace lost balls, hand out prizes, and reset cups between players. Smallest, fastest, and most space-efficient of our games — perfect for indoor venues and tight spaces.

  • ✓ Trained staff member to run the booth — included in price
  • ✓ Staff handles scoring, ball retrieval, prizes & cup resets
  • ✓ 12-cup numbered grid (1 / 2 / 3 / 5 / 10-point bonus)
  • ✓ 12 × pingpong balls (replaceable mid-event if lost)

  • ✓ Smallest footprint of any of our games
