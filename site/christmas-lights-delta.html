Delta install patterns

Ladner Village heritage residential

Older homes with detailed trim, complex rooflines, and narrow-street truck access. Heritage-friendly clip-style installs that respect the village character. Some addresses require ladder-only access from the front of the property — we factor it into the install timeline. Most run the Classic package.

Tsawwassen and Boundary Bay coastal

Strait of Georgia and Boundary Bay open-water exposure. Sustained-wind-rated install hardware is the default; commercial-grade LED strands rather than delicate mini-lights on exposed rooflines. Sidewall lighting on porches handles wind cleanly through the season.

North Delta and Sunshine Hills residential

Standard urban-density residential. Most lots accept the Starter or Classic package — warm-white C9 along the main roofline with optional tree accents. $8.50–$10/ft pricing for most. Sunshine Hills properties tend toward larger lots and run the Classic package.

Tilbury and ferry terminal commercial

Industrial-area commercial properties along Tilbury Industrial Park and the BC Ferries terminal area sometimes book Christmas-light installs for tenant events or year-round festive lighting. Commercial-grade install with after-5pm access timing.

Free off-season storage and the year-2 math

Year one, you pay for design, install, lights, takedown, and storage as a fixed package. Year two, the math gets meaningfully better — your strands are already in our warehouse, labeled with your section map. For Tsawwassen coastal installs especially, the sustained-wind-rated commercial-grade LEDs are a significant year-one cost; year-2 pricing typically drops 20–30% because those materials are already paid for.

A typical Delta install

For a North Delta two-storey home with main rooflines, two front-yard trees, and a covered porch, a typical Classic package install looks like:

  • ~150 linear feet of warm-white C9 LED along main and gable rooflines
  • ~60 linear feet of porch wrap and column accent
  • ~80 linear feet of mini-lights wrapping front-yard trees
  • ~30 linear feet of pathway lights along the front walk
  • Clip-style attachment, WCB-covered crew
  • One on-site design walk-through, season-long maintenance, January takedown, free storage

Setup runs 4–6 hours; takedown 2–3 hours. Both quoted up front. For Tsawwassen coastal-exposed properties, swap to sustained-wind-rated hardware and add ~30 minutes to setup time.

FAQ

Christmas Light Questions — Delta

How much does Christmas light installation cost in Delta?
Delta installations run $8.50–$12 per linear foot. Tsawwassen and Boundary Bay-facing coastal properties sometimes price slightly higher within that range given the wind-rated install hardware. Quote covers commercial-grade LED strands, design walk-through, install, season-long maintenance, takedown, and free off-season storage.
Do you install Christmas lights on Ladner Village heritage homes?
Yes — Ladner Village's historic homes are some of our favourite Delta installs. The catch is access: Ladner's narrow streets and heritage-protected sightlines limit truck staging. Some addresses require ladder-only access from the front of the property. We use clip-style installs (no staples, no nails) that respect heritage trim. Most heritage installs run the Classic package.
Can you install at coastal Tsawwassen or Boundary Bay properties?
Yes — coastal-exposed properties are routine Delta bookings. Strait of Georgia and Boundary Bay open-water wind exposure produces sustained afternoon gust patterns; we use sustained-wind-rated install hardware and commercial-grade LED strands rather than delicate mini-lights on exposed rooflines.
When do you install in Delta — and when's the deadline to book?
Delta installs run mid-October through early December. Booking opens in August; September–early October is the comfortable window. Tsawwassen coastal installs sometimes book earlier than inland Delta because weather windows for safe coastal install narrow as the season progresses.
What if a strand fails during the season?
We're on-site within 24 to 48 hours to replace it — no charge. Most installers make you wait until spring. We don't.
What happens to my lights after the season?
We come back, take it all down, label your strands by section, and store them in our climate-controlled warehouse until next October. No storage fee. Year two onward, you skip the buy-new step entirely.
