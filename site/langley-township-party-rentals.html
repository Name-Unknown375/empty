Vineyards, acreages, parks, and the Township’s four operational rhythms

A vineyard-row ceremony staged on Township grass.

Vineyard and berry-farm weddings

Krause Berry Farms, Township 7 Vineyards, Domaine de Chaberton, and the smaller agritourism properties between Brookswood and Aldergrove. Each venue’s harvesting and tasting-room schedule shapes our delivery window — we coordinate the load-in directly with the venue’s events team.

Brookswood and Murrayville acreages

Single-family rural-residential properties with the standard backyard concerns plus the Township’s open-field wind. Most acreages accept a 20×60 marquee comfortably; some accommodate two linked 20×40 tents for 200+ guest setups.

Campbell Valley Regional Park

Open-air park ceremony zones with farmland-style afternoon wind. The Township and Metro Vancouver Regional Parks run separate permit processes; we know which zones cover which footprints. Equestrian-adjacent ceremonies often skip the tent entirely.

Per-item pricing

The same in Brookswood as in Willoughby. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes is delivery — distance across the Township, anchor strategy, multi-venue — never the per-item rate.

Township-wide events: weddings + community + corporate

The Township’s geography supports a meaningful share of community-organization events outside the wedding category. Brookswood and Murrayville schools run grad ceremonies and field tents, agricultural fairs run at the Aldergrove area, and the Township’s community centres host civic functions throughout the year. These setups typically need 3–4 weeks of lead time (faster than wedding procurement) and often run on Fanback chairs, banquet tables, and bistro lighting — no marquee, single-truck delivery.

A 100-guest Langley Township vineyard wedding rental order

For a 100-guest Township vineyard wedding — say a Saturday in August at Domaine de Chaberton with ceremony among the vines and reception under a marquee on the lawn — a typical order looks like:

  • 1 × 20×60 marquee tent with sidewalls (covered reception; ceremony is open-air among the vines)
  • 100 × white Chiavari chairs (ceremony rows in the vineyard, then re-staged at reception tables)
  • 12 × 5ft round tables for the reception
  • 6 × 6ft banquet tables (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
  • 1 × 12×16 portable dance floor
  • 2 × cocktail highboys for the cocktail hour
  • Bistro string lighting along the marquee ridge
  • Engineered ground stakes (default for grass and farm soil)

Setup runs 5–6 hours; teardown 3–4. Both quoted up front. For other guest counts, the 50/100/150/200 rental checklist maps the same configuration to your headcount.

FAQ

Party Rental Questions — Langley Township

What's included on a Langley Township rental quote?
Every Township quote we send is itemised: rental items by line, delivery, crew setup time, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Township-zone uplifts, no surprises at the truck. If your quote says $X, you pay $X.
Do you deliver to Township vineyards like Domaine de Chaberton or Township 7?
Yes — both are regular Township delivery sites. Each venue runs its own vendor coordination process, and we book the delivery slot through their events team during quoting. Vineyard and farm weddings tend toward 80–150 guests with full marquee setups; we plan around the venue's harvesting and tasting-room schedule when relevant.
Can you tent at a Brookswood or Murrayville acreage wedding?
Yes — Brookswood and Murrayville acreages are some of our most-photogenic Township deliveries. Most properties have plenty of footprint for a 20×60 or 30×60 marquee. Engineered ground stakes are our default anchor on grass; we confirm sprinkler infrastructure and irrigation lines before staking. We won't install on artificial turf or on slopes steeper than 5% without an engineered platform.
How clean are the chairs and tables when they arrive?
Every chair is individually wiped and inspected before it leaves our warehouse. Cushions are spot-cleaned, legs checked for damage, spindles checked for cracks. Tables get the same treatment. Anything that doesn't meet our condition bar comes off the inventory roster, not onto your booking.
Can you set up tents at Campbell Valley Regional Park?
Yes — Campbell Valley is one of our regular Township park venues. The Township of Langley and Metro Vancouver Regional Parks run separate permit processes depending on the specific site within the park; the couple pulls the permits, but we know which ceremony zones at Campbell Valley accept which tent footprints. Equestrian and trail-adjacent zones often skip the tent entirely; chair-only ceremonies are common there.
How much do party rentals cost in Langley Township?
Party rental pricing in Langley Township depends on the items and your guest count. As a guide, chairs start around $3–$4 each per day, 6-foot banquet and 5-foot round tables from about $10–$13, and marquee tents from roughly $400 depending on size. Bundling a tent, tables and chairs into one of our packages saves 10%, and paying in full within 24 hours of your quote saves another 10%. Build your order online for an instant, itemised Langley Township quote — delivery and setup are priced up front with no hidden fees or fuel surcharges.
What does the delivery and setup fee cover?
When you add delivery and setup to your order, our crew handles round-trip transport, professional setup of tents and dance floors, takedown, and pickup. Chairs and tables are stacked and placed where you want them. Delivery and setup are quoted up front with no hidden charges or fuel surcharges — the price you see at checkout is the price you pay.
Can you set up a tent on grass, gravel, or a paved driveway?
Yes to all three. Our marquee tents use stake-down installation on grass and ballasted (weighted) installation on pavement, gravel, and stamped concrete. Tell us the surface when you book and we'll send the right hardware. We do not tent on artificial turf.
What happens if the weather is bad or I need to cancel?
Our 125% Cancellation Protection means if we ever cancel your booking, you receive 125% of your deposit back. If you need to reschedule because of weather, we'll roll your deposit forward to a new date at no charge — as long as we're notified before crew dispatch. Cancellations initiated by the customer are subject to our standard deposit terms.
