Burnaby’s two install rhythms — commercial and residential

Brentwood and Metrotown commercial

Strata buildings, retail plazas, restaurant exteriors, and commercial complexes book the install differently than residential homes. Building-management coordination on access windows. Strata-board approvals on aesthetic and color. After-5pm or weekend install timing to avoid disrupting tenants. Most commercial installs run on warm-white C9 or alternating-pattern designs depending on the brand or strata aesthetic. Pricing typically lands in the $10–$12/ft range given the install complexity and access constraints.

Deer Lake-area heritage residential

Older homes with detailed trim and complex rooflines. Most run the Classic or Premium package — warm-white C9 along the rooflines plus accent strands on porches, columns, and front-yard trees. Heritage homes get the same clip-style install (no staples, no nails) we use on West Side Vancouver heritage installs. Pricing lands in the $8.50–$10/ft range for most.

North Burnaby and Edmonds residential

Detached single-family homes with simpler rooflines than the heritage character. Most accept the Starter or Classic package — clean warm-white C9 along the main roofline with optional tree accents. $8.50/ft pricing for most Starter installs.

High-rise condo balconies

Brentwood Town Centre and Metrotown condos increasingly book balcony installs — string lights along the railing, mini-lights along the planter line, sometimes a small artificial-tree install on the balcony itself. We coordinate freight-elevator access with building management; install non-damaging mounting (no drilling).

Free off-season storage and the year-2 math

Year one, you pay for the design, install, lights, takedown, and storage as a fixed package. Year two, the math gets meaningfully better — your strands are already in our warehouse, labeled with your section map. Most Burnaby clients see year-2 pricing 15–25% lower than year-1 because the materials are already paid for. The install crew arrives on day one with the same lights, the same plan, and the same install pattern.

This is why most Burnaby commercial properties book us multi-year — strata boards don’t want to redesign their light scheme every November.

Christmas Light Questions — Burnaby

How much does Christmas light installation cost in Burnaby?
Our Burnaby installations run $8.50–$12 per linear foot. The price covers commercial-grade LED strands, the on-site design walk-through, install, season-long maintenance, takedown, and free off-season storage in our climate-controlled warehouse. Fixed-price quote within 24 hours of your free consultation.
Do you install Christmas lights on Brentwood or Metrotown commercial buildings?
Yes — strata buildings, retail plazas, restaurant exteriors, and commercial complexes around Brentwood Town Centre and Metrotown are some of our most-frequent Burnaby installs. Commercial installs need building-management coordination on access windows; we book the slot and confirm permit-or-bylaw considerations with the strata or building during quoting.
Can you do high-rise condo balcony lights?
Yes — balcony lighting installs at high-rise condos are common Burnaby bookings. We coordinate freight-elevator access with building management, install the lights with non-damaging mounting (no drilling, no permanent fixtures), and handle takedown and storage like any residential install.
When do you install in Burnaby — and when's the deadline to book?
Burnaby installs run mid-October through early December, with takedown happening between January 5 and 31. Booking opens in August; the comfortable window is September–early October. Commercial installs sometimes book earlier than residential since strata-board approvals add time.
What if a strand fails during the season?
We're on-site within 24 to 48 hours to replace it — no charge. Most installers make you wait until spring or charge an emergency-service fee. We don't.
What happens to my lights after the season?
We come back, take it all down, label your strands by section, and store them in our climate-controlled warehouse until next October. No storage fee — it's part of the package. Year two onward, you skip the buy-new step entirely.
