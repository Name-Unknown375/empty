What our Delta chair lineup covers

A Chiavari ready to load — individually wiped, inspected, stacked.

Three chair lines that cover almost every Delta event:

  • White Chiavari ($8.50/day) — the wedding-reception standard. Lacquered hardwood frame, ivory cushion, stacks 8 high. Best for Ladner Village heritage receptions, indoor Tsawwassen restaurant weddings, Tilbury corporate events, and any Delta venue where the photos prioritize formal seating.
  • Fanback folding ($3.25/day) — the workhorse. Steel frame, folds flat, stacks compactly. Right for ceremony rows, school events at Delta campuses, corporate registrations at Tilbury industrial park venues, and any high-headcount booking.
  • Resin Garden ($5.50/day) — the outdoor-resilient pick. Resin frame and seat, more substantial visual than Fanback but more forgiving than Chiavari to weather and damp grass. The right call for Boundary Bay shoreline ceremonies, Reifel Bird Sanctuary outdoor weddings, and Tsawwassen acreage outdoor receptions.

Mixing lines is free. We carry enough of each that the full ceremony + reception split ships without short-shipping.

Boundary Bay, Ladner, Tsawwassen — Delta chair patterns

Boundary Bay shoreline and Reifel Sanctuary

Boundary Bay Regional Park’s shoreline and Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary’s nature-walk ceremony zones run on damp grass, salt air, and afternoon onshore wind. Resin Garden every time. Truck access is straightforward through the park access roads; we coordinate delivery windows around the venue’s hours.

Ladner Village heritage

Ladner Village’s heritage streets are tight on truck access — we use shorter delivery windows and pre-stage equipment near the property line. Most Ladner weddings are smaller (60–100 guests) and lean Chiavari indoors at the heritage venues along 48 Avenue.

Tsawwassen acreage and ferry-adjacent

Tsawwassen acreage weddings near the ferry terminal run on rural roads with ample truck access. Most Tsawwassen bookings are larger (100–200 guests) with a two-chair-line configuration: Resin Garden outdoor ceremony, Chiavari indoor reception under a marquee.

North Delta, Sunshine Hills, Tilbury

North Delta and Sunshine Hills run standard residential backyard patterns. Tilbury’s industrial park hosts corporate events — registration banquets and seated-row Fanback configurations are typical there.

FAQ

Chair Rentals Questions — Delta

What's included on a Delta chair rental quote?
Every Delta chair quote we send is itemised: chairs by line, delivery, crew positioning to your floor plan, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Delta-zone uplifts, no surprises at the truck.
Which chair works for a Boundary Bay or Reifel ceremony?
For Boundary Bay's shoreline ceremony zone and Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary's nature-walk weddings, Resin Garden is the strongest pick — the resin frame and seat handle damp grass, salt air, and the dew off the bay. Chiavari's lacquered hardwood doesn't love full-day shoreline exposure. Most Boundary Bay weddings book Resin Garden for the ceremony and Chiavari indoors at a Tsawwassen or Ladner reception venue.
Can you deliver chairs to Tsawwassen acreage or Ladner Village?
Yes — both are routine Delta bookings. Tsawwassen acreage weddings near the ferry terminal use standard delivery; the rural roads accept our truck without issue. Ladner Village's tighter heritage streets occasionally need a smaller staging window — we coordinate during quoting to avoid blocking village storefronts.
Can I mix chair types for a Delta event?
Yes — Fanback folding for the ceremony rows, Chiavari at the reception tables; or Resin Garden for an outdoor Boundary Bay ceremony followed by Chiavari indoors at a Ladner heritage reception. Single quote, single delivery, no mixing surcharge.
How do you handle Boundary Bay's wind and salt air?
Boundary Bay's afternoon onshore wind is reliable enough that we always recommend Resin Garden (heavier) over Fanback for the shoreline ceremony zone. For larger setups, sandbags or ballasted chair clips are an optional add-on if the forecast looks gusty.
