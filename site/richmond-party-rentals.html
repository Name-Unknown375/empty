Three operational patterns specific to Richmond

Cocktail highboys staged for a Richmond cocktail hour.

Cultural-wedding timing precision

Multi-stage ceremonies often start at 8 AM or earlier — tea ceremonies typically run before brunch. We dispatch the truck to be on-site 90 minutes before your earliest milestone, with the chairs set, aisle-spaced, and angled before family arrival.

Steveston wind exposure

Garry Point and the Steveston dyke trails sit on open water with afternoon wind that’s meaningfully different from Vancouver-side or inland Richmond venues. Sidewalls year-round, heavier anchor strategy, and a slightly larger marquee than headcount strictly requires.

Multi-venue events

Richmond cultural weddings often span two venues — ceremony at one site, reception at another. One quote, two delivery windows on the same day, same crew across both. We coordinate the timing directly with whoever’s running point on your day.

Per-item pricing

The same in Steveston as in central Richmond. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, multi-venue, wind-anchor — never the per-item rate.

Olympic Oval and Richmond corporate events

Richmond’s other operational rhythm is corporate — galas, product launches, conference functions, and charity events at the Olympic Oval and the larger downtown venues. The pattern is faster than weddings: corporate clients usually have approved-vendor lists and shorter procurement cycles, so 3–4 weeks of lead time is typical instead of the 6–8 we recommend for peak-season Saturdays. Most large-venue corporate setups skip the marquee entirely (the building’s interior is the venue) and run on banquet seating, Chiavari chairs, cocktail highboys, and bistro lighting for ambience.

A 100-guest Richmond wedding rental order

For a 100-guest Richmond wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Steveston-area outdoor venue with ceremony and reception both running on-site — a typical order looks like:

  • 1 × 20×60 marquee tent with sidewalls (Steveston wind + evening enclosure)
  • 100 × white Chiavari chairs (ceremony rows, then re-staged at reception tables)
  • 12 × 5ft round tables for the reception
  • 6 × 6ft banquet tables (head, family, gift, cake, DJ, bar)
  • 1 × 12×16 portable dance floor
  • 2 × cocktail highboys for the cocktail hour
  • Bistro string lighting along the marquee ridge
  • Ballasted weight kits (Steveston-area; engineered stakes elsewhere on grass)

Setup runs 5–6 hours; teardown 3–4. Both quoted up front.

For multi-stage cultural weddings the chair count typically scales (200–300 guest weddings are common), and the table side picks up additional banquet tables for larger family seating. The 50/100/150/200 rental checklist maps the configuration to larger headcounts; the square-footage-per-guest guide is the next read for sizing.

FAQ

Party Rental Questions — Richmond

What's included on a Richmond rental quote?
Every Richmond quote we send is itemised: rental items by line, delivery, crew setup time, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Richmond-zone uplifts, no surprises at the truck. If your quote says $X, you pay $X.
Can you accommodate cultural-wedding timing requirements?
Yes — multi-stage cultural weddings (tea ceremony, family blessing, reception) typically have strict timing. We confirm the full event schedule during quoting and dispatch the truck to be on-site 90 minutes before your earliest milestone. Mid-event chair re-stage between ceremony and reception is standard for Richmond cultural weddings; we plan crew availability around it.
How clean are the chairs and tables when they arrive?
Every chair is individually wiped and inspected before it leaves our warehouse. Cushions are spot-cleaned, legs checked for damage, spindles checked for cracks. Tables get the same treatment. Anything that doesn't meet our condition bar comes off the inventory roster, not onto your booking.
What's your wind-and-rain plan for Steveston-area outdoor ceremonies?
Steveston and Garry Point are exposed to the Strait of Georgia, which produces meaningfully more lateral wind load than inland Richmond venues. For any Steveston-area outdoor ceremony we recommend sidewalls year-round and a heavier-anchor strategy than typical urban installs. If a forecast forces a date change, we waive the change fee when called before crew dispatch.
Can I split one quote across two Richmond venues on the same day?
Yes — Richmond cultural weddings often span two venues (ceremony at one site, reception at another). We can split a single quote across two delivery windows on the same day, with the same crew handling both load-ins. One quote, two stops, one team accountable end-to-end.
How much do party rentals cost in Richmond?
Party rental pricing in Richmond depends on the items and your guest count. As a guide, chairs start around $3–$4 each per day, 6-foot banquet and 5-foot round tables from about $10–$13, and marquee tents from roughly $400 depending on size. Bundling a tent, tables and chairs into one of our packages saves 10%, and paying in full within 24 hours of your quote saves another 10%. Build your order online for an instant, itemised Richmond quote — delivery and setup are priced up front with no hidden fees or fuel surcharges.
What does the delivery and setup fee cover?
When you add delivery and setup to your order, our crew handles round-trip transport, professional setup of tents and dance floors, takedown, and pickup. Chairs and tables are stacked and placed where you want them. Delivery and setup are quoted up front with no hidden charges or fuel surcharges — the price you see at checkout is the price you pay.
Can you set up a tent on grass, gravel, or a paved driveway?
Yes to all three. Our marquee tents use stake-down installation on grass and ballasted (weighted) installation on pavement, gravel, and stamped concrete. Tell us the surface when you book and we'll send the right hardware. We do not tent on artificial turf.
What happens if the weather is bad or I need to cancel?
Our 125% Cancellation Protection means if we ever cancel your booking, you receive 125% of your deposit back. If you need to reschedule because of weather, we'll roll your deposit forward to a new date at no charge — as long as we're notified before crew dispatch. Cancellations initiated by the customer are subject to our standard deposit terms.
