What our Richmond table lineup covers

A 5ft round set for 8 — the Richmond wedding-reception standard.

Three tables, three jobs. Richmond event floor plans usually need all three:

  • 5ft round tables ($13.50/day, seats 8) — the classic wedding-reception configuration. Eight guests, room for centerpieces, fits two-deep into most marquee footprints (a 20×40 holds 6 rounds; a 20×60 holds 12). Standard for Brighouse hotel banquet weddings.
  • 6ft rectangular banquet tables ($10.95/day, seats 6–8) — the single most versatile SKU we ship. Family-style dining rows, buffet stations, gift tables, DJ booths, bar setups, registration desks at Olympic Oval galas, head tables for Steveston and Garry Point weddings. Under 75 lb so they re-stage quickly mid-event.
  • Cocktail highboy tables ($15.50/day, standing) — 42-inch standing-height rounds for cocktail hour, networking events, and pre-dinner mingling. Plan ~1 highboy per 10–12 standing guests.

Tables care most about access and floor plan — we don’t add a per-table setup charge when delivery is on the quote.

Richmond venue access and floor-plan staging

Navy rounds and highboys staged for a company party.

Brighouse hotel ballrooms

Banquet weddings at Sheraton, Pacific Gateway, Marriott, and the Hilton run 200–400 guests with banquet-style table mixes (often 10-per-round configurations rather than 8-per-round). Loading-dock access is controlled and we book the delivery window during quoting.

Olympic Oval and River Rock — gala-scale

Olympic Oval freight access is ample but the calendar is tightly programmed. Large highboy counts (50+ for cocktail receptions) are common for galas; we ship them on dedicated trucks rather than mixing with banquet rounds.

Steveston and Garry Point — waterfront

Steveston Heritage Village runs constrained: smaller table counts, village-coordinated delivery windows, and tight setback rules. Garry Point Park is more flexible on size but adds wind considerations — banquet bases over highboy bistros where the site is fully exposed.

Hamilton, Thompson, Seafair backyards

Detached-home Richmond backyards are flat and large. Highboys on the patio, banquets and rounds on the grass — we level them during the staging window.

Per-item pricing

The same in Brighouse as on Garry Point. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — dock access, after-hours timing, distance — never the per-table rate.

Mid-event flips and ceremony-to-reception staging

Many Richmond weddings run a single-tent ceremony-to-reception flip — same chairs, same marquee, with our crew rearranging the table layout in 30–45 minutes during the cocktail-hour window. Banquet tables fold flat and re-position quickly; rounds re-stack to the truck while the dinner configuration goes up. This is included on single-tent bookings, not an add-on line.

A 100-guest Richmond table rental order

For a 100-guest Richmond wedding with a marquee reception and a separate cocktail-hour space, a typical table-side order looks like:

  • 12 × 5ft round tables (8 guests per round = 96, with 2 extras for kids and overflow)
  • 6 × 6ft banquet tables (head linking 2, gift, cake, DJ, registration)
  • 2 × 6ft banquet tables for the buffet line
  • 3 × cocktail highboys for the pre-dinner cocktail hour

Total: 23 tables, all positioned to your floor plan during a single delivery window. For larger Brighouse banquet weddings (10-per-round configurations, 250+ guests), the round count and head-table linkage shift accordingly. The 50/100/150/200 rental checklist maps the table mix to your headcount.

FAQ

Table Rentals Questions — Richmond

What's included on a Richmond table rental quote?
Every Richmond table quote we send is itemised: tables by line, delivery, crew positioning to your floor plan, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No setup surcharge — every table is unfolded and positioned during the delivery window.
How many tables for a 100-guest Richmond wedding?
For seated dinner at 5ft rounds, plan ~12 rounds (8 guests per round = 96; round up for kids and split parties). Add 5–8 banquet tables for head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, and buffet. Add 2–3 cocktail highboys for the pre-dinner cocktail hour. Richmond banquet-style weddings often run 10-per-round at the head-table cluster, which adjusts the round count slightly.
Can you deliver tables to the Olympic Oval or River Rock?
Yes — large-scale Richmond venues are routine. Olympic Oval freight access is ample and we coordinate the delivery window during quoting. River Rock Show Theatre runs on a controlled dock schedule we book during quoting, not on event day.
Do you supply linens, runners, or chargers?
We don't stock linens directly, but we coordinate through our preferred Lower Mainland linen partners and bundle them onto the same delivery window if you want. Chargers, runners, and place settings sit with the catering provider.
How do you handle tables at a Garry Point or Steveston waterfront event?
Tables at exposed waterfront sites need the same wind discipline as the marquee. Lightweight high-tops can lift in Richmond's onshore wind — we recommend banquet bases (heavier) over highboy bistros if your Garry Point setup is fully exposed. For cocktail-hour highboys, we either stage them under the tent canopy or weight the bases.
