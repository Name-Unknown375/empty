What our North Vancouver chair lineup covers

A North Shore ceremony — water, trees, and clean white seating.

Three chair lines that cover almost every North Vancouver event:

  • White Chiavari ($8.50/day) — the wedding-reception standard. Lacquered hardwood frame, ivory cushion, stacks 8 high. Best for indoor and covered-patio receptions, Lonsdale-area restaurants, and any photographed ceremony where the formal frame is the aesthetic priority.
  • Fanback folding ($3.25/day) — the workhorse. Steel frame, folds flat, stacks compactly. Right for high-headcount ceremony rows, school events, and budget-conscious bookings.
  • Resin Garden ($5.50/day) — the outdoor-resilient pick. Resin frame and seat, more substantial visual than Fanback but more forgiving than Chiavari to weather, damp grass, and forest floor. The right call for Lynn Canyon forest ceremonies, mountain-adjacent bookings, and any October–April outdoor North Van wedding.

The surface chooses the chair before the photograph does. We don’t book Chiavari onto saturated forest floor.

Forest, mountain, waterfront — North Van chair patterns

A Chiavari ready for the reception — individually wiped before loading.

Forest-floor ceremonies

Lynn Canyon, Capilano Suspension Bridge Park, and the wooded zones along the North Shore favour Resin Garden chairs. The forest floor is uneven and the soil holds moisture; Chiavari hardwood doesn’t love either condition.

Mountain-elevation ceremonies

Grouse Mountain and Mount Seymour ceremonies sit higher than they look on a map. Wind exposure is real, and morning dew on the elevated turf affects chair choice. Resin Garden again — and we recommend ballasted weight backup on the chair-side decor (signs, flowers) at any mountain venue.

Waterfront ceremonies

Lonsdale Quay-area weddings and waterfront restaurant patios accept Chiavari fine — the surface is concrete or controlled patio rather than damp grass. Wind exposure here is moderate; sidewall infrastructure isn’t usually needed for chair-only setups.

Per-item pricing

The same on Grouse Mountain as in Edgemont. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — grade, mountain access, distance — never the per-chair rate.

Mixed forest-and-reception orders

Many North Van weddings combine an outdoor forest or waterfront ceremony with an indoor or covered reception elsewhere — Lonsdale-area restaurants, Capilano-area private homes, Edgemont and Lynn Valley backyards under marquee. The standard mixed order is 100 × Resin Garden for the ceremony rows + 100 × Chiavari at the reception tables. One quote, one delivery, no mixing surcharge.

A 100-guest North Vancouver chair rental order

For a 100-guest North Vancouver wedding — say a Saturday in August with ceremony at Lynn Canyon and reception at a Lynn Valley acreage — a typical chair-side order looks like:

  • 100 × Resin Garden chairs (forest-floor ceremony rows under canopy)
  • 100 × white Chiavari chairs (reception under marquee at the acreage)

The two-line setup adds a few hundred dollars but gives the photographer cleaner forest-floor frames and the reception a more formal aesthetic. For dry-summer Edgemont backyard weddings, 100 × Chiavari handles both phases with our crew flipping the chairs in the cocktail hour.

Chair Rentals Questions — North Vancouver

What's included on a North Vancouver chair rental quote?
Every North Van chair quote we send is itemised: chairs by line, delivery, crew positioning to your floor plan, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no North Shore uplifts, no surprises at the truck.
Which chair works for a Lynn Canyon or forest-floor ceremony?
For forest-floor sites at Lynn Canyon and the wooded zones along the North Shore, Resin Garden is the strongest pick — the resin frame and seat handle damp soil and uneven forest floors better than Chiavari's lacquered hardwood. Most October–April forest bookings skew toward Resin Garden.
How clean are the chairs when they arrive?
Every chair is individually wiped and inspected before it leaves our warehouse. Cushions are spot-cleaned, legs checked for damage, spindles checked for cracks. Anything that doesn't meet our condition bar comes off the inventory roster, not onto your booking.
Can I mix chair types for a North Van wedding?
Yes — Resin Garden for the forest or mountain ceremony rows, Chiavari at the reception tables. Single quote, single delivery, no mixing surcharge. We carry enough of each line to ship the full split without short-shipping.
How fast can you deliver chairs for a North Vancouver event?
Same-week chair-only orders are usually feasible if our trucks aren't already booked. Even with North Van's wider delivery window, chair orders typically come together inside 5–7 days during peak season. Larger 200+ chair orders need at least 10 days.
