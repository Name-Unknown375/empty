What our North Van Christmas crew brings to your roofline

We’re a small enough team that the person who quotes your install is on the roof during install day. We’re large enough that we own every part of the service — design, install, maintenance, takedown, storage — and don’t sub-contract.

On the truck for a typical North Van install:

  • Commercial-grade LED strands — 5+ season durability, rated for North Shore wind
  • Warm white C9 LED roofline (the most-booked North Van aesthetic)
  • Sustained-wind-rated clip-style hardware for mountain-adjacent and waterfront installs
  • Steep-roof certification on our crew for North Shore pitches

  • Mini-light tree and pathway accents for sheltered yards under canopy

Mountain wind cuts the same direction in November as in October. We rate the install for the gust load before quoting, not after.

North Shore install patterns

Steep North Shore residential

Most North Van homes sit on steeper roof pitches than urban Vancouver. Our crew is certified for two- and three-storey installs on these pitches; we use commercial-grade clip hardware that holds in sustained wind. Most installs run the Classic or Premium package — warm-white C9 along main and gable rooflines plus tree accents.

Mountain-adjacent and elevation-aware

Properties along Grouse, Mount Seymour, upper Capilano, and Heritage Mountain boundaries get an elevation-aware install plan. Sustained-wind-rated hardware. Commercial-grade LED strands rather than delicate mini-lights on exposed rooflines. We schedule install windows around weather windows — November installs sometimes happen earlier in the month here than in valley-floor residential because mountain weather narrows the safe-install window.

Lonsdale and waterfront residential

Lonsdale-area residential and the waterfront homes along the Burrard Inlet share open-water wind exposure. Sidewall lighting on porches, sustained-wind-rated install on the main rooflines.

Edgemont, Lynn Valley, Deep Cove

Established residential neighborhoods with mature tree cover. Overhead-branch clearance for our install ladder access is a routine variable we measure during the design walk. Most properties run the Classic package given the linear footage of established-suburb rooflines.

Free off-season storage and the year-2 math

Year one, you pay for design, install, lights, takedown, and storage as a fixed package. Year two, the math gets meaningfully better — your strands are already in our warehouse, labeled with your roof’s section map. Most North Van clients see year-2 pricing 15–25% lower than year-1 because the materials are already paid for. For mountain-adjacent installs, the wind-rated commercial-grade LEDs are a significant year-one cost — and they’re already in storage for year two.

A typical North Vancouver residential install

For a steep two-storey Lynn Valley home with rooflines, two large front-yard trees with overhead-branch clearance, and a sloped driveway approach, a typical Classic package install looks like:

  • ~150 linear feet of warm-white C9 LED along main and gable rooflines (sustained-wind-rated hardware)
  • ~80 linear feet of mini-lights wrapping front-yard trees (sheltered side, branch-clearance confirmed)
  • ~30 linear feet of pathway lights along the driveway approach
  • Steep-roof clip-style attachment, WCB-covered crew
  • One on-site design walk-through, season-long maintenance, January takedown, free storage

Setup runs 5–7 hours given the steep-roof access; takedown 3–4 hours. Both quoted up front.

FAQ

Christmas Light Questions — North Vancouver

How much does Christmas light installation cost in North Vancouver?
North Van installations run $8.50–$12 per linear foot. Steep-roof and mountain-adjacent properties sometimes price slightly higher within that range given the access complexity and roof certification required. Quote covers commercial-grade LED strands, design walk-through, install, season-long maintenance, takedown, and free off-season storage.
Can your crew handle steep North Shore rooflines?
Yes — our crews are WCB-certified on two- and three-storey roofs and rated for steeper-than-urban pitches typical of North Shore homes. We confirm roof angle and access during the design walk-through and never install on slopes our crew isn't certified for. Some Capilano and upper-Lonsdale properties have access constraints we factor into the install timeline.
Do you install at mountain-adjacent properties (Grouse, Mount Seymour)?
Yes — mountain-adjacent properties are some of our most-photogenic North Van installs. Elevation-driven afternoon wind is meaningful at these properties; we use sustained-wind-rated install hardware and commercial-grade LED strands. Driveway grade access during November and early December (when light snow can begin) is a logistical variable we plan around.
When do you install in North Vancouver — and when's the booking deadline?
North Van installs run mid-October through early December. Booking opens in August; September–early October is the comfortable window. Mountain-adjacent properties sometimes book earlier than valley-floor residential because weather windows for safe install narrow as the season progresses.
What if a strand fails during the season?
We're on-site within 24 to 48 hours to replace it — no charge. Most installers make you wait until spring. We don't.
What happens to my lights after the season?
We come back, take it all down, label your strands by section, and store them in our climate-controlled warehouse until next October. No storage fee. Year two onward, you skip the buy-new step entirely.
