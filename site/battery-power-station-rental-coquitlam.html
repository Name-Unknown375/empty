Coquitlam Battery & Power Station Rentals FAQ

Can I run a tent heater off an EcoFlow at a Coquitlam event?
A 1500 W ceramic heater pulls roughly 1500 Wh per hour — a Delta 3 Max (2048 Wh) gives you about 75 minutes of heat, and a Delta 3 Ultra (3072 Wh) about 2 hours. For all-evening heat in Coquitlam we recommend our propane tent heaters; for short cocktail-hour warming or AV+light loads, the EcoFlows are silent and indoor-safe.
Is this safe to run inside a marquee tent or indoor venue in Coquitlam?
Yes — EcoFlow power stations have zero emissions, near-silent operation (~30 dB in standby), and no fuel storage. They're rated for indoor use and we routinely deploy them inside enclosed marquees, banquet halls, and historic Coquitlam venues that ban gas generators.
How long can I keep the rental for a Coquitlam multi-day event?
Every battery has two rate tiers: a 2-day weekend rate (Friday delivery, Sunday pickup) and a weekly rate (5–7 day window) for festivals, week-long activations, or production schedules. Both include delivery, on-site walkthrough, and pickup in Coquitlam.
Do I need the 220 W solar panel for a one-day event?
Usually no — a fully charged Delta 3 Max or Ultra will power most one-day Coquitlam events on its own. The solar panel earns its keep on multi-day activations (festivals, week-long pop-ups) where you can't get to a wall outlet between days.
Can the Delta 3 Ultra really power a whole reception in Coquitlam?
For lighting, AV, DJ, and small kitchen appliances — yes. With extra batteries it scales to 11 kWh, which covers a typical 5-hour reception with bistro lights, a sound system, and a coffee/dessert bar. For HVAC or large catering kitchens we still recommend grid power; we'll spec the right unit when you book.
Do you deliver party rentals to Coquitlam?
Yes — we deliver and set up throughout Coquitlam, including Town Centre, Westwood Plateau, and Burke Mountain. Standard Coquitlam delivery runs about 35-45 minutes. Delivery and setup are available as add-on services — we'll quote them up front with no surprise fees at the venue.
Have you worked at venues in Coquitlam like Lafarge Lake?
Yes — we regularly deliver rentals to Lafarge Lake, Town Centre Park, and Mundy Park and dozens of private residences across Coquitlam. We know the setback rules for most public parks in Coquitlam and can advise on what permits (if any) your venue requires for tents.
