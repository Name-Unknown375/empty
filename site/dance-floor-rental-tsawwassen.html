Dance Floor Rentals Questions — Tsawwassen

What size dance floor do I need for my Tsawwassen event?
A general rule of thumb: ~4.5 square feet per dancer, assuming half your guests are dancing at once. A 12×12 floor works for about 50 guests; 15×15 for 100; 18×18 for 150; and 20×20 for 200+. For Tsawwassen weddings, we'll help you right-size during booking.
Can you install the dance floor on grass or under a tent in Tsawwassen?
Yes — our dance floor includes a subfloor system that levels over grass, carpet, uneven pavement, or existing venue floors. For Tsawwassen outdoor weddings under a marquee tent, we stage the dance floor first, then install the tent around it.
How long does it take to install the dance floor?
For a standard 15×15 or 18×18 floor in Tsawwassen, our crew needs roughly 60–90 minutes of clear floor time to install and level. We coordinate the delivery window with your venue so the floor is in place before florals, staging, and head table dressing.
Do you deliver party rentals to Tsawwassen?
Yes — we deliver and set up throughout Tsawwassen, including Tsawwassen Village, English Bluff, and Beach Grove. Standard Tsawwassen delivery runs about 25-35 minutes via Highway 17. Delivery and setup are available as add-on services — we'll quote them up front with no surprise fees at the venue.
Have you worked at venues in Tsawwassen like Centennial Beach?
Yes — we regularly deliver rentals to Centennial Beach, Diefenbaker Park, and Tsawwassen Mills events space and dozens of private residences across Tsawwassen. We know the setback rules for most public parks in Tsawwassen and can advise on what permits (if any) your venue requires for tents.
