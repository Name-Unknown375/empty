Heritage village access, Westham Island, and Ladner operational realities

A heritage-village ceremony staged for a smaller Ladner wedding.

Ladner Village access

Heritage-protected streets, narrow alleys, and driveway grade variations mean we confirm access during quoting for every Ladner Village booking. Some addresses require staging at the road and hand-carrying equipment. Most village-core weddings stay smaller (under 80 guests) and skip the marquee entirely.

Westham Island bridge crossing

Westham Island has one bridge connecting it to Ladner mainland. We factor the bridge crossing into the delivery window — never tight on event day. Once on the island, the venues themselves typically have plenty of footprint and standard farm-soil staking works well.

Marina-side ceremonies

Ladner’s working marinas (Captain’s Cove and the smaller boatyards along the river) host occasional ceremony and reception events. Marina events need ballast (no staking on dock surfaces), windbreak from the river, and a smaller-scale setup that respects the working-water character of the venue.

Per-item pricing

The same on Westham Island as in central Ladner. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — bridge crossing, hand-carry, distance — never the per-item rate.

Reifel Bird Sanctuary-area events

The Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary’s surrounding land hosts occasional fundraisers, conservation events, and small charity functions throughout the year. These setups typically run smaller than weddings (50–80 guests), often skip the marquee entirely (open-air ceremonies in the mild months), and need a quieter setup pattern that respects the wildlife area’s character. Lead times are often shorter (2–4 weeks) since charity events run on their own calendars.

An 80-guest Ladner heritage village wedding rental order

For an 80-guest Ladner wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Ladner Village heritage home with cocktail hour in the front yard and reception indoors — a typical order looks like:

  • 80 × white Chiavari chairs (cocktail hour staging, then re-positioned indoors at reception)
  • 10 × 5ft round tables for the reception
  • 5 × 6ft banquet tables (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration)
  • 1 × 12×12 portable dance floor (indoor)
  • 2 × cocktail highboys for the front-yard cocktail hour
  • Bistro string lighting (front-yard cocktail hour)

Setup runs 3–4 hours; teardown 2–3. Both quoted up front. For Westham Island farm weddings (where reception runs outdoors under marquee), the order picks up a 20×60 marquee with sidewalls plus engineered ground stakes. Ladner FAQ

What's included on a Ladner rental quote?
Every Ladner quote we send is itemised: rental items by line, delivery, crew setup time, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Ladner-zone uplifts, no surprises at the truck. If your quote says $X, you pay $X.
Do you handle Ladner Village heritage-home weddings?
Yes — Ladner Village's historic homes are some of our favourite Delta delivery sites. The catch is access: Ladner Village's narrow streets and heritage-protected sightlines limit truck staging, and several homes have driveway constraints we confirm during quoting. Most Ladner heritage-home weddings stay small (under 80 guests) and skew toward Chiavari chairs, banquet tables, and bistro lighting rather than full marquee setups.
Can you tent at a Westham Island farm wedding?
Yes — Westham Island's working farms are among our most-photogenic Ladner deliveries. The island has limited access (one bridge from Ladner), but once we're across the venue footprint is generous. Engineered ground stakes work well on farm soil; we confirm sprinkler infrastructure and irrigation lines before staking.
How clean are the chairs and tables when they arrive?
Every chair is individually wiped and inspected before it leaves our warehouse. Cushions are spot-cleaned, legs checked for damage, spindles checked for cracks. Tables get the same treatment. Anything that doesn't meet our condition bar comes off the inventory roster, not onto your booking.
How fast can you deliver Ladner rentals?
Ladner is one of our shorter delivery routes, which means same-week chair and table orders are usually feasible if our trucks aren't already booked, even in peak season. Marquee tents 20×40 and larger need 7+ days of lead time. Westham Island deliveries factor the bridge crossing into the delivery window.
How much do party rentals cost in Ladner?
Party rental pricing in Ladner depends on the items and your guest count. As a guide, chairs run $3.25–$8.50 each per day, 6-foot banquet and 5-foot round tables $10.95–$13.50 per day, popup tents from $75 per day, and marquee tents from $550 per event — setup and takedown included. Bundling a tent, tables and chairs into one of our packages saves 10%, and paying in full within 24 hours of your quote saves another 10%. Build your order online for an instant, itemised Ladner quote — delivery and setup are priced up front with no hidden fees or fuel surcharges.
What does the delivery and setup fee cover?
When you add delivery and setup to your order, our crew handles round-trip transport, professional setup of tents and dance floors, takedown, and pickup. Chairs and tables are stacked and placed where you want them. Delivery and setup start at $175 and depend on distance from our Surrey warehouse — quoted up front with no hidden charges or fuel surcharges. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.
Can you set up a tent on grass, gravel, or a paved driveway?
Yes to all three. Our marquee tents use stake-down installation on grass and ballasted (weighted) installation on pavement, gravel, and stamped concrete. Tell us the surface when you book and we'll send the right hardware. We do not tent on artificial turf.
What happens if the weather is bad or I need to cancel?
If you need to reschedule because of weather, we'll roll your deposit forward to a new date at no charge — as long as we're notified before crew dispatch. Your date is locked once the deposit is paid; deposits aren't refundable if you cancel outright.
