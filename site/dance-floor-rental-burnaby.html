What our Burnaby dance floor lineup covers

A Burnaby reception in full swing.

Three floor sizes that cover almost every Burnaby event:

  • 12×12 portable dance floor — 60–80 guests, intimate venues, smaller heritage rooms.
  • 12×16 portable dance floor — the workhorse. 100-guest weddings, most marquee setups, most rooftop installs.
  • 16×20 portable dance floor — 150–200 guests, larger acreage and corporate-gala receptions.
  • Subfloor panels — engineered platforms that level grass, sloped lawns, and uneven patios beneath the dance floor. Adds 90–120 minutes to setup.
  • Edge ramps — included on every floor install. Trip-hazard safety, not aesthetic.

The floor goes down level. Heels don’t catch panel seams. We don’t ship a floor that bounces under load.

Burnaby dance floor install patterns we cover

A 12×16 dance floor in use at a Burnaby reception.

Heritage venue installs

Hart House, Burnaby Village Museum, and the Shadbolt Centre all have existing flooring that accepts the dance floor directly with edge ramps only. No subfloor needed; setup runs 45–60 minutes for a 12×16.

Brentwood and Metrotown rooftops

Rooftop dance floors need three things confirmed before quoting: freight-elevator dimensions, weight rating, and the rooftop’s structural capacity. Floor panels at <60 lb each fit nearly every freight elevator. Ballast goes on tent components; the floor sits flat on the rooftop surface.

Deer Lake Park and backyard receptions

Grass installs require engineered subfloor underneath the dance floor. Adds 90–120 minutes to setup but ensures the floor stays gap-free under 80–100 dancing guests at midnight.

Per-item pricing

The same in Brentwood as on Deer Lake. Our 12×16 portable dance floor is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery and subfloor — surface, elevator, slope — never the per-floor rate.

A 100-guest Burnaby dance floor rental order

For a 100-guest Burnaby wedding at Deer Lake Park’s lakefront lawn under marquee, a typical dance floor order looks like:

  • 1 × 12×16 portable dance floor (standard for 100 guests)
  • Subfloor panels (level the grass beneath the floor)

Dance Floor Rentals Questions — Burnaby

What's included on a Burnaby dance floor rental quote?
Every Burnaby dance floor quote we send is itemised: panel count, subfloor where the surface needs it, edge ramps, delivery, professional crew install, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Burnaby-zone uplifts, no surprises at the truck.
What size dance floor for a 100-guest Burnaby wedding?
12×16 is the standard for 100 guests — about 30–40% will be on the floor at any moment, which is the comfort target. For 60–80 guests, 12×12 works. For 150+ guests or weddings with a strong dance culture, 16×20.
Can you install a dance floor on grass or uneven surfaces?
Yes — we install over grass, sloped lawns, gravel, and uneven patios using engineered subfloor panels that level the surface beneath the dance floor. Subfloor adds 90–120 minutes to setup and gets quoted as a separate line.
Do you install dance floors on Brentwood or Metrotown rooftops?
Yes — rooftop dance floor installs are common Burnaby bookings. We confirm freight-elevator dimensions, weight rating, and rooftop structural capacity before quoting. Panels at <60 lb each fit nearly every freight elevator. The floor itself sits flat on the rooftop surface; ballast goes on the surrounding tent components, not the floor.
Can you install a dance floor at Hart House or Burnaby Village?
Yes — heritage venues with existing flooring accept our dance floor directly with edge ramps only. No subfloor needed. Setup is faster (45–60 minutes for a 12×16) than grass installs at Deer Lake Park or backyard receptions.
