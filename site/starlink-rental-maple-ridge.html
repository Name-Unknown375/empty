Starlink Internet Rentals Questions — Maple Ridge

Will Starlink work at my Maple Ridge venue?
Starlink needs a clear view of the sky — ideally pointing north with no large trees, buildings, or roof overhangs blocking the dish's field of view. The Starlink app has a built-in obstruction tool we can run during your Maple Ridge site visit. In dense forest or downtown alleys with tall buildings on all sides, performance can degrade. We'll flag any concerns during booking.
What speeds should I expect at a Maple Ridge event?
On the Roam plan with a clear sky, expect 100–200 Mbps download, 10–20 Mbps upload, and 25–50 ms latency. That's enough for Zoom calls, 4K video streaming, simultaneous DJ streaming + guest WiFi, livestream rigs, and credit-card payment terminals all running at the same time. We've stress-tested it at multiple Maple Ridge events with no degradation.
How long does setup take?
About 5 minutes once you place the dish. The Standard Actuated dish is self-aligning — you set it down with a clear view of the sky, plug in power, and it tilts itself to find the satellites. The router broadcasts WiFi within ~30 seconds of connecting. For Maple Ridge events we deliver it pre-configured and walk through setup with your crew on-site.
How does this work with our EcoFlow rental?
Starlink draws roughly 50–75 W continuous (peaks at ~150 W when the dish is melting snow). On a 2048 Wh Delta 3 Max that's 25–30 hours of continuous Starlink, or roughly 8 hours of Starlink + lighting + DJ at a typical Maple Ridge reception load. Most events power Starlink through the EcoFlow's USB-C or AC outlet — we'll confirm the right combo when you book.
What's the rental minimum?
Two-day weekend is the minimum (Friday delivery, Sunday pickup) — that's the same minimum as our power station rentals so Maple Ridge weekend events can book the kits together. For longer activations (festivals, week-long film shoots, multi-day production), we offer a weekly (5–7 day) rate that works out cheaper per day.
Do you deliver party rentals to Maple Ridge?
Yes — we deliver and set up throughout Maple Ridge, including Hammond, Silver Valley, and Albion. Standard Maple Ridge delivery runs just 30-40 minutes. Delivery and setup are available as add-on services — we'll quote them up front with no surprise fees at the venue.
Have you worked at venues in Maple Ridge like Golden Ears Provincial Park?
Yes — we regularly deliver rentals to Golden Ears Provincial Park, Alouette Lake, and Kanaka Creek Regional Park and dozens of private residences across Maple Ridge. We know the setback rules for most public parks in Maple Ridge and can advise on what permits (if any) your venue requires for tents.
