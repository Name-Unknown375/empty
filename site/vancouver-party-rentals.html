Delivery, setup, and what’s actually on the quote

A Vancouver quote from us is itemised end-to-end. No fuel surcharges. No zone uplifts. No surprises at the truck.

Delivery windows

We give you a one-hour delivery window when we book the date — not “morning of.” Vancouver traffic is real, and Friday-afternoon load-ins and Sunday-morning teardowns get planned around it. We plan around the traffic so you don’t pay for it.

Crew setup

Our crew levels the tent on slope, ballasts the legs where staking isn’t allowed (rooftops, paved lots, certain park surfaces), positions chairs and tables to your floor plan, and lays the dance floor on a level subfloor where the surface needs it. Mid-event re-staging — ceremony-to-reception flips on the same chair set — is included on single-tent bookings.

Same-day load-in and load-out

Feasible for most Vancouver venues. The exception is permit-restricted park ceremonies where the access window is tight; on those, we flag a separate mobilization day on the quote so the timeline isn’t a surprise.

Per-item pricing

Every chair is individually wiped, inspected, and stacked before it loads.

The same in Yaletown as in any neighbourhood we serve. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, elevator access, slope — never the per-item rate.

We carry the chair in volume: the same Chiavari we ship to a 50-guest backyard reception is the same one stacked 100-deep on the truck for a Lower Mainland vineyard wedding. No “premium” line, no “deluxe” line, no upcharge for the photogenic option.

FAQ

Party Rental Questions — Vancouver

What's included on a Vancouver rental quote?
Every Vancouver quote we send is itemised: rental items by line, delivery, crew setup time, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. There are no fuel surcharges, no Vancouver-zone uplifts, and no surprises at the truck. If your quote says $X, you pay $X.
Do you deliver and set up, or is it pickup-only?
Both — but the value is in the setup. Our crew arrives in your delivery window, levels and stakes the marquee, positions chairs and tables to your floor plan, and lays the dance floor before guests arrive. Pickup is available for smaller orders (chair-only, table-only) when it makes sense for your budget.
How clean are the chairs and tables when they arrive?
Every chair is individually wiped and inspected before it leaves our warehouse. Cushions are spot-cleaned, legs checked for damage, spindles checked for cracks. Tables get the same treatment. Anything that doesn't meet our condition bar comes off the inventory roster, not onto your booking.
How far in advance should I book Vancouver tent rentals for peak season?
For peak-season Saturdays in May–September, 6–8 weeks out is the comfortable window — earlier if your date is in July or August. Off-season and weekday events typically have 2–3 weeks of room. Chair-only and table-only orders are usually our most flexible same-week category.
What if it rains the morning of a Vancouver outdoor event?
Our marquee tents are weather-rated. Sidewalls are an add-on year-round and recommended October through April. If forecast forces a date change, we waive the change fee when you call us before crew dispatch.
Can I mix chair lines and tent sizes on one Vancouver order?
Yes — Chiavari for the reception, Fanback or Resin Garden for the ceremony rows, mixed marquee sizes for ceremony-and-reception venues. Single quote, single delivery, no mixing surcharge. We carry enough of each line to ship the full split without short-shipping.
How much do party rentals cost in Vancouver?
Party rental pricing in Vancouver depends on the items and your guest count. As a guide, chairs start around $3–$4 each per day, 6-foot banquet and 5-foot round tables from about $10–$13, and marquee tents from roughly $400 depending on size. Bundling a tent, tables and chairs into one of our packages saves 10%, and paying in full within 24 hours of your quote saves another 10%. Build your order online for an instant, itemised Vancouver quote — delivery and setup are priced up front with no hidden fees or fuel surcharges.
What does the delivery and setup fee cover?
When you add delivery and setup to your order, our crew handles round-trip transport, professional setup of tents and dance floors, takedown, and pickup. Chairs and tables are stacked and placed where you want them. Delivery and setup are quoted up front with no hidden charges or fuel surcharges — the price you see at checkout is the price you pay.
Can you set up a tent on grass, gravel, or a paved driveway?
Yes to all three. Our marquee tents use stake-down installation on grass and ballasted (weighted) installation on pavement, gravel, and stamped concrete. Tell us the surface when you book and we'll send the right hardware. We do not tent on artificial turf.
What happens if the weather is bad or I need to cancel?
Our 125% Cancellation Protection means if we ever cancel your booking, you receive 125% of your deposit back. If you need to reschedule because of weather, we'll roll your deposit forward to a new date at no charge — as long as we're notified before crew dispatch. Cancellations initiated by the customer are subject to our standard deposit terms.
