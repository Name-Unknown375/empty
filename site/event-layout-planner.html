Event Layout Planner

Drag tents, tables, chairs & dance floors into your venue. See live capacity counts. Export your floor plan as an image — completely free, no signup.

Tip: hold Shift while dragging for 0.25 ft snapping, or while rotating for 15° steps — hold Alt for 90° snap or ⌘/Ctrl for 180°.

How It Works

Plan Your Layout in 3 Steps

Designed for backyard weddings, corporate events, and milestone celebrations across the Lower Mainland.

01

Set Your Venue

Enter the width and depth of your event space in feet — your backyard, banquet hall, or tented area. The grid scales automatically so everything stays to size.

02

Drag & Drop Items

Pull tents, tables, chairs, dance floors, and stages from the sidebar onto the canvas. Every item is sized from our actual rental inventory — what you see is what we deliver.

03

See the Price & Quote

The planner adds up your items into a live cost estimate as you build. Save your layout as an image, save the project to keep editing later, or send it straight to us — your full itemized list comes with the request, so we can quote without back-and-forth.

Embed This Tool on Your Site

Run a party rental, catering, or event planning company elsewhere? Help your customers visualize their layout and embed our planner directly on your site.

  • Free to embed — no API keys, no rate limits, no signup.
  • Always up-to-date — improvements roll out everywhere automatically.
  • Your customers won't see our pricing or quote form. The planner auto-detects when it's embedded on a partner site and hides FPR-specific pricing and the "Get a Quote" form so they don't compete with yours.
  • Just keep the visible "Powered by Forever Party Rentals" credit link in place.
  • Don't iframe inside another iframe (some browsers block this).
