South Surrey peninsula — the marquee calendar core

A backyard reception running late under string lights.

South Surrey acreage weddings are the largest single share of our Surrey marquee bookings. Crescent Beach, Hazelmere Valley, Elgin, and the broader peninsula run on rural-road truck access with firm lawn or graded acreage as the surface.

The standard install is a 20×60 marquee with sidewalls, engineered ground stakes, bistro string lighting along the ridge, and (October–April) a tent heater. Setup runs 4–5 hours; teardown is 2–3. For larger weddings (150–200 guests), 30×60 marquee on a single footprint or linked 20×40s for split ceremony/reception zones.

Hazelmere afternoon wind off the peninsula is reliable enough that we recommend sidewalls year-round on open-field installs here. The marquee itself doesn’t change between a Cloverdale install and a Hazelmere install — what changes is anchor count.

For Cloverdale Rodeo–adjacent corporate galas and the Surrey Fair grounds-style events, the marquee is one piece of a much larger order: dance floor for the central tent, bistro lighting strands across the ridge, sidewalls for noise control and weather, and the chairs and tables that go inside.

Bear Creek Park, Surrey Civic Plaza, and ceremony permits

Bear Creek Park’s ceremony zones take 20×40 and 20×60 footprints depending on the permit zone. Civic Plaza hardscape installs cap at 20×40 in most configurations — coordinated with the City’s events team during quoting.

Park-permit ceremony marquees are typically smaller than acreage receptions (50–100 guest range). We’ve worked the Bear Creek calendar enough to know the standard install windows and which zones accept which footprints.

Tent Rentals Questions — Surrey

How quickly can you install a tent in Surrey?
Faster than anywhere else we serve. Same-day install is feasible for 20×20 popups and supplemental 20×40 marquees when our trucks have capacity; 24-hour turnaround on 20×60 and larger is routine. For weekend bookings, we typically install Friday for a Saturday event — the install crew is back in the same Surrey warehouse when teardown happens Sunday.
Do you tent on the Cloverdale Rodeo Grounds hardscape?
Yes — Cloverdale's Stetson Bowl and rodeo grounds run hardscape installs with full ballast on every leg, no stakes. We've installed 30×60 and linked 20×40 configurations there enough times to know the loading-dock access patterns. Coordinate the install window with the venue's primary programming.
What's the standard install for a South Surrey acreage wedding?
20×60 or 30×60 marquee on the lawn, engineered ground stakes, sidewalls year-round (Hazelmere afternoon wind off the peninsula is real), bistro lighting along the ridge, optional tent heater for shoulder-season bookings. Truck access is rural-road and ample. Most South Surrey acreage installs are 5–6 hour crew windows.
Can you handle a Bear Creek Park or Surrey Civic Plaza permit install?
Yes — both run on city event permits with venue-coordinated delivery windows. Bear Creek's ceremony zone takes 20×40 footprints comfortably; the Civic Plaza hardscape needs full ballast. We coordinate the permit timing with you during quoting.
Does the Surrey-warehouse location lower the quote?
Yes — Surrey delivery line is the cheapest on our map. We don't charge a Surrey-distance premium because there isn't one. The savings show up in the delivery line item, not in the per-tent rate (which is the same Surrey as it is on a Maple Ridge or Vancouver install).
