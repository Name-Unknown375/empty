The Burnaby logistics that change the quote

Burnaby venue access varies more than most cities — and that variation is what shapes the quote.

Heritage and lakefront venues

Cocktail highboys staged for a reception.

Hart House, Burnaby Village Museum, the Shadbolt Centre, and Deer Lake Park all run their own programming calendars. Vendor access is venue-coordinated; we book the load-in window with the events team during quoting so the date isn’t a surprise. Deer Lake Park alone has multiple ceremony zones with different vehicle-access lanes — we know which ones fit a 20×60 marquee and which top out at a 20×40.

Brentwood and Metrotown high-rise events

Rooftop and tenant events at Brentwood Town Centre and Metrotown buildings need three things confirmed up front: freight-elevator dimensions, freight-elevator weight rating, and the building manager’s Saturday availability. Marquees on rooftops are case-by-case based on the rooftop’s structural rating; tables and chairs ship freely.

Detached-home neighbourhoods

Backyards in North Burnaby, the Deer Lake area, and Edmonds run the standard pattern: setbacks, slope, sprinkler heads, and where the truck can stop without blocking the alley. Most accept a 20×40 comfortably.

Per-item pricing

The same in Brentwood as in any neighbourhood we serve. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — distance, elevator access, slope — never the per-item rate.

A 100-guest Burnaby wedding rental order

For a 100-guest Burnaby wedding — say a Saturday in August at Deer Lake Park’s lakefront lawn, with ceremony and reception both running under a single tent — a typical rental order looks like:

  • 1 × 20×60 marquee tent with sidewalls (covered ceremony + reception)
  • 100 × white Chiavari chairs (ceremony rows, then re-positioned at reception tables — same chairs, our crew handles the flip)
  • 12 × 5ft round tables (8 guests per table = 96, plus 2 extras)
  • 6 × 6ft banquet tables (head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, bar)
  • 1 × 12×16 portable dance floor
  • 2 × cocktail highboys for the cocktail hour
  • Bistro string lighting along the marquee ridge

Setup runs 5–6 hours; teardown 3–4. Both quoted up front. This is a single-tent ceremony-to-reception flip — our ceremony-to-reception flip playbook covers the layout that lets the same chairs run both phases.

FAQ

Party Rental Questions — Burnaby

What's included on a Burnaby rental quote?
Every Burnaby quote we send is itemised: rental items by line, delivery, crew setup time, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Burnaby-zone uplifts, no surprises at the truck. If your quote says $X, you pay $X.
Do you deliver and set up rooftop and high-rise events?
Yes — Brentwood and Metrotown rooftop bookings are common Burnaby work for us. We confirm the building's freight-elevator dimensions and weight rating before quoting, ballast (never stake) on rooftops, and book the building manager's Saturday window during quoting.
How clean are the chairs and tables when they arrive?
Every chair is individually wiped and inspected before it leaves our warehouse. Cushions are spot-cleaned, legs checked for damage, spindles checked for cracks. Tables get the same treatment. Anything that doesn't meet our condition bar comes off the inventory roster, not onto your booking.
How far in advance should I book Burnaby tent rentals for peak season?
For peak-season Saturdays in May–September, 6–8 weeks out is the comfortable window. Off-season and weekday events typically have 2–3 weeks of room. Chair-only and table-only orders are usually our most flexible same-week category.
Can I bundle Christmas light installation with my Burnaby event rental?
Yes — Burnaby is one of our 14 Christmas light installation cities. The two services are quoted separately because Christmas runs October through January and event rentals run year-round. Free off-season storage is included on every Christmas light install.
How much do party rentals cost in Burnaby?
Party rental pricing in Burnaby depends on the items and your guest count. As a guide, chairs start around $3–$4 each per day, 6-foot banquet and 5-foot round tables from about $10–$13, and marquee tents from roughly $400 depending on size. Bundling a tent, tables and chairs into one of our packages saves 10%, and paying in full within 24 hours of your quote saves another 10%. Build your order online for an instant, itemised Burnaby quote — delivery and setup are priced up front with no hidden fees or fuel surcharges.
What does the delivery and setup fee cover?
When you add delivery and setup to your order, our crew handles round-trip transport, professional setup of tents and dance floors, takedown, and pickup. Chairs and tables are stacked and placed where you want them. Delivery and setup are quoted up front with no hidden charges or fuel surcharges — the price you see at checkout is the price you pay.
Can you set up a tent on grass, gravel, or a paved driveway?
Yes to all three. Our marquee tents use stake-down installation on grass and ballasted (weighted) installation on pavement, gravel, and stamped concrete. Tell us the surface when you book and we'll send the right hardware. We do not tent on artificial turf.
What happens if the weather is bad or I need to cancel?
Our 125% Cancellation Protection means if we ever cancel your booking, you receive 125% of your deposit back. If you need to reschedule because of weather, we'll roll your deposit forward to a new date at no charge — as long as we're notified before crew dispatch. Cancellations initiated by the customer are subject to our standard deposit terms.
