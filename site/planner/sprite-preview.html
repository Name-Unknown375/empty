Sprite preview · v2

Revised after researching how the actual products look from above + how Tripleseat / Prismm / AllSeated / AutoCAD AIA blocks draw plan-view furniture symbols. Each row shows the planner's current rendering vs. the proposed v2 sprite, with a 2× zoom column for detail visibility.

What changed since v1 (deltas baked into this page):
  • Chiavari: reduced slats 4 → 3, increased backrest band contrast, made the seat slightly trapezoidal (back edge narrower) to match the real silhouette.
  • Fanback: fan rises 0.55 ft (from 0.4), now reads as a half-disc rather than a small bump; ribs are vertical chord lines inside the arc instead of antennas above it.
  • Resin Garden: added small armrest bumps at the upper corners (the real differentiator from a Chiavari at small sizes); slimmed the backrest slab; pure white fill.
  • 5ft Round: dropped the (invisible) inner ring; replaced with 4 short spider-leg hints + a stronger center pedestal disc.
  • 6ft Banquet: dropped the corner leg ticks (not a real plan-view convention; legs are hidden from above anyway). Kept the inner bevel.
  • 8ft Banquet: dropped both the middle leg pair (factually inaccurate — 8ft folders use 2 leg pairs, not 3) and the corner ticks. Differs from 6ft only by length, like the industry convention.
  • Cocktail Highboy: X-base now extends to ~76 % of the radius (real X-bases reach near the rim for stability); added a small dark center disc where the column meets the top.
