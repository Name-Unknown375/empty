Coquitlam tent install patterns we cover

Cocktail highboys staged for a Town Centre corporate event.

Lafarge Lake and Town Centre civic

Lafarge Lake’s lakeside lawn and Town Centre Park run on city event permits with specific setback rules and venue-coordinated delivery windows. Stakes-plus-ballast is standard for the lakeside zone given variable inlet wind. Most civic-scale marquees here run 20×40 to 20×60.

Westwood Plateau Golf — slope-aware ceremony installs

Westwood’s ceremony lawn is graded for golf and the slope changes tent placement options. We walk the site during quoting to confirm the marquee position; for steeper grades we adjust the footprint or add supplemental anchor.

Burke Mountain hillside acreage

Hillside acreage along the upper Burke streets often requires engineered slope-staking, supplemental downhill ballast, or — for the steepest sites — a smaller marquee than the headcount would otherwise suggest. We don’t squeeze a 20×60 onto a slope that calls for two 20×30s.

Minnekhada and Mundy forest ceremonies

Forest-zone ceremonies need permit coordination, hand-carry delivery from the parking lot, and uneven-ground anchor strategies. Smaller marquee footprints (20×20, 20×30) are standard for the forest sites; larger footprints stay on the open meadow.

Maillardville, Eagle Ridge, Town Centre backyards

Standard backyard pattern: setbacks, slope, sprinkler heads, and where the truck can stop without blocking the laneway. Maillardville’s heritage streets are tight on truck access; we use shorter delivery windows and pre-stage equipment closer to the property line.

Per-item pricing

The same in Maillardville as on Burke Mountain. Our 20×40 marquee is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — slope, anchor strategy, after-hours timing — never the tent rate.

Same-day setup and ceremony-to-reception flips

Many Coquitlam weddings run a single-tent ceremony-to-reception flip — same chairs, same marquee handle both phases, with our crew rearranging the layout in 30–45 minutes during the cocktail hour. Our ceremony-to-reception flip playbook covers the layout math; the operational reality is that one tent and one crew save you a meaningful amount on the quote versus two-venue setups.

A 100-guest Coquitlam tent rental order

For a 100-guest Coquitlam wedding — say a Saturday in August at Westwood Plateau Golf, ceremony and reception under a single tent on the ceremony lawn — a typical tent-side order looks like:

  • 1 × 20×60 marquee tent (covered ceremony + reception in single footprint)
  • 4 × tent sidewalls (full enclosure for evening)
  • 8 × bistro string-light strands across the marquee ridge
  • 1 × tent heater (October–April bookings; optional in summer)
  • Engineered ground stakes plus slope-supplement ballast (default for Westwood’s graded lawn)

Setup runs 4–5 hours for a 20×60 + sidewall configuration; teardown is 2–3 hours. Both quoted up front. The 50/100/150/200 rental checklist maps the marquee sizing. FAQ

Tent Rentals Questions — Coquitlam

What's included on a Coquitlam tent rental quote?
Every Coquitlam tent quote we send is itemised: the marquee, sidewalls, anchor kit, lighting add-ons, delivery, professional crew install, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Coquitlam-zone uplifts, no hill-access premiums for Burke Mountain or Westwood, no surprises at the truck.
Can you install a marquee at Lafarge Lake or Mundy Park?
Yes — both are regular Coquitlam venues for our crew. Lafarge Lake's lakeside lawn accepts most marquee footprints with a city event permit (we coordinate during quoting). Mundy Park is more flexible on size but also requires permitting; the meadow zones work for marquees up to 30×60. Both venues run on stakes plus ballast given Coquitlam's variable wind from the inlet.
Do you install tents at Westwood Plateau or on hillside acreage?
Yes — Westwood Plateau Golf's ceremony lawn and Burke Mountain hillside acreage are routine Coquitlam installs. Westwood's lawn is graded for golf and the slope changes the tent placement options; we walk the site during quoting to confirm. Burke Mountain installs occasionally need engineered slope-staking or supplemental ballast on the downhill side.
What tent size for a 100-guest Coquitlam wedding?
For a covered ceremony + reception under one tent, a 20×60 marquee is the right starting size for 100 guests. For 150–200 guests, 30×60 or two linked 20×40 tents. Most Westwood Plateau and Burke Mountain backyards accept a 20×60 with the right placement; we walk the site during quoting if the slope is significant.
How fast can you deliver a Coquitlam marquee on short notice?
Same-week is usually feasible for chair, table, and 10×10 popup orders. Marquee tents 20×40 and larger need 7+ days of installer scheduling, especially for venues with permit or hillside-coordination steps. Call before booking online and we'll tell you straight whether the timeline works.
