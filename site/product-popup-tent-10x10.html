10x10 Popup Tent Rental

Starting from $75.00 / day · delivery & setup available

Not every event needs a 40-foot marquee. The 10×10 popup tent is our quick-set canopy — ideal for small backyard gatherings (covers ~20 standing or 1 six-foot table), market stalls, vendor booths at community events, and any situation where you need shelter up in under 30 minutes. Rent it with delivery-and-setup or pick it up from our warehouse if you'd rather stage it yourself. Available with optional sidewalls and weighted-ballast kits for pavement installs.

  • ✓ Quick-set — under 30 minutes from arrival to up
  • ✓ Covers 20 standing guests or one 6ft banquet table
  • ✓ Pickup or full delivery-and-setup both available
  • ✓ Sidewalls + weight kits for pavement installs
  • ✓ Popular for markets, vendor booths, small backyards
