What our Mission chair lineup covers

A Chiavari ready to load — individually wiped, inspected, stacked.

Three chair lines cover almost every Mission event:

  • White Chiavari ($8.50/day) — the wedding-reception standard. Lacquered hardwood frame, ivory cushion, stacks 8 high. Best for Hatzic and Silverhill acreage receptions under marquee, downtown Mission indoor venues, abbey-view restaurant receptions, and any Mission booking where the photos prioritize formal seating against valley-and-mountain backdrops.
  • Fanback folding ($3.25/day) — the workhorse. Steel frame, folds flat, stacks compactly. Right for Fraser River Heritage Park ceremony rows, Mission Leisure Centre community events, school events at Mission campuses, and any high-headcount booking.
  • Resin Garden ($5.50/day) — the outdoor-resilient pick. Right for Fraser River Heritage Park’s river-overlook ceremonies, Hatzic Lake outdoor weddings, Cedar Valley acreage ceremonies on damp grass, and any Mission booking where the morning surface is dew-prone.

Mixing lines is free. Lougheed corridor delivery means we can absorb the larger Mission acreage orders on a single Saturday run.

Hatzic, Silverhill, Heritage Park — Mission chair patterns

Ceremony chairs staged on a Mission acreage lawn.

Hatzic Valley and Hatzic Lake acreage

Hatzic Valley acreage weddings drive the upscale Mission calendar. Most are 100–150 guest configurations with a two-chair-line setup. Hatzic Lake outdoor ceremonies run on lakeside grass; Resin Garden every time. Truck access on Hatzic-area rural roads is ample.

Silverhill and Cedar Valley — valley-view acreage

Silverhill and Cedar Valley sit higher up the valley with longer driveways and more pronounced mountain-and-valley views. Most are 100–150 guests with Chiavari-only configurations under marquee. The view is the photographic anchor; the chairs frame it.

Fraser River Heritage Park

Fraser River Heritage Park’s river-overlook ceremony zones are heritage-permit coordinated. Resin Garden for the damp-grass and river-mist exposure. Hand-carry from the parking lot to the specific ceremony zone for some configurations.

Westminster Abbey-adjacent and abbey-view venues

The Benedictine monastery at Westminster Abbey doesn’t host weddings, but its hillside position shapes the photo aesthetic at the surrounding venues. Abbey-view restaurant and residence receptions skew Chiavari-only with intimate guest counts (60–100 typical).

Mission Leisure Centre and downtown community events

Mission Leisure Centre and downtown Mission community-venue events run on Fanback folding for the high-headcount registrations and seated-row community events. Truck access is standard.

Per-item pricing

The same on a Hatzic acreage as at a downtown Mission venue. Our Chiavari chair is one rate, full stop. The Lougheed corridor delivery line is the variable, not the per-chair rate.

Long-haul logistics

Mission delivery from our warehouse runs 75 minutes one-way via Highway 7 / Lougheed corridor. Short-notice mid-event swaps run 2–2.5 hours to on-site during business hours.

A 100-guest Mission acreage chair rental order

For a 100-guest Mission wedding — say a Saturday in August with ceremony at Fraser River Heritage Park followed by a Hatzic Valley acreage reception under marquee — a typical chair-side order looks like:

  • 100 × Resin Garden chairs river-overlook ceremony at Heritage Park
  • 100 × white Chiavari chairs reception under marquee at the Hatzic acreage

The two-chair-line setup is the standard Mission wedding configuration when the ceremony is at a riverside or park venue. For Silverhill or Cedar Valley single-venue weddings (ceremony and reception at the same acreage), 100 × Chiavari handles both phases. For other guest counts, the 50/100/150/200 rental checklist maps the chair count + table count to your headcount. Mission acreage weddings rarely stop at chairs — the marquee, tables, dance floor, and lighting all ship on the same Saturday delivery, off our Mission party rentals catalogue.

FAQ

Chair Rentals Questions — Mission

What's included on a Mission chair rental quote?
Every Mission chair quote we send is itemised: chairs by line, delivery, crew positioning to your floor plan, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Fraser Valley uplifts, no surprises at the truck. The Lougheed corridor delivery line is the only thing that shifts between a Hatzic and a downtown Mission booking.
Which chair works for a Fraser River Heritage Park ceremony?
Fraser River Heritage Park's open lawn and river-overlook ceremony zones run on damp grass and afternoon onshore breeze off the river. Resin Garden every time — Chiavari's lacquered hardwood doesn't love riverside humidity. Most Heritage Park weddings flip the reception to a Silverhill or Cedar Valley acreage; Chiavari for that phase.
Can you handle a Hatzic Valley or Silverhill acreage wedding?
Yes — Hatzic and Silverhill acreage weddings are the largest single share of our Mission marquee + chair calendar. Rural-road truck access is ample; most acreages have driveway runs measured in hundreds of metres. The standard two-chair-line setup is Resin Garden outdoor ceremony, Chiavari reception under marquee.
Can I mix chair types for a Mission event?
Yes — Fanback folding for the ceremony rows at Heritage Park or Leisure Centre, Chiavari at a Hatzic acreage reception; or Resin Garden for an outdoor Cedar Valley ceremony followed by Chiavari indoors at a Silverhill venue. Single quote, single delivery, no mixing surcharge — Lougheed corridor delivery means we can absorb the larger Mission orders efficiently.
How clean are the chairs when they arrive at a Mission acreage?
Every chair is individually wiped and inspected before it leaves our warehouse. Cushions are spot-cleaned, legs checked for damage. Acreage chair work in Mission's rural conditions puts more wear on inventory than urban setups; chairs that don't meet the condition bar come off the roster, not onto your booking.
