What our North Vancouver table lineup covers

A 5ft round set for 8 — the North Shore wedding-reception standard.

Three tables, three jobs. North Vancouver event floor plans usually need all three:

  • 5ft round tables ($13.50/day, seats 8) — the classic wedding-reception configuration. The reception workhorse for Lynn Valley acreages, Capilano-area private homes, and any seated-dinner event.
  • 6ft rectangular banquet tables ($10.95/day, seats 6–8) — the single most versatile SKU we ship. Family-style dining rows, buffet stations, gift tables, DJ booths, bar setups. Under 75 lb so they handle hand-carry from the road when steep driveways require it.
  • Cocktail highboy tables ($15.50/day, standing) — 42-inch standing-height rounds for cocktail hour and networking events. Plan ~1 highboy per 10–12 standing guests.

North Shore deliveries reward planning. Steep driveways, overhead branches, and bridge-traffic windows all show up before the quote, not after.

North Shore access and table staging

Banquet rows staged outside an office for a staff lunch.

Steep driveways and hand-carry

Some Edgemont and lower Capilano properties have grade or width constraints that push us to stage at the road. Our 6ft banquet tables at <75 lb handle hand-carry; the 8ft are heavier and we sometimes recommend swapping to 6ft if the carry distance is meaningful.

Bridge + traffic timing

We plan delivery windows around the Ironworkers Memorial crossing and Lonsdale traffic patterns. Friday afternoon and Sunday morning teardowns get the widest windows.

Mountain-adjacent venues

Grouse, Mount Seymour, upper Capilano properties often run smaller table counts (60–80 guests) with full-formal place settings. We level rounds on bench-style platforms when the lawn isn’t level enough for direct staging.

Per-item pricing

The same on a Lynn Valley acreage as on Lonsdale. Our 5ft round table is one rate, full stop. The line that changes between bookings is delivery — driveway grade, hand-carry, distance — never the per-table rate.

Lonsdale Quay restaurant and waterfront events

The other half of our North Van table calendar is restaurant patio events and waterfront corporate functions along Lonsdale Quay. The pattern here is smaller-footprint (a 20×20 popup typically clears the patio), faster setup, and a stronger emphasis on cocktail-hour highboys than full-scale dinner seating. Most Quay-area corporate events run 60–80 attendees on cocktail-hour configurations rather than seated-dinner setups.

A 100-guest North Vancouver table rental order

For a 100-guest North Vancouver wedding — say a Saturday in August at a Lynn Valley acreage with marquee reception — a typical table-side order looks like:

  • 12 × 5ft round tables (8 guests per round = 96, with 2 extras for kids and overflow)
  • 6 × 6ft banquet tables (head linking 2, gift, cake, DJ, registration)
  • 2 × 6ft banquet tables for the buffet line
  • 3 × cocktail highboys for the pre-dinner cocktail hour

Total: 23 tables, all positioned to your floor plan during a single delivery window. The 50/100/150/200 rental checklist maps the table mix to your headcount.

FAQ

Table Rentals Questions — North Vancouver

What's included on a North Vancouver table rental quote?
Every North Van table quote we send is itemised: tables by line, delivery, crew positioning to your floor plan, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no North Shore uplifts, no setup surcharge — every table is positioned during the delivery window.
How many tables for a 100-guest North Vancouver wedding?
For seated dinner at 5ft rounds, plan ~12 rounds (8 guests per round = 96; round up for kids and split parties). Add 5–8 banquet tables for head, gift, cake, DJ, registration, and buffet. Add 2–3 cocktail highboys for the pre-dinner cocktail hour.
Can your truck handle steep North Van driveways with table loads?
In most cases yes — our delivery vans handle grades up to about 10% loaded. For unusually steep or narrow lots (some Edgemont and lower Capilano properties) we stage at the road and hand-carry tables to the venue. Hand-carry distance gets factored into the crew-time line, not surfaced as a surprise on event day.
Do you supply linens, runners, or chargers?
We don't stock linens directly, but we coordinate through our preferred Lower Mainland linen partners and bundle them onto the same delivery window if you want. Chargers, runners, and place settings sit with the catering provider.
Can you re-stage tables mid-event for a North Van wedding?
Yes — for any single-tent ceremony-to-reception flip, our crew handles the table re-stage during the cocktail hour. Banquet tables fold flat and re-position quickly; rounds re-stack to the truck while the dinner configuration goes up.
