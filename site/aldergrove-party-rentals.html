Party Rental Questions — Aldergrove

What's included on an Aldergrove rental quote?
Every Aldergrove quote we send is itemised: rental items by line, delivery, crew setup time, optional add-ons, and applicable taxes. No fuel surcharges, no Aldergrove-zone uplifts, no surprises at the truck. If your quote says $X, you pay $X.
Do you handle Greater Vancouver Zoo events?
Yes — the Zoo runs corporate events, family functions, and conservation-organization gatherings that we deliver to regularly. The Zoo coordinates vendor access through its events team; we book delivery slots during quoting. Most Zoo setups skip the marquee (the Zoo's existing event spaces handle the footprint) and run on banquet tables, Chiavari chairs, cocktail highboys, and bistro lighting.
Can you tent at an Aldergrove acreage or rural wedding?
Yes — Aldergrove rural acreages are among our most-photogenic Township deliveries. Most properties have plenty of footprint for a 20×60 or 30×60 marquee. Engineered ground stakes are our default anchor on grass; we confirm sprinkler infrastructure and irrigation lines before staking. Rural-residential properties often have 1.5+ km driveway approaches we factor into the delivery window.
How clean are the chairs and tables when they arrive?
Every chair is individually wiped and inspected before it leaves our warehouse. Cushions are spot-cleaned, legs checked for damage, spindles checked for cracks. Tables get the same treatment. Anything that doesn't meet our condition bar comes off the inventory roster, not onto your booking.
Can you deliver to Aldergrove Regional Park ceremonies?
Yes — Aldergrove Regional Park is one of our regular Township park venues. Metro Vancouver Regional Parks runs the permit process for tents above their threshold; couples pull the permits, but we know which ceremony zones at the park accept which marquee footprints. Open-air park ceremonies have farmland-style wind exposure we plan for.
How much do party rentals cost in Aldergrove?
Party rental pricing in Aldergrove depends on the items and your guest count. As a guide, chairs start around $3–$4 each per day, 6-foot banquet and 5-foot round tables from about $10–$13, and marquee tents from roughly $400 depending on size. Bundling a tent, tables and chairs into one of our packages saves 10%, and paying in full within 24 hours of your quote saves another 10%. Build your order online for an instant, itemised Aldergrove quote — delivery and setup are priced up front with no hidden fees or fuel surcharges.
What does the delivery and setup fee cover?
When you add delivery and setup to your order, our crew handles round-trip transport, professional setup of tents and dance floors, takedown, and pickup. Chairs and tables are stacked and placed where you want them. Delivery and setup are quoted up front with no hidden charges or fuel surcharges — the price you see at checkout is the price you pay.
Can you set up a tent on grass, gravel, or a paved driveway?
Yes to all three. Our marquee tents use stake-down installation on grass and ballasted (weighted) installation on pavement, gravel, and stamped concrete. Tell us the surface when you book and we'll send the right hardware. We do not tent on artificial turf.
What happens if the weather is bad or I need to cancel?
Our 125% Cancellation Protection means if we ever cancel your booking, you receive 125% of your deposit back. If you need to reschedule because of weather, we'll roll your deposit forward to a new date at no charge — as long as we're notified before crew dispatch.
